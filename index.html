YouTube Plans to Offer AV2 Streaming on Web in Early 2027

YouTube计划2027年初在网页端提供AV2流媒体

10月25日 08:35重要可靠性 B

据TechPowerUp 10月25日报道，开放媒体联盟本周更新AV2进展，YouTube工程总监Des Keane确认平台将开始AV2流媒体，目标是“最早2027年初在网页端提供”。AV2是AV1的免版税后继者，联盟称其压缩效率比AV1提升约30%，并增加多流、多视点视频、更高帧率和改进HDR支持。

Google Chrome工程副总裁Jim Bankoski表示已在浏览器中演示AV2播放，但未说明哪个Chrome版本支持或是否默认开启。报道指出，目前尚无消费级GPU或电视支持AV2硬件解码，早期播放可能依赖CPU软件解码，VideoLAN称AV2解码复杂度约为AV1的五倍。Netflix和Prime Video正在探索该格式，但未公布时间表。

According to a TechPowerUp report on October 25, the Alliance for Open Media updated on AV2 progress this week, and YouTube Engineering Director Des Keane confirmed that the platform will begin AV2 streaming, with the goal of "offering it on the web as early as early 2027." AV2 is the royalty-free successor to AV1, and the alliance says its compression efficiency is about 30% higher than AV1's, with added support for multi-stream and multi-view video, higher frame rates, and improved HDR.

据TechPowerUp 10月25日报道，开放媒体联盟本周更新AV2进展，YouTube工程总监Des Keane确认平台将开始AV2流媒体，目标是“最早2027年初在网页端提供”。AV2是AV1的免版税后继者，联盟称其压缩效率比AV1提升约30%，并增加多流、多视点视频、更高帧率和改进HDR支持。

Google Chrome Engineering Vice President Jim Bankoski said AV2 playback has been demonstrated in the browser, but did not say which Chrome version supports it or whether it is enabled by default. The report notes that no consumer GPUs or TVs currently support AV2 hardware decoding, so early playback may rely on CPU software decoding, and VideoLAN says AV2 decoding complexity is about five times that of AV1. Netflix and Prime Video are exploring the format but have not announced a timeline.

Google Chrome工程副总裁Jim Bankoski表示已在浏览器中演示AV2播放，但未说明哪个Chrome版本支持或是否默认开启。报道指出，目前尚无消费级GPU或电视支持AV2硬件解码，早期播放可能依赖CPU软件解码，VideoLAN称AV2解码复杂度约为AV1的五倍。Netflix和Prime Video正在探索该格式，但未公布时间表。

US Department of Energy Issues First Non-Exclusive License for TRIG Coal Gasification Technology

美国能源部首次就TRIG煤气化技术发出非独占许可

10月16日 01:27重大可靠性 A进展

美国能源部碳氢化合物与地热能办公室10月15日宣布，该部已与Latitude Energy Services执行首个TRIG煤气化技术非独占许可，范围仅限电力领域。Latitude另独立持有该技术在工业领域的权利，此次许可与之互补。

TRIG是由美国能源部与产业伙伴联合开发的煤气化系统，设计针对低阶、高水分煤。能源部称，此次许可被视为该项联邦共同开发技术转移和商业化的重要节点，并为美国煤炭行业增加商业部署机会提供路径。

公告未披露许可费、项目装机规模、气化效率或成本数据，也未说明是否已有商业运行项目，因此尚不能判断该技术当前处于示范还是商业阶段。

The U.S. Department of Energy's Office of Hydrocarbons and Geothermal Energy announced on October 15 that the department has executed its first non-exclusive license for TRIG coal gasification technology with Latitude Energy Services, covering the power sector only. Latitude separately holds the rights to the technology in the industrial sector on its own, and this license is complementary to those rights.

美国能源部碳氢化合物与地热能办公室10月15日宣布，该部已与Latitude Energy Services执行首个TRIG煤气化技术非独占许可，范围仅限电力领域。Latitude另独立持有该技术在工业领域的权利，此次许可与之互补。

TRIG is a coal gasification system jointly developed by the U.S. Department of Energy and industry partners, designed for low-rank, high-moisture coal. The Department of Energy said the license is regarded as an important milestone in the transfer and commercialization of this federally co-developed technology, and provides a path to expand commercial deployment opportunities for the U.S. coal industry.

TRIG是由美国能源部与产业伙伴联合开发的煤气化系统，设计针对低阶、高水分煤。能源部称，此次许可被视为该项联邦共同开发技术转移和商业化的重要节点，并为美国煤炭行业增加商业部署机会提供路径。

The announcement did not disclose the license fee, project capacity, gasification efficiency or cost data, nor whether any commercial operating project exists, so it is not yet possible to determine whether the technology is currently at the demonstration or commercial stage.

公告未披露许可费、项目装机规模、气化效率或成本数据，也未说明是否已有商业运行项目，因此尚不能判断该技术当前处于示范还是商业阶段。

Mathematicians Begin to Parse So-Called 'AI Proof' of the Quasi-Riemann Hypothesis: Significant but Details Not Yet Fully Public

数学家开始解读所谓“AI证明拟黎曼猜想”：意义重大但细节仍未完全公开

10月19日 23:40重大可靠性 B进展

Quanta Magazine 10月19日刊文称，在OpenAI于10月6日公布的377项结果中，有一项声称解决了“拟黎曼猜想”——黎曼猜想的弱化版本。该刊称，这与此前一些AI数学成果不同，已有人类专家对其展开解读。

黎曼猜想断言黎曼ζ函数的非平凡零点实部均为1/2，它与素数分布紧密相关，已悬置160余年。拟黎曼猜想是较弱形式：纽约大学Maksym Radziwiłł对Quanta表示，黎曼猜想的大部分推论已可由拟黎曼猜想推出，其影响“惊人”。斯坦福的Jared Duker Lichtman称，这是迄今AI在数学上“最重要的成果，且领先幅度相当大”。

报道称，与数学家此前预期相反，该证明没有引入新概念，而是把已知思想作了出人意料的综合，并部分依赖Radziwiłł与佐治亚理工Alexander Dunn等人发展的技术。数论学者正合作理解证明内容，但报道未说明证明是否经过正式评审或复现。

Quanta Magazine reported on October 19 that among the 377 results OpenAI announced on October 6, one claims to have solved the "quasi-Riemann hypothesis," a weakened version of the Riemann hypothesis. The magazine said that, unlike some earlier AI results in mathematics, this one has already begun to be interpreted by human experts.

Quanta Magazine 10月19日刊文称，在OpenAI于10月6日公布的377项结果中，有一项声称解决了“拟黎曼猜想”——黎曼猜想的弱化版本。该刊称，这与此前一些AI数学成果不同，已有人类专家对其展开解读。

The Riemann hypothesis asserts that all non-trivial zeros of the Riemann zeta function have real part 1/2; it is closely tied to the distribution of prime numbers and has remained open for more than 160 years. The quasi-Riemann hypothesis is a weaker form: Maksym Radziwiłł of New York University told Quanta that most corollaries of the Riemann hypothesis can already be derived from the quasi-Riemann hypothesis, and that its implications are "amazing." Jared Duker Lichtman of Stanford called it the "most important result" AI has produced in mathematics to date, "and by a fairly wide margin."

黎曼猜想断言黎曼ζ函数的非平凡零点实部均为1/2，它与素数分布紧密相关，已悬置160余年。拟黎曼猜想是较弱形式：纽约大学Maksym Radziwiłł对Quanta表示，黎曼猜想的大部分推论已可由拟黎曼猜想推出，其影响“惊人”。斯坦福的Jared Duker Lichtman称，这是迄今AI在数学上“最重要的成果，且领先幅度相当大”。

The report said that, contrary to mathematicians' earlier expectations, the proof introduces no new concepts but instead combines known ideas in unexpected ways, drawing partly on techniques developed by Radziwiłł and Alexander Dunn of Georgia Tech and others. Number theorists are collaborating to understand the proof's contents, but the report did not say whether the proof has undergone formal review or replication.

中国商务部新闻发言人10月24日就美方发布新版对外投资限制拟议规则答记者问。商务部称，该拟议规则歧视性扩大对华投资限制范围、加大限制力度，是泛化国家安全做法，违背两国元首达成的重要共识，干扰中美企业正常经贸合作。中方表示严重关切、坚决反对，将密切关注事态后续发展，并保留采取相应措施的权利。

商务部答问中提及，美财政部于10月23日发布该拟议规则并征求公众评论意见。商务部敦促美方尊重市场经济规律，停止泛化国家安全概念，停止将经贸问题政治化、武器化。

On October 24, a spokesperson for China's Ministry of Commerce answered reporters' questions regarding the new proposed rules on outbound investment restrictions released by the United States. The Ministry of Commerce said that the proposed rules discriminatorily expand the scope and intensity of investment restrictions on China, constitute a practice of over-generalizing national security, violate the important consensus reached by the two heads of state, and disrupt normal economic and trade cooperation between Chinese and American enterprises. The Chinese side expresses serious concern and firm opposition, will closely follow the subsequent development of the situation, and reserves the right to take corresponding measures.

中国商务部新闻发言人10月24日就美方发布新版对外投资限制拟议规则答记者问。商务部称，该拟议规则歧视性扩大对华投资限制范围、加大限制力度，是泛化国家安全做法，违背两国元首达成的重要共识，干扰中美企业正常经贸合作。中方表示严重关切、坚决反对，将密切关注事态后续发展，并保留采取相应措施的权利。

In the question-and-answer, the Ministry of Commerce mentioned that the US Department of the Treasury released the proposed rules on October 23 and sought public comments. The Ministry of Commerce urged the United States to respect the laws of the market economy, stop over-generalizing the concept of national security, and stop politicizing and weaponizing economic and trade issues.
