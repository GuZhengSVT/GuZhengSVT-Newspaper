Rapidus 2nm SRAM Yield Reportedly Zero, Putting FY2027 Mass Production Target Under Pressure

Rapidus 2纳米SRAM良率据报为零，2027财年量产目标承压

11月23日 08:15重大可靠性 B进展

据彭博社报道、TechNews转述，日本晶圆代工厂Rapidus采用环绕式闸极（GAA）架构的2纳米制程，未能在2026年夏季达成SRAM功能单元的关键开发里程碑，目前SRAM良率仍为0%。北海道千岁市IIM-1晶圆厂还传出无尘室微粒污染问题，使原定2027财年下半年量产的目标增添变数。

Rapidus成立于2022年，技术开发主要依赖IBM的2纳米制程合作。2025年7月，执行长小池淳义曾宣布IIM-1厂已成功制造出具预期电气特性的2纳米GAA晶体管。但单颗晶体管能运作，与整合成可用的高密度芯片是不同门槛；SRAM阵列高度规律，任何微小制程偏差都可能造成读写失效，因此SRAM良率常被视为评估先进制程整合能力的重要指标。

作为对照，台积电N2的256Mb SRAM测试晶片良率在2023年4月突破50%，2024年4月超过90%。Rapidus仍强调按原定计划，在2027财年下半年、即最迟2028年3月底前启动量产。不过启动量产并不等于达到稳定的大规模量产，在此之前仍需完成SRAM功能验证、大型阵列测试、良率提升与制程认证等阶段。

Bloomberg reported, as relayed by TechNews, that Japanese foundry Rapidus's 2-nanometer process using a gate-all-around (GAA) architecture failed to meet a key development milestone for SRAM functional units in summer 2026, and that its SRAM yield remains 0%. The IIM-1 fab in Chitose, Hokkaido, has also been reported to have a cleanroom particle contamination problem, adding uncertainty to the original target of mass production in the second half of fiscal 2027.

Rapidus was founded in 2022, and its technology development mainly depends on 2-nanometer process cooperation with IBM. In July 2025, CEO Atsuyoshi Koike announced that the IIM-1 fab had successfully manufactured 2-nanometer GAA transistors with the expected electrical characteristics. However, a single working transistor and integration into a usable high-density chip are different thresholds; SRAM arrays are highly regular, and any tiny process deviation may cause read/write failure, so SRAM yield is often regarded as an important indicator for assessing the integration capability of advanced processes.

By comparison, the yield of TSMC's N2 256Mb SRAM test chip exceeded 50% in April 2023 and 90% in April 2024. Rapidus still emphasizes that, according to the original plan, it will start mass production in the second half of fiscal 2027, or by the end of March 2028 at the latest. However, starting mass production does not equal achieving stable large-scale mass production; before that, it still needs to complete stages such as SRAM functional verification, large-array testing, yield improvement, and process certification.

Sugar-Based Hydrogel Strengthens Rather Than Fatigues After Repeated Bending

糖基水凝胶反复弯折后变强而非疲劳

11月22日 04:00重大可靠性 B

华盛顿大学圣路易斯分校化学教授Chenfeng Ke（Ke）领导的团队在《自然·材料》发表研究，报告一种由糖晶体和聚乙二醇构成的水凝胶在反复弯曲、拉伸后强度提高，而非像多数材料那样疲劳弱化。团队在模拟膝关节的柔性接头上弯折1万次，称材料表现与预测一致。

材料中的环糊精形成环状结构，拉伸使环糊精环簇解体并沿聚合物链滑动，材料松弛时重组为更小的结晶区域，从而在应变位置局部增强。研究人员认为这种机制类似胶原蛋白等生物材料，并设想用于人工关节或组织修复。

团队表示目前一次仅能制备数十克材料，医疗应用仍需与骨科和器械专家合作探索。

A team led by Chenfeng Ke (Ke), a chemistry professor at Washington University in St. Louis, published research in Nature Materials reporting that a hydrogel made of sugar crystals and polyethylene glycol increases in strength after repeated bending and stretching, rather than weakening from fatigue like most materials. The team bent the material 10,000 times on a flexible joint simulating a knee joint and said its behavior matched predictions.

华盛顿大学圣路易斯分校化学教授Chenfeng Ke（Ke）领导的团队在《自然·材料》发表研究，报告一种由糖晶体和聚乙二醇构成的水凝胶在反复弯曲、拉伸后强度提高，而非像多数材料那样疲劳弱化。团队在模拟膝关节的柔性接头上弯折1万次，称材料表现与预测一致。

Cyclodextrin in the material forms ring-shaped structures; stretching causes clusters of cyclodextrin rings to disassemble and slide along polymer chains, and when the material relaxes, they reassemble into smaller crystalline regions, thereby locally reinforcing the strained locations. The researchers believe this mechanism resembles biological materials such as collagen and envision applications in artificial joints or tissue repair.

The team said it can currently prepare only tens of grams of the material at a time, and medical applications still require exploration in collaboration with orthopedic and device experts.

Metal-free molecular electrocatalyst reduces oxygen to hydrogen peroxide

无金属分子电催化剂将氧还原为过氧化氢

11月20日 08:20重大可靠性 B

罗格斯大学纽瓦克分校与波恩大学研究人员在《自然通讯》发表研究，描述一种不含金属、也不使用氢气的过氧化氢制备方法：以氧、电流和质子源为原料，用分子电催化剂完成氧还原。

实验室结果显示，超过99%的电荷用于生成目标产物，催化剂转换频率很高；计算与实验将反应归为“自由基回弹”机制，即生成的过氧羟基自由基先与催化剂结合，再释放过氧化氢。

研究团队指出，催化剂稳定性和寿命仍需测试改进，反应放大及在水中的运行也待验证，目前尚处实验室阶段。

Researchers at Rutgers University–Newark and the University of Bonn have published a study in Nature Communications describing a method for producing hydrogen peroxide that uses neither metal nor hydrogen: with oxygen, electric current, and a proton source as inputs, a molecular electrocatalyst carries out oxygen reduction.

罗格斯大学纽瓦克分校与波恩大学研究人员在《自然通讯》发表研究，描述一种不含金属、也不使用氢气的过氧化氢制备方法：以氧、电流和质子源为原料，用分子电催化剂完成氧还原。

Laboratory results showed that more than 99% of the charge was used to generate the target product and that the catalyst’s turnover frequency was high; calculations and experiments attributed the reaction to a “radical rebound” mechanism, in which the generated hydroperoxyl radical first binds to the catalyst and then releases hydrogen peroxide.

实验室结果显示，超过99%的电荷用于生成目标产物，催化剂转换频率很高；计算与实验将反应归为“自由基回弹”机制，即生成的过氧羟基自由基先与催化剂结合，再释放过氧化氢。

The research team noted that the catalyst’s stability and lifetime still require testing and improvement and that scaling up the reaction and running it in water remain to be verified; the work is currently still at the laboratory stage.

研究团队指出，催化剂稳定性和寿命仍需测试改进，反应放大及在水中的运行也待验证，目前尚处实验室阶段。

New Test of Cosmic Microwave Background Polarization Rotation Yields 0.37-Degree Birefringence Estimate

检验宇宙微波背景偏振旋转的新方法给出0.37度双折射估计

11月17日 20:14重要可靠性 B进展

加州大学圣迭戈分校团队在《天体物理学报快报》发表一种检验宇宙微波背景偏振旋转的新方法，分析基于普朗克卫星的观测数据。

宇宙微波背景是宇宙约38万岁时的余辉，其偏振在传播中若发生极微小旋转（宇宙双折射），可能指向标准粒子模型之外的物理。困难在于望远镜偏振探测器若存在同等微小的对准误差，会制造几乎相同的假信号；研究改为比较不同探测器组生成的8张偏振图，真实信号应在各图中一致出现。

团队将相对定标结果与既有Minami-Komatsu分析给出的共有定标值结合，估计双折射角为0.37±0.12度，与该分析一致。由于方法扣除了所有偏振图共有的旋转，它不能独立测定绝对偏振角，仍需独立定标基准才能判断宇宙本身让光旋转了多少。

A University of California, San Diego team has published a new method for testing cosmic microwave background polarization rotation in The Astrophysical Journal Letters, with the analysis based on observations from the Planck satellite.

加州大学圣迭戈分校团队在《天体物理学报快报》发表一种检验宇宙微波背景偏振旋转的新方法，分析基于普朗克卫星的观测数据。

The cosmic microwave background is the afterglow from when the universe was about 380,000 years old; if its polarization undergoes an extremely tiny rotation during propagation (cosmic birefringence), this could point to physics beyond the standard particle model. The difficulty is that if a telescope's polarization detectors have an equally tiny alignment error, they can create an almost identical false signal; the study instead compares eight polarization maps generated by different detector groups, since a real signal should appear consistently in each map.

宇宙微波背景是宇宙约38万岁时的余辉，其偏振在传播中若发生极微小旋转（宇宙双折射），可能指向标准粒子模型之外的物理。困难在于望远镜偏振探测器若存在同等微小的对准误差，会制造几乎相同的假信号；研究改为比较不同探测器组生成的8张偏振图，真实信号应在各图中一致出现。

The team combined the relative calibration results with the common calibration value given by the existing Minami-Komatsu analysis and estimated the birefringence angle to be 0.37±0.12 degrees, consistent with that analysis. Because the method subtracts the rotation common to all polarization maps, it cannot independently determine the absolute polarization angle, and an independent calibration reference is still needed to judge how much the universe itself has rotated the light.

团队将相对定标结果与既有Minami-Komatsu分析给出的共有定标值结合，估计双折射角为0.37±0.12度，与该分析一致。由于方法扣除了所有偏振图共有的旋转，它不能独立测定绝对偏振角，仍需独立定标基准才能判断宇宙本身让光旋转了多少。

γ-Fe Persists in Chang'e-6 Lunar Soil Impact Glass and Forms a Stable Single-Magnetic-Vortex State

嫦娥六号月壤撞击玻璃中留存γ-Fe并形成稳定单磁涡旋

11月17日 00:00重要可靠性 A进展

月球已不存在全球性磁场，但月面仍留有磁化痕迹。这些痕迹来自月球内部活动还是撞击产生的瞬态磁场，一直缺少可靠的记录者。

中科院合肥物质科学研究院、地质与地球物理研究所等在嫦娥六号月壤的撞击玻璃颗粒中发现大量纳米级金属铁颗粒，其中部分颗粒的主要铁相是理论上无法在室温存在的γ-Fe。γ-Fe是铁的高温形态，一般只在900多摄氏度以上稳定，冷却后变回日常所见的α-Fe。

团队用离轴电子全息技术观察到，部分较大γ-Fe颗粒内部磁化沿环状排列，形成稳定的单磁涡旋态；外界磁场变化时结构响应稳定，具备保存磁场信息的潜力。

研究推测，撞击后的快速冷却、微量元素作用与周围玻璃的约束可能共同固定了这一结构。作者指出，该发现为理解月球古磁场记录提供新视角，但尚不能为古磁场来源定案，后续需结合磁学实验与微磁模拟评估其记录瞬态撞击场和发电机磁场的能力。成果发表在《美国国家科学院院刊》（PNAS）。

The Moon no longer has a global magnetic field, but magnetized traces remain on the lunar surface. Whether these traces come from lunar internal activity or from transient magnetic fields generated by impacts has long lacked a reliable recorder.

月球已不存在全球性磁场，但月面仍留有磁化痕迹。这些痕迹来自月球内部活动还是撞击产生的瞬态磁场，一直缺少可靠的记录者。

Researchers from the Hefei Institutes of Physical Science and the Institute of Geology and Geophysics of the Chinese Academy of Sciences, among other institutions, found numerous nanoscale metallic iron particles in impact glass particles from Chang'e-6 lunar soil, and in some of these particles the main iron phase is γ-Fe, which theoretically cannot exist at room temperature. γ-Fe is a high-temperature form of iron that is generally stable only above about 900 °C and reverts to the familiar α-Fe after cooling.

中科院合肥物质科学研究院、地质与地球物理研究所等在嫦娥六号月壤的撞击玻璃颗粒中发现大量纳米级金属铁颗粒，其中部分颗粒的主要铁相是理论上无法在室温存在的γ-Fe。γ-Fe是铁的高温形态，一般只在900多摄氏度以上稳定，冷却后变回日常所见的α-Fe。

Using off-axis electron holography, the team observed that within some larger γ-Fe particles the magnetization is arranged in a ring, forming a stable single-magnetic-vortex state; the structure responds stably to changes in the external magnetic field and has the potential to preserve magnetic field information.

团队用离轴电子全息技术观察到，部分较大γ-Fe颗粒内部磁化沿环状排列，形成稳定的单磁涡旋态；外界磁场变化时结构响应稳定，具备保存磁场信息的潜力。

The study speculates that rapid cooling after impact, the action of trace elements, and confinement by the surrounding glass may have jointly fixed this structure. The authors note that this finding offers a new perspective for understanding records of the Moon's ancient magnetic field, but it cannot yet settle the origin of the ancient magnetic field; future work needs to combine magnetic experiments and micromagnetic simulations to assess its ability to record transient impact fields and dynamo magnetic fields. The results were published in the Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences (PNAS).

Dual Near-Infrared Light-Controlled Switch Enables Implant-Free, On-Demand On–Off Neuromodulation

双近红外光控开关实现无植入、按需启停的神经调控

11月17日 00:00重要可靠性 A

中科院脑科学与智能技术卓越创新中心开发出名为“双近红外开关”的神经调控技术，用两束穿颅近红外光分别开启和终止神经元兴奋，成果发表在《美国化学会志》。

技术将近红外响应的双色正交发光上转换纳米颗粒与光敏蛋白SOUL组合：一次短暂980纳米照射即可启动神经元兴奋并自行维持，需要终止时再用808纳米照射复位。光照只在开关瞬间起作用，累计照射与随之而来的光热风险因此下降。
