DeepMind Demonstrates Antigravity Cheminformatics Skills, Says It Proposed Candidate Macrocycles in One Hour

DeepMind展示Antigravity化学信息学技能，称一小时提出候选大环

11月03日 23:00重要可靠性 A

Google DeepMind在11月3日的官方博客中展示，Google Antigravity配合新的Cheminformatics Skills可加速药物发现工作流。

该技能套件基于开源化学信息学工具RDKit，并连接ChEMBL和PubChem的整理资源；语言模型负责流程编排、数据库导航和科学推理，确定性化学工具执行分子计算。

官方以真实肺癌治疗药物为例，称代理检索数千种化合物、进行匹配分子对分析，并在约一小时内提出带合成路线的刚性大环候选。上述为官方演示结果。

In an official blog post on November 3, Google DeepMind demonstrated that Google Antigravity, combined with new Cheminformatics Skills, can accelerate drug discovery workflows.

Google DeepMind在11月3日的官方博客中展示，Google Antigravity配合新的Cheminformatics Skills可加速药物发现工作流。

The skills suite is based on the open-source cheminformatics toolkit RDKit and connects to curated resources from ChEMBL and PubChem; the language model handles workflow orchestration, database navigation, and scientific reasoning, while deterministic chemistry tools perform molecular calculations.

该技能套件基于开源化学信息学工具RDKit，并连接ChEMBL和PubChem的整理资源；语言模型负责流程编排、数据库导航和科学推理，确定性化学工具执行分子计算。

In an official example using a real lung cancer treatment drug, the agent was said to have retrieved thousands of compounds, performed matched molecular pair analysis, and proposed rigid macrocycle candidates with synthetic routes in about one hour. The above are results from an official demonstration.

官方以真实肺癌治疗药物为例，称代理检索数千种化合物、进行匹配分子对分析，并在约一小时内提出带合成路线的刚性大环候选。上述为官方演示结果。

openJiuwen launches AgentMemory multimodal memory, claims up to 97% lower latency in long-video question answering

openJiuwen上线AgentMemory多模态记忆，称长视频问答时延最高降97%

11月04日 10:49重要可靠性 B

openJiuwen在AgentMemory 0.2.0上线OmniMem-Lens多模态记忆能力，项目方称其通过视觉和听觉双流按真实事件切分长视频，并用三层记忆结构离线建库、在线检索。

项目方公布测试称，配合Qwen3-VL-8B，OmniMem-Lens在Video-MME-L上召回精度提升8%，LVbench提升6.4%，EgoLife-QA提升45.7%；在线问答时延最高降低97%，LVbench从34.5秒降至1.72秒。

该能力已在openJiuwen AgentMemory 0.2.0版本正式上线。

openJiuwen has launched the OmniMem-Lens multimodal memory capability in AgentMemory 0.2.0; the project team says it segments long videos by real events through visual and auditory dual streams and uses a three-layer memory structure for offline database construction and online retrieval.

openJiuwen在AgentMemory 0.2.0上线OmniMem-Lens多模态记忆能力，项目方称其通过视觉和听觉双流按真实事件切分长视频，并用三层记忆结构离线建库、在线检索。

The project team published tests claiming that, with Qwen3-VL-8B, OmniMem-Lens improved recall accuracy by 8% on Video-MME-L, by 6.4% on LVbench, and by 45.7% on EgoLife-QA; online question-answering latency fell by up to 97%, with LVbench dropping from 34.5 seconds to 1.72 seconds.

项目方公布测试称，配合Qwen3-VL-8B，OmniMem-Lens在Video-MME-L上召回精度提升8%，LVbench提升6.4%，EgoLife-QA提升45.7%；在线问答时延最高降低97%，LVbench从34.5秒降至1.72秒。

The capability has officially launched in openJiuwen AgentMemory version 0.2.0.

Envision says it deployed an 800 V DC power system at an AI data center in Ulanqab

远景称在乌兰察布AI数据中心部署800V直流供电系统

11月02日 22:13重大可靠性 B

据pv magazine 11月2日报道，远景能源称其在中国乌兰察布Galaxy AI数据中心部署了集成固态变压器和电池储能的800 V直流供电系统，并称这是全球首个在大型数据中心商业部署的集成800 V直流架构。

系统将10 kV中压电经固态变压器转换为800 V直流母线，电池储能提供二次支撑和分钟级备用，控制器以毫秒级协调以吸收GPU负载波动。

远景称Galaxy园区2026年8月投运，规划容量3 GW，预计80%以上电力直接来自可再生能源；公司还提出到2030年在沙漠地区建设5 GW绿色AI数据中心容量的Mission Gobi计划。上述“全球首个”和运行效果均为企业说法。

According to a November 2 report by pv magazine, Envision Energy said it has deployed an 800 V DC power supply system integrating solid-state transformers and battery energy storage at the Galaxy AI data center in Ulanqab, China, and claimed this is the world's first integrated 800 V DC architecture commercially deployed in a large data center.

据pv magazine 11月2日报道，远景能源称其在中国乌兰察布Galaxy AI数据中心部署了集成固态变压器和电池储能的800 V直流供电系统，并称这是全球首个在大型数据中心商业部署的集成800 V直流架构。

The system converts 10 kV medium-voltage electricity via solid-state transformers to an 800 V DC bus, while battery storage provides secondary support and minute-level backup, and controllers coordinate at the millisecond level to absorb GPU load fluctuations.

系统将10 kV中压电经固态变压器转换为800 V直流母线，电池储能提供二次支撑和分钟级备用，控制器以毫秒级协调以吸收GPU负载波动。

Envision said the Galaxy campus was put into operation in August 2026, with a planned capacity of 3 GW and an expected more than 80% of its power coming directly from renewable energy; the company also proposed its Mission Gobi plan to build 5 GW of green AI data center capacity in desert areas by 2030. The above “world's first” claim and the operating results are both company statements.

远景称Galaxy园区2026年8月投运，规划容量3 GW，预计80%以上电力直接来自可再生能源；公司还提出到2030年在沙漠地区建设5 GW绿色AI数据中心容量的Mission Gobi计划。上述“全球首个”和运行效果均为企业说法。

US Team Reports First Experimental Evidence of Helicon Wave Current Drive

美国团队报告螺旋波电流驱动首次实验证据

10月27日 22:29重大可靠性 B

据The Debrief 10月27日报道，美国通用原子公司团队在DIII-D托卡马克上首次获得螺旋波电流驱动的实验证据，相关论文发表在《物理评论快报》。研究用兆瓦级螺旋波系统向等离子体发射射频波，把能量和动量传给电子，并观测到与额外电流产生一致的磁场结构变化。

论文作者称，这些结果构成在任意装置上首次明确观测到螺旋波辅助电流驱动的强证据。该实验针对托卡马克依赖中心线圈、需中断运行重置的问题，但报道明确指出，它尚未证明可连续产生聚变电力。

According to an October 27 report by The Debrief, a team from General Atomics in the United States has obtained the first experimental evidence of helicon wave current drive on the DIII-D tokamak, and the related paper was published in Physical Review Letters. The study used a megawatt-class helicon wave system to launch radio-frequency waves into the plasma, transferring energy and momentum to electrons, and observed changes in the magnetic field structure consistent with the generation of additional current.

据The Debrief 10月27日报道，美国通用原子公司团队在DIII-D托卡马克上首次获得螺旋波电流驱动的实验证据，相关论文发表在《物理评论快报》。研究用兆瓦级螺旋波系统向等离子体发射射频波，把能量和动量传给电子，并观测到与额外电流产生一致的磁场结构变化。

The paper's authors say these results constitute strong evidence for the first clear observation of helicon-wave-assisted current drive on any device. The experiment addresses the problem that tokamaks rely on central coils and require interrupting operation to reset, but the report explicitly notes that it has not yet demonstrated that fusion electricity can be produced continuously.

论文作者称，这些结果构成在任意装置上首次明确观测到螺旋波辅助电流驱动的强证据。该实验针对托卡马克依赖中心线圈、需中断运行重置的问题，但报道明确指出，它尚未证明可连续产生聚变电力。

UK and EU Reach Draft Deal to Scrap Brexit Checks on Food Exports

英国与欧盟就取消食品出口脱欧检查达成草案协议

11月04日 04:53重要可靠性 B进展

据BBC报道，英国与欧盟已就取消食品和农产品出口脱欧检查达成草案协议，200页文本已分发欧盟国家，供周三会议审议。该协议被视为英国“脱欧重置”迄今最重要部分，但尚未正式签署。

协议拟允许向欧盟出口鲜香肠、汉堡、贝类、种薯等，取消200英镑出口健康证书和25英镑植物检疫证书等要求，并恢复英国宠物进入欧盟的宠物护照。作为交换，英国预计承诺动态对齐欧盟植物、动物和食品贸易标准。

英国政府称协议最终每年带来51亿英镑收益，首相称可额外提升GDP最多0.4%。这些是政府评估，协议仍需峰会确认。

According to the BBC, the UK and EU have reached a draft agreement to remove Brexit checks on food and agricultural exports, and a 200-page text has been distributed to EU countries for consideration at a meeting on Wednesday. The agreement is seen as the most important part so far of the UK's 'Brexit reset,' but it has not yet been formally signed.

据BBC报道，英国与欧盟已就取消食品和农产品出口脱欧检查达成草案协议，200页文本已分发欧盟国家，供周三会议审议。该协议被视为英国“脱欧重置”迄今最重要部分，但尚未正式签署。

The agreement would allow exports to the EU of fresh sausages, burgers, shellfish, seed potatoes and other products, remove requirements such as the £200 export health certificate and the £25 phytosanitary certificate, and restore pet passports for UK pets entering the EU. In exchange, the UK is expected to commit to dynamic alignment with EU standards for trade in plants, animals and food.

协议拟允许向欧盟出口鲜香肠、汉堡、贝类、种薯等，取消200英镑出口健康证书和25英镑植物检疫证书等要求，并恢复英国宠物进入欧盟的宠物护照。作为交换，英国预计承诺动态对齐欧盟植物、动物和食品贸易标准。

The UK government says the agreement will ultimately bring £5.1 billion in benefits each year, and the prime minister says it could boost GDP by up to an additional 0.4%. These are government assessments, and the agreement still needs confirmation at a summit.

英国政府称协议最终每年带来51亿英镑收益，首相称可额外提升GDP最多0.4%。这些是政府评估，协议仍需峰会确认。
