Canva Launches Craft, Its First In-House Image Generation Model, Which Plans Before It Generates

Canva发布首个自研图像生成模型Craft，先规划再生成

10月23日 05:51重要可靠性 B

Canva于10月22日发布其首个自研AI图像生成模型Canva Craft。据The Verge报道，该模型采用两阶段架构，先生成设计方案再生成图像，Canva称这带来“更高的精确度、一致性和控制力”。模型支持最高2K分辨率和30多种宽高比，可用于社交媒体内容、营销素材、写实场景和数字艺术等，并声称能更准确地生成需要清晰文字和精确物体位置的复杂图像。

Canva称Craft使用平台内容库中“数百万份人工制作的模板”训练。在众包基准Design Arena的图像生成模型排行榜上，该模型目前位列第四，落后于OpenAI的GPT-Image-2.5 Sunburst、GPT-Image-2.5 Flare和GPT-Image-2。Canva Craft已向全球所有用户开放，入口在Canva左侧菜单的Dream Lab。

Canva released Canva Craft, its first in-house AI image generation model, on October 22. According to The Verge, the model uses a two-stage architecture that first generates a design plan and then generates the image, which Canva says brings "greater precision, consistency, and control." The model supports resolutions up to 2K and more than 30 aspect ratios, and can be used for social media content, marketing materials, photorealistic scenes, and digital art, among other things, and it is claimed to generate complex images that require clear text and precise object placement more accurately.

China Telecom's Xingchen Lab releases and open-sources intent-understanding model Xing4.0-29B-Intent

中电信星辰实验室发布并开源意图理解专用模型Xing4.0-29B-Intent

10月22日 15:11重要可靠性 B

中电信人工智能科技有限公司星辰通用人工智能实验室发布并开源意图理解专用模型Xing4.0-29B-Intent，同步开放275.3万条监督微调训练语料、模型权重与推理镜像。模型基于星辰通用基座Xing4.0-29B-A4B微调，把意图识别与参数抽取结合，将用户一句话转换为业务系统可处理的结构化JSON信息，代码采用Apache 2.0许可。据量子位文章，该模型已在电信万号客服、智能家居音箱等场景应用。

技术团队公布的评测显示，在48个中英文公开数据集抽取的6000道题上，该模型总分为58，高于其参评的18款通用模型中最高总分49。第二组5606道类别判断题中，其总决策分为70，对比模型Jev为56、GPT-6-Luna为65。文章注明上述评测由该公司技术团队开展，反映本次数据和测试设置下的表现，不代表所有任务和实际部署场景。

The Xingchen Artificial General Intelligence Lab of China Telecom Artificial Intelligence Technology Co., Ltd. has released and open-sourced the intent-understanding dedicated model Xing4.0-29B-Intent, simultaneously opening 2.753 million supervised fine-tuning training corpus entries, the model weights and the inference image. The model is fine-tuned on the Xingchen general base model Xing4.0-29B-A4B, combines intent recognition with parameter extraction, and converts a user's single sentence into structured JSON information that business systems can process, with the code under the Apache 2.0 license. According to an article by QbitAI, the model has been applied in scenarios such as telecom ten-thousand-number customer service and smart home speakers.

中电信人工智能科技有限公司星辰通用人工智能实验室发布并开源意图理解专用模型Xing4.0-29B-Intent，同步开放275.3万条监督微调训练语料、模型权重与推理镜像。模型基于星辰通用基座Xing4.0-29B-A4B微调，把意图识别与参数抽取结合，将用户一句话转换为业务系统可处理的结构化JSON信息，代码采用Apache 2.0许可。据量子位文章，该模型已在电信万号客服、智能家居音箱等场景应用。

An evaluation published by the technical team shows that on 6,000 questions extracted from 48 Chinese and English public datasets, the model scored 58 in total, higher than the highest total score of 49 among the 18 general models it was evaluated against. In a second set of 5,606 category judgment questions, its total decision score was 70, compared with 56 for the comparison model Jev and 65 for GPT-6-Luna. The article notes that the above evaluation was conducted by the company's technical team, reflects performance under this data and test setup, and does not represent performance on all tasks and in actual deployment scenarios.

US Department of Energy Issues First Non-Exclusive License for TRIG Coal Gasification Technology

美国能源部首次就TRIG煤气化技术发出非独占许可

美国能源部碳氢化合物与地热能办公室10月15日宣布，该部已与Latitude Energy Services执行首个TRIG煤气化技术非独占许可，范围仅限电力领域。Latitude另独立持有该技术在工业领域的权利，此次许可与之互补。

TRIG是由美国能源部与产业伙伴联合开发的煤气化系统，设计针对低阶、高水分煤。能源部称，此次许可被视为该项联邦共同开发技术转移和商业化的重要节点，并为美国煤炭行业增加商业部署机会提供路径。

公告未披露许可费、项目装机规模、气化效率或成本数据，也未说明是否已有商业运行项目，因此尚不能判断该技术当前处于示范还是商业阶段。

The U.S. Department of Energy's Office of Hydrocarbons and Geothermal Energy announced on October 15 that the department has executed its first non-exclusive license for TRIG coal gasification technology with Latitude Energy Services, covering the power sector only. Latitude separately holds the rights to the technology in the industrial sector on its own, and this license is complementary to those rights.

美国能源部碳氢化合物与地热能办公室10月15日宣布，该部已与Latitude Energy Services执行首个TRIG煤气化技术非独占许可，范围仅限电力领域。Latitude另独立持有该技术在工业领域的权利，此次许可与之互补。

TRIG is a coal gasification system jointly developed by the U.S. Department of Energy and industry partners, designed for low-rank, high-moisture coal. The Department of Energy said the license is regarded as an important milestone in the transfer and commercialization of this federally co-developed technology, and provides a path to expand commercial deployment opportunities for the U.S. coal industry.

TRIG是由美国能源部与产业伙伴联合开发的煤气化系统，设计针对低阶、高水分煤。能源部称，此次许可被视为该项联邦共同开发技术转移和商业化的重要节点，并为美国煤炭行业增加商业部署机会提供路径。

Mathematicians Begin to Parse So-Called 'AI Proof' of the Quasi-Riemann Hypothesis: Significant but Details Not Yet Fully Public

Quanta Magazine 10月19日刊文称，在OpenAI于10月6日公布的377项结果中，有一项声称解决了“拟黎曼猜想”——黎曼猜想的弱化版本。该刊称，这与此前一些AI数学成果不同，已有人类专家对其展开解读。

Quanta Magazine reported on October 19 that among the 377 results OpenAI announced on October 6, one claims to have solved the "quasi-Riemann hypothesis," a weakened version of the Riemann hypothesis. The magazine said that, unlike some earlier AI results in mathematics, this one has already begun to be interpreted by human experts.

Quanta Magazine 10月19日刊文称，在OpenAI于10月6日公布的377项结果中，有一项声称解决了“拟黎曼猜想”——黎曼猜想的弱化版本。该刊称，这与此前一些AI数学成果不同，已有人类专家对其展开解读。

The Riemann hypothesis asserts that all non-trivial zeros of the Riemann zeta function have real part 1/2; it is closely tied to the distribution of prime numbers and has remained open for more than 160 years. The quasi-Riemann hypothesis is a weaker form: Maksym Radziwiłł of New York University told Quanta that most corollaries of the Riemann hypothesis can already be derived from the quasi-Riemann hypothesis, and that its implications are "amazing." Jared Duker Lichtman of Stanford called it the "most important result" AI has produced in mathematics to date, "and by a fairly wide margin."

黎曼猜想断言黎曼ζ函数的非平凡零点实部均为1/2，它与素数分布紧密相关，已悬置160余年。拟黎曼猜想是较弱形式：纽约大学Maksym Radziwiłł对Quanta表示，黎曼猜想的大部分推论已可由拟黎曼猜想推出，其影响“惊人”。斯坦福的Jared Duker Lichtman称，这是迄今AI在数学上“最重要的成果，且领先幅度相当大”。

The report said that, contrary to mathematicians' earlier expectations, the proof introduces no new concepts but instead combines known ideas in unexpected ways, drawing partly on techniques developed by Radziwiłł and Alexander Dunn of Georgia Tech and others. Number theorists are collaborating to understand the proof's contents, but the report did not say whether the proof has undergone formal review or replication.
