OpenAI rolls out GPT-Image-2.6 with GIF and SVG export

OpenAI上线GPT-Image-2.6，支持GIF与SVG导出

11月05日 09:59重要可靠性 B

据量子位11月5日报道，OpenAI的GPT-Image-2.6已在ChatGPT、Codex和API全量上线，免费与付费用户均可使用。

报道称该版本整体生成时间缩短50%；其中Flare型号在低画质档位下单张图生成时间最快6秒。该版本原生支持GIF动图，并可导出SVG矢量格式。

API端首次分出Flare和Sunburst两个型号，并把场景规划复杂度与画质拆成独立参数；Flare侧重速度和排版，Sunburst在中等和高画质档位下提供更高细节精度。

According to a November 5 report by QbitAI, OpenAI's GPT-Image-2.6 has been fully rolled out on ChatGPT, Codex and the API and is available to both free and paid users.

据量子位11月5日报道，OpenAI的GPT-Image-2.6已在ChatGPT、Codex和API全量上线，免费与付费用户均可使用。

The report said the version cuts overall generation time by 50%; in the low-quality tier, the Flare model can generate a single image in as little as 6 seconds. This version natively supports animated GIFs and can export SVG vector format.

报道称该版本整体生成时间缩短50%；其中Flare型号在低画质档位下单张图生成时间最快6秒。该版本原生支持GIF动图，并可导出SVG矢量格式。

On the API side, it is the first time the offering has been divided into two models, Flare and Sunburst, and scene-planning complexity and image quality have been split into independent parameters; Flare focuses on speed and layout, while Sunburst provides higher detail precision in the medium- and high-quality tiers.

API端首次分出Flare和Sunburst两个型号，并把场景规划复杂度与画质拆成独立参数；Flare侧重速度和排版，Sunburst在中等和高画质档位下提供更高细节精度。

OpenAI Says It Found 948 Linux Kernel Privilege-Escalation Vulnerabilities and Proposes Patches

OpenAI称在Linux内核发现948个提权漏洞并提出补丁建议

11月05日 09:54重要可靠性 B

据量子位11月5日报道，OpenAI称其使用一个尚未发布的网络安全模型搭建自动化流水线，在Linux内核中发现、验证并生成利用代码，最终锁定948个独立本地提权漏洞，成本约120万美元。

报道给出的流程是：先筛出约280万行高价值代码，12小时内提交2308个疑似漏洞；30小时内复现1724个内核崩溃，命中率74.7%；随后写出1169个成功拿到root权限的利用，合并同类变体后得到948个。

报道还称，OpenAI通过“Patch the Planet”项目将算力用于开源漏洞发现和修补，并已提出948个补丁建议。

According to a November 5 report by QbitAI, OpenAI said it used an unreleased cybersecurity model to build an automated pipeline that discovered, verified, and generated exploit code for vulnerabilities in the Linux kernel, ultimately identifying 948 distinct local privilege-escalation vulnerabilities at a cost of about $1.2 million.

据量子位11月5日报道，OpenAI称其使用一个尚未发布的网络安全模型搭建自动化流水线，在Linux内核中发现、验证并生成利用代码，最终锁定948个独立本地提权漏洞，成本约120万美元。

The process described in the report was as follows: first, select about 2.8 million lines of high-value code and submit 2,308 suspected vulnerabilities within 12 hours; second, reproduce 1,724 kernel crashes within 30 hours, for a hit rate of 74.7%; then, write 1,169 exploits that successfully gained root privileges, and after merging similar variants, obtain 948.

报道给出的流程是：先筛出约280万行高价值代码，12小时内提交2308个疑似漏洞；30小时内复现1724个内核崩溃，命中率74.7%；随后写出1169个成功拿到root权限的利用，合并同类变体后得到948个。

The report also said that OpenAI, through the "Patch the Planet" project, is directing compute toward discovering and patching open-source vulnerabilities and has already proposed 948 patches.

报道还称，OpenAI通过“Patch the Planet”项目将算力用于开源漏洞发现和修补，并已提出948个补丁建议。

Envision says it deployed an 800 V DC power system at an AI data center in Ulanqab

11月02日 22:13重大可靠性 B

据pv magazine 11月2日报道，远景能源称其在中国乌兰察布Galaxy AI数据中心部署了集成固态变压器和电池储能的800 V直流供电系统，并称这是全球首个在大型数据中心商业部署的集成800 V直流架构。

系统将10 kV中压电经固态变压器转换为800 V直流母线，电池储能提供二次支撑和分钟级备用，控制器以毫秒级协调以吸收GPU负载波动。

远景称Galaxy园区2026年8月投运，规划容量3 GW，预计80%以上电力直接来自可再生能源；公司还提出到2030年在沙漠地区建设5 GW绿色AI数据中心容量的Mission Gobi计划。上述“全球首个”和运行效果均为企业说法。

According to a November 2 report by pv magazine, Envision Energy said it has deployed an 800 V DC power supply system integrating solid-state transformers and battery energy storage at the Galaxy AI data center in Ulanqab, China, and claimed this is the world's first integrated 800 V DC architecture commercially deployed in a large data center.

据pv magazine 11月2日报道，远景能源称其在中国乌兰察布Galaxy AI数据中心部署了集成固态变压器和电池储能的800 V直流供电系统，并称这是全球首个在大型数据中心商业部署的集成800 V直流架构。

The system converts 10 kV medium-voltage electricity via solid-state transformers to an 800 V DC bus, while battery storage provides secondary support and minute-level backup, and controllers coordinate at the millisecond level to absorb GPU load fluctuations.

系统将10 kV中压电经固态变压器转换为800 V直流母线，电池储能提供二次支撑和分钟级备用，控制器以毫秒级协调以吸收GPU负载波动。

Envision said the Galaxy campus was put into operation in August 2026, with a planned capacity of 3 GW and an expected more than 80% of its power coming directly from renewable energy; the company also proposed its Mission Gobi plan to build 5 GW of green AI data center capacity in desert areas by 2030. The above “world's first” claim and the operating results are both company statements.

US Team Reports First Experimental Evidence of Helicon Wave Current Drive

美国团队报告螺旋波电流驱动首次实验证据

10月27日 22:29重大可靠性 B

据The Debrief 10月27日报道，美国通用原子公司团队在DIII-D托卡马克上首次获得螺旋波电流驱动的实验证据，相关论文发表在《物理评论快报》。研究用兆瓦级螺旋波系统向等离子体发射射频波，把能量和动量传给电子，并观测到与额外电流产生一致的磁场结构变化。

论文作者称，这些结果构成在任意装置上首次明确观测到螺旋波辅助电流驱动的强证据。该实验针对托卡马克依赖中心线圈、需中断运行重置的问题，但报道明确指出，它尚未证明可连续产生聚变电力。

According to an October 27 report by The Debrief, a team from General Atomics in the United States has obtained the first experimental evidence of helicon wave current drive on the DIII-D tokamak, and the related paper was published in Physical Review Letters. The study used a megawatt-class helicon wave system to launch radio-frequency waves into the plasma, transferring energy and momentum to electrons, and observed changes in the magnetic field structure consistent with the generation of additional current.

据The Debrief 10月27日报道，美国通用原子公司团队在DIII-D托卡马克上首次获得螺旋波电流驱动的实验证据，相关论文发表在《物理评论快报》。研究用兆瓦级螺旋波系统向等离子体发射射频波，把能量和动量传给电子，并观测到与额外电流产生一致的磁场结构变化。

The paper's authors say these results constitute strong evidence for the first clear observation of helicon-wave-assisted current drive on any device. The experiment addresses the problem that tokamaks rely on central coils and require interrupting operation to reset, but the report explicitly notes that it has not yet demonstrated that fusion electricity can be produced continuously.

论文作者称，这些结果构成在任意装置上首次明确观测到螺旋波辅助电流驱动的强证据。该实验针对托卡马克依赖中心线圈、需中断运行重置的问题，但报道明确指出，它尚未证明可连续产生聚变电力。
