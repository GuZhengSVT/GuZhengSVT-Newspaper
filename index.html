A study published online in Nature Materials on November 20 reported that the research team fabricated a high-loading all-solid-state lithium–sulfur battery cathode by partially lithiating the sulfur–sulfide interface and eliminating the electron-blocking oxide layer at the sulfur–carbon interface. Superconductivity research: electron pairs can remain intact at room temperature, and phase coherence may be the bottleneck for room-temperature superconductivity

超导研究：电子对可室温保持，相位相干或成室温超导瓶颈

11月18日 20:16重大可靠性 B单一来源报道

超导材料可让电流无电阻通过，但需要电子先结成库珀对，再让这些对保持同一量子相位、协同运动。常规超导体中，电子配对与这种集体协同在相近温度出现。

来自乌得勒支、莱顿和慕尼黑的研究人员在《科学》发表研究，发现超导所需的一个基本条件可在不依赖极端冷却的情况下满足：高温超导体中的电子对能在远高于超导转变的温度形成，并可在室温保持完整。

斯瓦特说，束缚电子对的“胶水”在室温下仍足够强，这提示第二个关键条件——相位相干——是瓶颈；如果理解了相位相干，或许能找到通往更高温度超导的路径。

这意味着研究展示的是两个关键条件之一；仅有电子对，材料仍不会超导。该发现把问题指向相位相干，并未把现有材料变成室温超导体。

Superconducting materials can allow current to pass without resistance, but electrons must first form Cooper pairs and then these pairs must maintain the same quantum phase and move in a coordinated way. In conventional superconductors, electron pairing and this collective coordination appear at similar temperatures.

超导材料可让电流无电阻通过，但需要电子先结成库珀对，再让这些对保持同一量子相位、协同运动。常规超导体中，电子配对与这种集体协同在相近温度出现。

Researchers from Utrecht, Leiden and Munich, in a study published in Science, found that one basic condition required for superconductivity can be met without relying on extreme cooling: electron pairs in high-temperature superconductors can form at temperatures far above the superconducting transition and remain intact at room temperature.

来自乌得勒支、莱顿和慕尼黑的研究人员在《科学》发表研究，发现超导所需的一个基本条件可在不依赖极端冷却的情况下满足：高温超导体中的电子对能在远高于超导转变的温度形成，并可在室温保持完整。

Swart said the “glue” binding electron pairs remains strong enough at room temperature, suggesting that the second key condition—phase coherence—is the bottleneck; if phase coherence is understood, it may be possible to find a path to superconductivity at higher temperatures.

斯瓦特说，束缚电子对的“胶水”在室温下仍足够强，这提示第二个关键条件——相位相干——是瓶颈；如果理解了相位相干，或许能找到通往更高温度超导的路径。

This means the study demonstrates one of the two key conditions; with electron pairs alone, the material still will not superconduct. The finding points the problem to phase coherence and does not turn existing materials into room-temperature superconductors.

这意味着研究展示的是两个关键条件之一；仅有电子对，材料仍不会超导。该发现把问题指向相位相干，并未把现有材料变成室温超导体。

Quanta Reports Progress on Perfect Resilience Network Theory: Four Structures Cause Routing Loops

11月20日 22:50重要可靠性 B单一来源报道

Quanta Magazine 11月20日报道，计算机科学家确定了四类简单网络结构；通信链路故障时，消息会在这些结构中陷入循环路由。报道称，这补全了“完美韧性”网络理论。

报道用图论解释：网络可看作节点与边构成的图，通过删节点、删边或合并相邻节点得到“minor”；若原图具有完美韧性，其所有minor也具有完美韧性。结合Robertson-Seymour定理，非完美韧性的minor只有有限多个。

Quanta Magazine reported on November 20 that computer scientists had identified four types of simple network structures, in which messages become trapped in routing loops when communication links fail. The report said that this completes the “perfect resilience” network theory.

Quanta Magazine 11月20日报道，计算机科学家确定了四类简单网络结构；通信链路故障时，消息会在这些结构中陷入循环路由。报道称，这补全了“完美韧性”网络理论。

The report explained using graph theory that a network can be regarded as a graph made up of nodes and edges, and that a “minor” is obtained by deleting nodes, deleting edges, or merging adjacent nodes; if the original graph has perfect resilience, all its minors also have perfect resilience. Combined with the Robertson-Seymour theorem, this means that there are only finitely many minors that do not have perfect resilience.

《自然》新闻11月24日报道，一项发表于《细胞报告》的研究分析了创伤后应激障碍（PTSD）和抑郁症患者脑细胞的DNA甲基化。研究纳入101名PTSD患者、101名抑郁症患者和100名无精神疾病对照的死后脑组织与病历。

DNA methylation is a chemical modification that adds methyl groups to or removes them from DNA; it does not change the DNA sequence but can affect how genes work. The team examined methylation in more than 850,000 genomic regions in the amygdala, hippocampus, and prefrontal cortex.

DNA甲基化是在DNA上添加或移除甲基的化学修饰，不改变DNA序列，但可影响基因如何工作。团队检测了杏仁核、海马和前额叶皮层中超过85万个基因组区域的甲基化。

相比对照组，PTSD患者前额叶皮层有四个DNA区域呈现不同甲基化模式，杏仁核和海马未发现同样差异。

细胞层面，PTSD患者杏仁核的寡突胶质细胞显著减少；在58名成年期经历创伤的供体中，三个脑区都出现这种减少，而97名童年期创伤供体未呈现同样模式。

Study Says Global Warming May Have Interrupted a New Ice Age That Began About 2,000 Years Ago

研究称全球变暖可能中断约2000年前开始的新冰期

11月24日 17:29重要可靠性 B单一来源报道

ScienceDaily 11月24日转述哥伦比亚气候学院的研究称，约2000年前，加拿大北极一处巨大冰盖的残余开始重新扩张；研究者认为，这可能是地球下一次冰期的开端。

研究地点是加拿大北极的Barnes Ice Cap。与多数冰川不同，它冻结在基岩上、不在景观上滑动，因此被扩张冰盖掩埋的古植物能原位保存，供放射性碳定年。

到1960年代，夏季升温扭转了这一趋势，冰盖转为退缩。研究据此提出，全球变暖可能中断了一次已经启动的新冰期。

ScienceDaily reported on November 24, citing research from Columbia Climate School, that about 2,000 years ago the remnants of a huge ice cap in the Canadian Arctic began to expand again; the researchers believe this may have been the start of Earth's next ice age.

ScienceDaily 11月24日转述哥伦比亚气候学院的研究称，约2000年前，加拿大北极一处巨大冰盖的残余开始重新扩张；研究者认为，这可能是地球下一次冰期的开端。

The research site is the Barnes Ice Cap in the Canadian Arctic. Unlike most glaciers, it is frozen to the bedrock and does not slide over the landscape, so ancient plants buried by the expanding ice cap can be preserved in place for radiocarbon dating.

By the 1960s, summer warming had reversed this trend, and the ice cap began to retreat. On this basis, the study suggests that global warming may have interrupted a new ice age that had already begun.
