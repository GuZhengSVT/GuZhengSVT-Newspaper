NVIDIA Releases Open-Weight Speech Model Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe, Combining Transcription, Speaker Diarization and Translation

NVIDIA开放语音模型Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe，整合转写、说话人分离与翻译

10月28日 22:58重要可靠性 A进展

NVIDIA在Hugging Face发布开放权重语音模型Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe，整合转写、说话人分离、翻译和音频问答，支持40种语言转写及31种语言与英语互译。

NVIDIA称该模型在Hugging Face开放ASR英文榜以5.33%平均词错误率居开放权重模型首位，在开放权重模型中AA-WER指数以2.8%并列第一。

同步开放Granary v2数据集与整理管线及NeMo Speech微调工具；NVIDIA称医疗场景微调使留出说话人词错误率较最佳提示降低45.8%，量化版未发布。

NVIDIA released the open-weight speech model Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe on Hugging Face, integrating transcription, speaker diarization, translation and audio question answering, with support for transcription in 40 languages and translation between 31 languages and English.

NVIDIA在Hugging Face发布开放权重语音模型Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe，整合转写、说话人分离、翻译和音频问答，支持40种语言转写及31种语言与英语互译。

NVIDIA says the model ranks first among open-weight models on the Hugging Face Open ASR English leaderboard with an average word error rate of 5.33%, and is tied for first among open-weight models with an AA-WER index of 2.8%.

NVIDIA称该模型在Hugging Face开放ASR英文榜以5.33%平均词错误率居开放权重模型首位，在开放权重模型中AA-WER指数以2.8%并列第一。

It also released the Granary v2 dataset and curation pipeline and the NeMo Speech fine-tuning tools; NVIDIA says fine-tuning for medical scenarios reduced the word error rate on held-out speakers by 45.8% compared with the best prompt, and a quantized version has not been released.

Three-dimensional imaging reveals internal structural patches in a cuprate superconductor, with wide boundaries potentially affecting superconductivity

三维成像发现铜氧化物超导体内部结构拼块，宽边界或影响超导性

10月25日 10:19重大可靠性 B

华威大学10月24日发布的新闻稿称，该校化学系团队与法国欧洲同步辐射设施合作，用扫描三维X射线衍射检查一种铜氧化物超导体内部。结果显示，晶体并非过去40年理论假设的均匀结构，而是由两种略有差异的原子排列区域拼合而成，区域间边界宽度达到预期值的数百倍。

研究负责人 Mark Senn 表示，这些异常宽的边界区域可能干扰超导性，而不只是与超导区域共存；这可能解释不同超导材料性能差异，并意味着部分块体测量需要重新解释。新闻稿称论文发表于 Physical Review Letters，但未给出具体材料名称及定量超导性能数据。

这项工作的意义在于把超导铜氧化物的微观结构不均匀性推到理论前台，但结构缺陷与超导性能之间的因果关系仍属研究者推断。新闻稿没有报告对超导转变温度或临界电流的同步测量，也没有说明该现象在同类材料中的普遍程度，研究者仅表示怀疑类似结构可能在其他铜氧化物中广泛存在。

A press release issued by the University of Warwick on October 24 stated that a team from the university's Department of Chemistry, in collaboration with the French European Synchrotron Radiation Facility, used scanning three-dimensional X-ray diffraction to examine the interior of a cuprate superconductor. The results showed that the crystal is not the uniform structure assumed by theory over the past 40 years, but is instead composed of patches of two slightly different atomic arrangements, with the boundaries between the regions reaching hundreds of times the expected width.

华威大学10月24日发布的新闻稿称，该校化学系团队与法国欧洲同步辐射设施合作，用扫描三维X射线衍射检查一种铜氧化物超导体内部。结果显示，晶体并非过去40年理论假设的均匀结构，而是由两种略有差异的原子排列区域拼合而成，区域间边界宽度达到预期值的数百倍。

Research lead Mark Senn said that these unusually wide boundary regions may interfere with superconductivity rather than merely coexisting with superconducting regions; this may explain performance differences among different superconducting materials and implies that some bulk measurements need to be reinterpreted. The press release stated that the paper was published in Physical Review Letters but did not give the specific material name or quantitative superconducting performance data.

The significance of this work lies in bringing the microstructural inhomogeneity of superconducting cuprates to the forefront of theory, but the causal relationship between structural defects and superconducting performance remains the researchers' inference. The press release did not report simultaneous measurements of the superconducting transition temperature or critical current, nor did it explain how common this phenomenon is among similar materials; the researchers only said they suspect that similar structures may be widespread in other cuprates.

Nature Energy study: Heatwaves may create capacity bottlenecks in Spain's power grid

Nature Energy研究：热浪或令西班牙电网出现容量瓶颈

10月22日 00:00重大可靠性 B

10月22日发表于《Nature Energy》的一项研究提出一套“热浪感知”的电网分析框架，把气温相关的用电需求、发电机组降额，以及沿输电线路逐段计算的热限，同时纳入交流最优潮流的计算中。作者称，现有线性化模型难以完整刻画高温下的热电耦合，而全国尺度的分段导体热物理计算此前在算力上难以实现。

研究团队以西欧电网为对象，结合公开电网数据、高分辨率天气再分析资料和用电数据，模拟了2026至2030年间数百个预估热浪情景。在所分析的电网配置下，各国表现差异明显：西班牙电网出现温度导致的输电瓶颈与切负荷，德国电网则相对更有韧性。作者据此认为，考虑热浪的潮流分析能揭示结构性瓶颈，应纳入电网规划与适应工作。

上述结论来自模型对未来热浪情景的模拟，并非实测电网运行数据，且针对研究设定的电网配置与情景范围，不能直接外推为对全部欧洲电网的判断。

A study published on October 22 in Nature Energy proposes a "heatwave-aware" power grid analysis framework that incorporates temperature-related electricity demand, generator derating, and segment-by-segment thermal limits along transmission lines into an AC optimal power flow calculation. The authors state that existing linearized models struggle to fully capture the thermal-electric coupling under high temperatures, while nationwide segment-by-segment conductor thermal physics calculations were previously computationally difficult to achieve.

10月22日发表于《Nature Energy》的一项研究提出一套“热浪感知”的电网分析框架，把气温相关的用电需求、发电机组降额，以及沿输电线路逐段计算的热限，同时纳入交流最优潮流的计算中。作者称，现有线性化模型难以完整刻画高温下的热电耦合，而全国尺度的分段导体热物理计算此前在算力上难以实现。

The research team took the Western European power grid as its object and, combining public grid data, high-resolution weather reanalysis data, and electricity demand data, simulated hundreds of projected heatwave scenarios for the period from 2026 to 2030. Under the grid configurations analyzed, performance varied markedly across countries: Spain's power grid experienced temperature-induced transmission bottlenecks and load shedding, while Germany's power grid was relatively more resilient. On this basis, the authors argue that heatwave-aware power flow analysis can reveal structural bottlenecks and should be incorporated into power grid planning and adaptation efforts.

These conclusions come from model simulations of future heatwave scenarios rather than measured power grid operation data, and, given the grid configurations and scenario scope set in the study, cannot be directly extrapolated into judgments about all European power grids.

Asymmetric Aluminum Single-Atom Sites Improve Sodium–Sulfur Battery Cycling Performance

非对称铝单原子位点改善钠硫电池循环性能

10月28日 00:00重要可靠性 A

中国科学院合肥物质科学研究院的研究团队在平面Al-O4配位结构中引入一个N配体，构建出非对称Al-O3N-C单原子位点，用于提升钠硫电池的反应性能。

研究称，N配体打破配位对称性并诱导电子局域化，使Al 2p结合能蓝移0.12 eV，增强铝单原子与多硫化钠之间的p-p轨道杂化，形成吸附—催化增强机制。

基于该催化剂的钠硫电池在0.1C电流密度下循环100次后容量保持1196 mAh g−1。相关成果发表在《德国应用化学》，研究得到国家重点研发计划和国家自然科学基金支持。

A research team at the Hefei Institutes of Physical Science, Chinese Academy of Sciences, introduced an N ligand into a planar Al-O4 coordination structure to construct asymmetric Al-O3N-C single-atom sites aimed at improving the reaction performance of sodium–sulfur batteries.

中国科学院合肥物质科学研究院的研究团队在平面Al-O4配位结构中引入一个N配体，构建出非对称Al-O3N-C单原子位点，用于提升钠硫电池的反应性能。

The study states that the N ligand breaks coordination symmetry and induces electron localization, causing the Al 2p binding energy to blue-shift by 0.12 eV, enhancing p-p orbital hybridization between the aluminum single atoms and sodium polysulfides, and forming an adsorption–catalysis enhancement mechanism.

研究称，N配体打破配位对称性并诱导电子局域化，使Al 2p结合能蓝移0.12 eV，增强铝单原子与多硫化钠之间的p-p轨道杂化，形成吸附—催化增强机制。

The sodium–sulfur battery based on this catalyst maintained a capacity of 1196 mAh g−1 after 100 cycles at a current density of 0.1C. The related results were published in Angewandte Chemie, and the research was supported by the National Key Research and Development Program and the National Natural Science Foundation of China.

基于该催化剂的钠硫电池在0.1C电流密度下循环100次后容量保持1196 mAh g−1。相关成果发表在《德国应用化学》，研究得到国家重点研发计划和国家自然科学基金支持。

T Cells Can Sense Tissue Stiffness and Influence Memory Cell Formation

T细胞可感知组织硬度并影响记忆细胞形成

10月28日 08:54重要可靠性 B进展

据麦吉尔大学发布的新闻稿，该校团队发现T细胞能感知周围组织的硬度，并利用这一物理信号影响自身是否形成长寿命记忆细胞。

研究者把人和小鼠的T细胞放入模拟不同硬度的胶原凝胶：凝胶越硬，T细胞越容易获得组织驻留记忆细胞的特征。

新闻稿称，这些结果表明周围组织的物理性质可影响免疫细胞发育，为理解免疫记忆增加机械力维度；研究人员下一步希望找出让T细胞感知物理环境的特定分子传感器。论文发表在Nature Immunology。

According to a press release from McGill University, a team at the university has found that T cells can sense the stiffness of surrounding tissue and use this physical signal to influence whether they themselves form long-lived memory cells.

据麦吉尔大学发布的新闻稿，该校团队发现T细胞能感知周围组织的硬度，并利用这一物理信号影响自身是否形成长寿命记忆细胞。

The researchers placed human and mouse T cells into collagen gels that mimicked different levels of stiffness: the stiffer the gel, the more readily the T cells acquired features of tissue-resident memory cells.

研究者把人和小鼠的T细胞放入模拟不同硬度的胶原凝胶：凝胶越硬，T细胞越容易获得组织驻留记忆细胞的特征。

The press release states that these results indicate that the physical properties of surrounding tissue can influence immune cell development, adding a mechanical dimension to the understanding of immune memory; the researchers next hope to identify the specific molecular sensors that enable T cells to sense their physical environment. The paper was published in Nature Immunology.

新闻稿称，这些结果表明周围组织的物理性质可影响免疫细胞发育，为理解免疫记忆增加机械力维度；研究人员下一步希望找出让T细胞感知物理环境的特定分子传感器。论文发表在Nature Immunology。
