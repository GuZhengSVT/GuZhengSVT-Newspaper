OpenAI Launches ChatGPT Wallet for Dots Agents, With Per-Purchase Approval Required

OpenAI推出ChatGPT Wallet，Dots代理可代购但需逐笔批准

11月14日 04:45重大可靠性 B

据The Verge报道，OpenAI推出面向Dots代理的ChatGPT Wallet，11月13日起向美国18岁以上的ChatGPT Pro订阅用户逐步开放。用户需使用美国账单地址和符合条件的美国发行美国运通、Chase、万事达或Visa卡。

添加卡片后，Dots代理可依据用户提示选购商品，但每次结账前都要用户确认，并通过Face ID或指纹等生物验证；支付时用支付令牌替代实际卡号，商户和代理不会获得完整卡信息。

OpenAI称未来将允许用户设置消费限额、购买类别或时段，并预先批准符合条件的购买，让代理完全代购。部分零售商已限制或屏蔽AI代理购物，OpenAI表示需要网站和支付风控系统识别代理发起的合法交易。

According to The Verge, OpenAI has launched ChatGPT Wallet for Dots agents, gradually opening it from November 13 to US ChatGPT Pro subscribers aged 18 and over. Users need a US billing address and an eligible US-issued American Express, Chase, Mastercard or Visa card.

据The Verge报道，OpenAI推出面向Dots代理的ChatGPT Wallet，11月13日起向美国18岁以上的ChatGPT Pro订阅用户逐步开放。用户需使用美国账单地址和符合条件的美国发行美国运通、Chase、万事达或Visa卡。

After a card is added, Dots agents can select products based on user prompts, but each checkout requires user confirmation through biometric authentication such as Face ID or a fingerprint; payments use a payment token instead of the actual card number, so merchants and agents do not receive full card information.

添加卡片后，Dots代理可依据用户提示选购商品，但每次结账前都要用户确认，并通过Face ID或指纹等生物验证；支付时用支付令牌替代实际卡号，商户和代理不会获得完整卡信息。

OpenAI says it will in the future allow users to set spending limits, purchase categories or time windows, and pre-approve eligible purchases so that agents can complete purchases entirely on their behalf. Some retailers have restricted or blocked AI agent shopping, and OpenAI says websites and payment risk-control systems need to identify legitimate transactions initiated by agents.

OpenAI称未来将允许用户设置消费限额、购买类别或时段，并预先批准符合条件的购买，让代理完全代购。部分零售商已限制或屏蔽AI代理购物，OpenAI表示需要网站和支付风控系统识别代理发起的合法交易。

llama.cpp定位为跨平台本地AI推理框架，通过量化和专用后端在消费级设备到企业服务器上运行多模态大模型。项目称目前支持150多种架构变体和主流投机解码方法，并为Linux、macOS、Windows、Android、iOS及多个包管理器提供预发布构件。

ggml-org released llama.cpp v1.0.0 and ggml v1.0.0 on November 13. The project calls this a stability milestone for downstream developers: future incompatible changes to the public C APIs of llama.cpp and ggml will increment the major version number, backward-compatible features, backends or model support will increment the minor version number, and fixes without API changes will increment the patch number.

CGN Connects First 25 MW Unit of Tibet Ngari CSP Plant to Grid

中广核西藏阿里光热电站首台25兆瓦机组并网

11月09日 20:49重大可靠性 B进展

据能源媒体pv magazine 11月9日报道，中广核11月6日将西藏阿里50兆瓦光热电站首台25兆瓦机组并入区域电网。该电站位于噶尔县，是150兆瓦示范项目的一部分，包含100兆瓦光伏和50兆瓦光热，总投资约24亿元。

电站平均海拔4300米，中广核称其为全球海拔最高在运并网光热电站。项目配两台25兆瓦汽轮机组，第二台计划2026年底并网以达到满额定容量。

该电站用富余光伏电力驱动峰值205兆瓦熔盐电加热器，配置设计14小时储热和锂离子电池。中广核称这是首个整合光伏电加热、熔盐储热和异步电池储能的CSP设施，并称205兆瓦电加热器为全球CSP应用最大；报道指出两项说法均未获独立验证。

电站还作为热电联产设施，在冬季为阿里地区行政中心狮泉河提供区域供热。报道同时指出，其光伏电加热、储热和热电联产系统在持续运行、尤其是冬季条件下的性能仍待验证。

Energy media pv magazine reported on November 9 that CGN connected the first 25 MW unit of its 50 MW Tibet Ngari concentrated solar power (CSP) plant to the regional grid on November 6. Located in Gar County, the plant is part of a 150 MW demonstration project comprising 100 MW photovoltaic and 50 MW CSP, with total investment of about 2.4 billion yuan.

据能源媒体pv magazine 11月9日报道，中广核11月6日将西藏阿里50兆瓦光热电站首台25兆瓦机组并入区域电网。该电站位于噶尔县，是150兆瓦示范项目的一部分，包含100兆瓦光伏和50兆瓦光热，总投资约24亿元。

At an average altitude of 4,300 meters, CGN says it is the world's highest-altitude grid-connected CSP plant in operation. The project is equipped with two 25 MW steam turbine units, and the second is planned to be connected to the grid by the end of 2026 to reach full rated capacity.

电站平均海拔4300米，中广核称其为全球海拔最高在运并网光热电站。项目配两台25兆瓦汽轮机组，第二台计划2026年底并网以达到满额定容量。

The plant uses surplus photovoltaic power to drive 205 MW peak molten-salt electric heaters, with a designed 14-hour heat storage and lithium-ion batteries. CGN says this is the first CSP facility integrating photovoltaic electric heating, molten-salt heat storage and asynchronous battery storage, and calls the 205 MW electric heater the largest for CSP application globally; the report notes that neither claim has been independently verified.

该电站用富余光伏电力驱动峰值205兆瓦熔盐电加热器，配置设计14小时储热和锂离子电池。中广核称这是首个整合光伏电加热、熔盐储热和异步电池储能的CSP设施，并称205兆瓦电加热器为全球CSP应用最大；报道指出两项说法均未获独立验证。

The plant also serves as a combined heat and power facility, providing district heating in winter to Shiquanhe, the administrative center of the Ngari Prefecture. 11月09日 00:00重大可靠性 B

Nature Energy 11月9日刊发的一项研究，利用美国批发电价记录、输电计划和监管成本分摊案卷，分析数据中心扩张如何通过不同渠道加重美国能源负担。

研究称，到2025年，在受影响最严重的批发市场枢纽，数据中心导致的累计电价涨幅超过10%；在受监管电价中，居民用户按中位数承担47%的成本，尽管其对需求增长的贡献小得多。

研究还估计，成本分担使农村社区承担的负担增幅比城市社区高76%，最低收入家庭的额外能源负担最高达28个百分点。地方经济回报与支出几乎无关，支出弹性为0.16；承担最大能源负担增幅的州获得的回报最小。

A study published in Nature Energy on November 9 uses U.S. wholesale electricity price records, transmission plans, and regulatory cost-allocation dockets to analyze how data center expansion adds to the U.S. energy burden through different channels.

Nature Energy 11月9日刊发的一项研究，利用美国批发电价记录、输电计划和监管成本分摊案卷，分析数据中心扩张如何通过不同渠道加重美国能源负担。

The study says that by 2025, at the most severely affected wholesale market hubs, cumulative electricity price increases caused by data centers exceeded 10%; in regulated electricity rates, residential customers bear a median 47% of the costs, even though their contribution to demand growth is much smaller.

研究称，到2025年，在受影响最严重的批发市场枢纽，数据中心导致的累计电价涨幅超过10%；在受监管电价中，居民用户按中位数承担47%的成本，尽管其对需求增长的贡献小得多。

The study also estimates that cost-sharing makes the increase in burden borne by rural communities 76% higher than that borne by urban communities, and that the additional energy burden for the lowest-income households reaches as high as 28 percentage points. Local economic returns are almost unrelated to spending, with a spending elasticity of 0.16; the states bearing the largest increase in energy burden receive the smallest returns.

Pseudogap and electron-pairing signals coincide in cuprate superconductor

铜氧化物超导体中赝能隙与电子配对信号重合

11月10日 01:07重要可靠性 B

据The Brighter Side of News经雅虎新闻转载的报道，乌得勒支大学、莱顿大学和慕尼黑大学的研究人员测量了铜氧化物超导体Bi-2212，相关论文发表于《Science》。他们试图把长期争议的赝能隙与电子配对联系起来。

研究团队用可探测散粒噪声的扫描隧道显微镜测量。结果显示，配对能远大于按超导转变温度推算的尺度；配对信号出现的能量位置与赝能隙相符。作者据此认为，限制超导温度的是电子对之间的相位相干（保持量子协同），而非配对强度不足。

实验在极低温度下进行，没有展示普通温度下无电阻导电。报道称，该结果排除了仅用局域有序解释所测赝能隙，但不排除配对与其他电子序共存，也未解决所有铜氧化物中的赝能隙问题。

According to a report by The Brighter Side of News republished via Yahoo News, researchers from Utrecht University, Leiden University, and the University of Munich measured the cuprate superconductor Bi-2212, and the related paper was published in Science. They sought to link the long-debated pseudogap with electron pairing.

据The Brighter Side of News经雅虎新闻转载的报道，乌得勒支大学、莱顿大学和慕尼黑大学的研究人员测量了铜氧化物超导体Bi-2212，相关论文发表于《Science》。他们试图把长期争议的赝能隙与电子配对联系起来。

The research team used a scanning tunneling microscope capable of detecting shot noise. The results showed that the pairing energy was far larger than the scale estimated from the superconducting transition temperature; the energy position at which the pairing signal appeared matched the pseudogap. The authors therefore concluded that what limits the superconducting temperature is phase coherence between electron pairs (maintaining quantum coordination), rather than insufficient pairing strength.

研究团队用可探测散粒噪声的扫描隧道显微镜测量。结果显示，配对能远大于按超导转变温度推算的尺度；配对信号出现的能量位置与赝能隙相符。作者据此认为，限制超导温度的是电子对之间的相位相干（保持量子协同），而非配对强度不足。

The experiment was conducted at extremely low temperatures and did not demonstrate resistance-free conduction at ordinary temperatures. The report said that the result ruled out explaining the measured pseudogap solely through local order, but did not rule out the coexistence of pairing and other electronic orders, nor did it resolve the pseudogap problem in all cuprates.

实验在极低温度下进行，没有展示普通温度下无电阻导电。报道称，该结果排除了仅用局域有序解释所测赝能隙，但不排除配对与其他电子序共存，也未解决所有铜氧化物中的赝能隙问题。

New species of giant rodent discovered in Thailand, dating back about 34 million years

泰国发现约3400万年前巨型啮齿动物新种

11月11日 09:36重要可靠性 B

据麦吉尔大学发布的新闻稿（由ScienceDaily刊载），一个国际古生物团队在泰国甲米府邦马克矿坑发现两枚牙齿化石，将其命名为新种Yakamys sirindhornae。化石包括一枚下第二臼齿和一枚下门齿，年代约3400万年前。

研究者用七种基于牙齿测量的体重估算方法，推算其体重约30公斤，约为同时代已知啮齿动物的两倍。由于只有两枚牙齿，这一体重只是粗略估计，误差较大。

论文发表于《Scientific Reports》。研究者认为，这一发现表明巨型啮齿动物的出现时间更早、分布范围也比原先认识更广，东南亚在大型啮齿动物早期演化中扮演重要角色。

According to a press release from McGill University published by ScienceDaily, an international paleontology team discovered two fossil teeth in the Bang Mark pit in Krabi Province, Thailand, and named them as a new species, Yakamys sirindhornae. The fossils include a lower second molar and a lower incisor and date to about 34 million years ago.

据麦吉尔大学发布的新闻稿（由ScienceDaily刊载），一个国际古生物团队在泰国甲米府邦马克矿坑发现两枚牙齿化石，将其命名为新种Yakamys sirindhornae。化石包括一枚下第二臼齿和一枚下门齿，年代约3400万年前。

Using seven body-mass estimation methods based on tooth measurements, the researchers estimated its body mass at about 30 kilograms, roughly twice that of known rodents from the same period. Because there are only two teeth, this body-mass estimate is only rough and has a relatively large margin of error.

研究者用七种基于牙齿测量的体重估算方法，推算其体重约30公斤，约为同时代已知啮齿动物的两倍。由于只有两枚牙齿，这一体重只是粗略估计，误差较大。

The paper was published in Scientific Reports. The researchers suggest that this discovery shows giant rodents appeared earlier and had a broader distribution than previously recognized, and that Southeast Asia played an important role in the early evolution of large rodents.

论文发表于《Scientific Reports》。研究者认为，这一发现表明巨型啮齿动物的出现时间更早、分布范围也比原先认识更广，东南亚在大型啮齿动物早期演化中扮演重要角色。

Ionospheric Negative Storms Can Extend Flare-Induced Shortwave Interruptions to About 73 Minutes

电离层负暴可将耀斑所致短波中断延长至约73分钟

11月11日 00:00重要可靠性 A

中国科学院国家空间科学中心利用子午工程中国双子极光雷达网六部中纬高频雷达，分析2024年5月超级太阳风暴期间的观测，研究电离层负暴对耀斑所致短波中断的影响。

结果显示，仅由耀斑引发的中断约持续24分钟，受耀斑和负暴双重影响的中断平均约73分钟。研究认为，负暴期间O/N2比值下降、F层电子密度降低，可能改变高频信号折射和传播路径，降低最高可用频率。

团队还观测到，并非所有短波中断前都有Doppler flash；纯耀斑事件中出现约1分钟的正向上升，峰值约137米/秒，耀斑与负暴叠加事件中则没有。研究称这对建立复杂空间天气下的短波中断预报系统有理论价值。

Using six mid-latitude high-frequency radars of the China Dual Auroral Radar Network under the Meridian Project, the National Space Science Center of the Chinese Academy of Sciences analyzed observations during the May 2024 super solar storm to study the effects of ionospheric negative storms on flare-induced shortwave interruptions.

中国科学院国家空间科学中心利用子午工程中国双子极光雷达网六部中纬高频雷达，分析2024年5月超级太阳风暴期间的观测，研究电离层负暴对耀斑所致短波中断的影响。

The results show that interruptions caused by flares alone lasted about 24 minutes, while those affected by both flares and negative storms lasted about 73 minutes on average. The study suggests that during negative storms, a decrease in the O/N2 ratio and lower F-layer electron density may alter the refraction and propagation paths of high-frequency signals and reduce the maximum usable frequency.

结果显示，仅由耀斑引发的中断约持续24分钟，受耀斑和负暴双重影响的中断平均约73分钟。研究认为，负暴期间O/N2比值下降、F层电子密度降低，可能改变高频信号折射和传播路径，降低最高可用频率。

The team also observed that not all shortwave interruptions were preceded by a Doppler flash; pure flare events showed a positive rise of about 1 minute with a peak of about 137 m/s, whereas flare-plus-negative-storm events showed none. The study says this has theoretical value for building a shortwave interruption forecasting system under complex space weather conditions.

团队还观测到，并非所有短波中断前都有Doppler flash；纯耀斑事件中出现约1分钟的正向上升，峰值约137米/秒，耀斑与负暴叠加事件中则没有。研究称这对建立复杂空间天气下的短波中断预报系统有理论价值。

China releases new medical insurance drug catalog: 107 medicines added, including 51 Class 1 new drugs

中国新版医保药品目录公布：新增107种药品，含51个一类新药

11月14日 10:44重要可靠性 B

国家医保局11月14日在苏州发布2026年国家医保药品目录和商业健康保险创新药品目录。新版医保目录新增107种药品，其中51个为一类新药，数量再创历史新高，新增品种覆盖肿瘤、罕见病、慢性病、精神疾病等领域。

商业健康保险创新药品目录新增12种药品，包括全球首个实体瘤CAR-T、中国首个血友病基因治疗药物，调整后共28种。新版医保目录药品总数达到3331个，其中西药1935个、中成药1396个，将于2027年1月1日落地执行。

The National Healthcare Security Administration released the 2026 national medical insurance drug catalog and the commercial health insurance innovative drug catalog in Suzhou on November 14. The new medical insurance catalog adds 107 drugs, including 51 Class 1 new drugs, a number that once again set a record high, with the newly added products covering areas such as tumors, rare diseases, chronic diseases and mental illness.

国家医保局11月14日在苏州发布2026年国家医保药品目录和商业健康保险创新药品目录。新版医保目录新增107种药品，其中51个为一类新药，数量再创历史新高，新增品种覆盖肿瘤、罕见病、慢性病、精神疾病等领域。

The commercial health insurance innovative drug catalog adds 12 drugs, including the world's first CAR-T therapy for solid tumors and China's first gene therapy drug for hemophilia, bringing the total to 28 after the adjustment. The new medical insurance catalog contains a total of 3,331 drugs, including 1,935 Western medicines and 1,396 traditional Chinese patent medicines, and will take effect on January 1, 2027.

商业健康保险创新药品目录新增12种药品，包括全球首个实体瘤CAR-T、中国首个血友病基因治疗药物，调整后共28种。新版医保目录药品总数达到3331个，其中西药1935个、中成药1396个，将于2027年1月1日落地执行。

FAA Lifts Nationwide Ground Stop After More Than Four Hours as Air Traffic Control Communications System Overloads

美联邦航空局解除全国停飞令：空管通信系统过载，逾四小时后复飞

11月14日 11:10重大可靠性 B进展

美国联邦航空管理局（FAA）13日发布全国停飞令，禁止所有国内起飞航班，停止运行超过四小时，至美东时间当晚20时15分解除。这是2001年9月11日以来最长的一次全国范围停飞。

据航班追踪网站FlightAware统计，13日超过8500架次航班延误、近8000架次取消，停飞时已有3500多架飞机在空中。欧洲空管组织Eurocontrol称，应FAA请求暂停成员国赴美航班。

交通部长达菲在记者会上说，一套老式模拟空管通信系统的交换机和路由器因数据过载宕机，影响通信与雷达，系统需全面改造，估计耗资500亿美元；达菲称该系统还会继续故障。国土安全部长马林称没有证据显示是网络攻击，并排除与前一天开始的全球微软故障有关。两位部长都称停飞期间公众没有危险。

The U.S. Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) issued a nationwide ground stop on the 13th, barring all domestic departing flights and halting operations for more than four hours until it was lifted at 8:15 p.m. Eastern Time that evening. This was the longest nationwide ground stop since September 11, 2001.

美国联邦航空管理局（FAA）13日发布全国停飞令，禁止所有国内起飞航班，停止运行超过四小时，至美东时间当晚20时15分解除。这是2001年9月11日以来最长的一次全国范围停飞。

According to statistics from flight-tracking website FlightAware, more than 8,500 flights were delayed and nearly 8,000 were canceled on the 13th, while more than 3,500 aircraft were already in the air when the ground stop began. The European air traffic control organization Eurocontrol said it suspended member states' flights to the United States at the FAA's request.

据航班追踪网站FlightAware统计，13日超过8500架次航班延误、近8000架次取消，停飞时已有3500多架飞机在空中。欧洲空管组织Eurocontrol称，应FAA请求暂停成员国赴美航班。

Transportation Secretary Duffy said at a press conference that the switches and routers of an old analog air traffic control communications system had crashed due to data overload, affecting communications and radar; the system needs a comprehensive overhaul estimated to cost $50 billion, and Duffy said it would continue to fail. Homeland Security Secretary Malin said there was no evidence that it was a cyberattack and ruled out any link to the global Microsoft outage that began the previous day. Both ministers said there was no danger to the public during the ground stop.
