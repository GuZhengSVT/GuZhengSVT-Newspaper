Phison Shows Enterprise SSD Design With Built-in NPU That Can Run AI Frameworks

Phison展示内置NPU的企业级SSD设计，可运行AI框架

11月22日 09:23重要可靠性 B

存储控制器厂商Phison在芝加哥SC26超算展上展示了一款企业级SSD设计，把NPU和64GB板上内存放进盘内，内存带宽130GB/s，可运行PyTorch、vLLM、OpenVINO和llama.cpp等AI框架。

Phison称，该设计针对合适负载可把数据传输量减少10至100倍：数据在盘内并行扫描和处理，只把相关结果回传主机，而不必搬运整个数据集。其NPU性能用DMIPS衡量，没有采用业界常见的TOPS。

目前公布的信息有限，控制器、接口、外形规格和存储类型均未说明，相关产品仍在开发中，应视为概念验证而非可售产品。

Phison, a storage controller maker, showcased an enterprise SSD design at the SC26 supercomputing show in Chicago that places an NPU and 64GB of on-board memory inside the drive, provides 130GB/s of memory bandwidth, and can run AI frameworks such as PyTorch, vLLM, OpenVINO, and llama.cpp.

存储控制器厂商Phison在芝加哥SC26超算展上展示了一款企业级SSD设计，把NPU和64GB板上内存放进盘内，内存带宽130GB/s，可运行PyTorch、vLLM、OpenVINO和llama.cpp等AI框架。

Phison says the design can reduce data transfer volume by 10 to 100 times for suitable workloads: data is scanned and processed in parallel inside the drive, and only relevant results are returned to the host instead of moving the entire dataset. Its NPU performance is measured in DMIPS rather than the industry-common TOPS.

Phison称，该设计针对合适负载可把数据传输量减少10至100倍：数据在盘内并行扫描和处理，只把相关结果回传主机，而不必搬运整个数据集。其NPU性能用DMIPS衡量，没有采用业界常见的TOPS。

Information disclosed so far is limited, with the controller, interface, form factor, and storage type all unspecified; the related product is still under development and should be viewed as a proof of concept rather than a sellable product.

目前公布的信息有限，控制器、接口、外形规格和存储类型均未说明，相关产品仍在开发中，应视为概念验证而非可售产品。
