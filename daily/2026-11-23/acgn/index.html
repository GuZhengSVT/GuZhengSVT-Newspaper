Zenless Zone Zero Version 3.4 Launches December 2 With Honkai: Star Rail Crossover

《绝区零》3.4版本12月2日上线，联动《崩坏：星穹铁道》

11月23日 11:28重大可靠性 B进展

据HTH Gaming报道，HoYoverse将于2026年12月2日发布《绝区零》3.4版本“The Finality of the Day”。更新把罗斯卡利弗天空岛篇推向高潮，并开启与《崩坏：星穹铁道》的联动，Robin与Kafka将前往新艾利都。

报道称，Sunbringer以S级“Genesis Flare Armorer”身份加入，HoYoverse称其为新Void Hunter、游戏首位Enchainer Agent。Kafka在版本下半段登场，同期复刻S级物理异常角色Alice及S级异常W-Engine“Practiced Perfection”。

玩家更新后登录可免费领取联动S级Robin，并获得升至40级的升级材料和整套主属性匹配的Drive Discs。版本登陆PS5、PS5 Pro、Xbox Series X|S、Xbox Cloud Gaming、PC、iOS与Android，并通过Steam登陆PC。

According to HTH Gaming, HoYoverse will release Zenless Zone Zero Version 3.4, “The Finality of the Day,” on December 2, 2026. The update brings the Roscalifer Sky Island arc to its climax and launches a crossover with Honkai: Star Rail, with Robin and Kafka heading to New Eridu.

据HTH Gaming报道，HoYoverse将于2026年12月2日发布《绝区零》3.4版本“The Finality of the Day”。更新把罗斯卡利弗天空岛篇推向高潮，并开启与《崩坏：星穹铁道》的联动，Robin与Kafka将前往新艾利都。

According to the report, Sunbringer joins as an S-rank “Genesis Flare Armorer,” which HoYoverse describes as a new Void Hunter and the game’s first Enchainer Agent. Kafka appears in the second half of the version, alongside reruns of the S-rank Physical Anomaly character Alice and the S-rank Anomaly W-Engine “Practiced Perfection.”

报道称，Sunbringer以S级“Genesis Flare Armorer”身份加入，HoYoverse称其为新Void Hunter、游戏首位Enchainer Agent。Kafka在版本下半段登场，同期复刻S级物理异常角色Alice及S级异常W-Engine“Practiced Perfection”。

Players who log in after the update can claim the crossover S-rank Robin for free and receive upgrade materials to raise her to level 40, along with a full set of Drive Discs with matching main stats. The version will be available on PS5, PS5 Pro, Xbox Series X|S, Xbox Cloud Gaming, PC, iOS, and Android, as well as on PC via Steam.

玩家更新后登录可免费领取联动S级Robin，并获得升至40级的升级材料和整套主属性匹配的Drive Discs。版本登陆PS5、PS5 Pro、Xbox Series X|S、Xbox Cloud Gaming、PC、iOS与Android，并通过Steam登陆PC。

Three-Player PvP Action Game Arkheron Opens Steam Early Access on November 28

三人PvP动作游戏《Arkheron》11月28日开启Steam抢先体验

11月23日 07:00重要可靠性 B进展

据4Gamer报道，三人组队PvP动作游戏《Arkheron》计划于11月28日在Steam开启抢先体验，售价2980日元；报道页面列出的平台包括PC、PS5和Xbox Series X|S。

游戏以由生者世界记忆筑成的谜之塔为舞台。玩家扮演受无法回忆的过去束缚的角色，为逃离导致塔崩坏的深渊而向塔顶战斗；塔在破坏与再生中变化，战士“Eternal”们的过去也会逐步揭示。

游戏支持Friend Pass：一名玩家购买后，另外两人可加入其队伍免费游玩，并支持跨平台联机。

According to 4Gamer, the three-player team-based PvP action game Arkheron is scheduled to open Steam Early Access on November 28 at a price of 2,980 yen; the platforms listed on the report page include PC, PS5, and Xbox Series X|S.

据4Gamer报道，三人组队PvP动作游戏《Arkheron》计划于11月28日在Steam开启抢先体验，售价2980日元；报道页面列出的平台包括PC、PS5和Xbox Series X|S。

The game is set in a mysterious tower built from the memories of the world of the living. Players take on the roles of characters bound by a past they cannot remember, fighting toward the top of the tower to escape the abyss that causes the tower to collapse; the tower changes through destruction and regeneration, and the pasts of the warriors called “Eternal” are also gradually revealed.

游戏以由生者世界记忆筑成的谜之塔为舞台。玩家扮演受无法回忆的过去束缚的角色，为逃离导致塔崩坏的深渊而向塔顶战斗；塔在破坏与再生中变化，战士“Eternal”们的过去也会逐步揭示。

The game supports Friend Pass: after one player purchases it, two others can join that player’s team and play for free, and cross-platform multiplayer is also supported.

游戏支持Friend Pass：一名玩家购买后，另外两人可加入其队伍免费游玩，并支持跨平台联机。

来源：4gamer.net

据17173热点预告，《怪物猎人：崛起》Netflix版定档11月24日推出，支持手机端游玩。Netflix订阅用户可直接体验游戏本体和全部DLC内容。

This version supports online team play to challenge monsters, and the report says it recreates the core hunting experience of the console version; playing it requires a Netflix subscription.

Novel 'Sono Mono. Nochi ni...' Gets 2027 TV Anime With Kento Ito, Yui Ogura

小说《その者。のちに…》2027年TV动画化，伊东健人、小仓唯配音

11月22日 19:00重要可靠性 B进展

コミックナタリー11月22日报道，ナハァト的小说《その者。のちに…》将于2027年改编为TV动画，主人公ワズ由伊東健人配音，メアル由小倉唯配音。

动画由寿門堂制作，大畑晃一担任总监督；伊東健人还演唱片头主题曲。

原作在“小説家になろう”发表，经アース・スターノベル书籍化，并有成家慎一郎的漫画版。故事讲述失恋后山笼获得规格外力量的ワズ为追求后宫而旅行，与女主角们展开吵闹冒险。

Comic Natalie reported on November 22 that Nahaato's novel 'Sono Mono. Nochi ni...' will be adapted into a TV anime in 2027, with protagonist Waz voiced by Kento Ito and Mearu by Yui Ogura.

コミックナタリー11月22日报道，ナハァト的小说《その者。のちに…》将于2027年改编为TV动画，主人公ワズ由伊東健人配音，メアル由小倉唯配音。

The anime is produced by Jumondo, with Koichi Ohata as chief director; Kento Ito will also sing the opening theme.

动画由寿門堂制作，大畑晃一担任总监督；伊東健人还演唱片头主题曲。

The original work was published on 'Shosetsuka ni Naro,' was released in book form by Earth Star Novel, and has a manga adaptation by Shin'ichiro Nariie. The story follows Waz, who gains extraordinary power after a heartbreak in mountain seclusion, as he travels in pursuit of a harem and sets off on rowdy adventures with the heroines.

原作在“小説家になろう”发表，经アース・スターノベル书籍化，并有成家慎一郎的漫画版。故事讲述失恋后山笼获得规格外力量的ワズ为追求后宫而旅行，与女主角们展开吵闹冒险。

来源：natalie.mu
