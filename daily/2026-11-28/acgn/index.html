Hollow Knight: Silksong Free Expansion 'Sea of Sorrow' Set for December 11

《Hollow Knight: Silksong》免费扩展“Sea of Sorrow”定档12月11日

11月27日 23:11重大可靠性 B单一来源报道

据游戏媒体Gematsu报道，Team Cherry宣布《Hollow Knight: Silksong》的免费扩展包“Sea of Sorrow”将于12月11日上线。

按Team Cherry在报道中的说法，玩家将在Pharloom被遗忘的海洋上方和下方航行，穿越岛屿、堡垒和水下洞穴；扩展包含疯狂角色、冒险和史诗Boss，以及Christopher Larkin创作的音乐。

《Hollow Knight: Silksong》目前已登陆PlayStation 5、Xbox Series、Switch 2、PlayStation 4、Xbox One、Switch和PC，PC版通过Steam、GOG、Humble Store与Microsoft Store发售。

According to game media outlet Gematsu, Team Cherry announced that the free expansion pack "Sea of Sorrow" for Hollow Knight: Silksong will launch on December 11.

据游戏媒体Gematsu报道，Team Cherry宣布《Hollow Knight: Silksong》的免费扩展包“Sea of Sorrow”将于12月11日上线。

As stated by Team Cherry in the report, players will sail above and below the forgotten sea of Pharloom, traversing islands, fortresses, and underwater caves; the expansion includes mad characters, adventures, and epic bosses, as well as music created by Christopher Larkin.

按Team Cherry在报道中的说法，玩家将在Pharloom被遗忘的海洋上方和下方航行，穿越岛屿、堡垒和水下洞穴；扩展包含疯狂角色、冒险和史诗Boss，以及Christopher Larkin创作的音乐。

Hollow Knight: Silksong is currently available on PlayStation 5, Xbox Series, Switch 2, PlayStation 4, Xbox One, Switch, and PC, with the PC version sold through Steam, GOG, Humble Store, and Microsoft Store.

《Hollow Knight: Silksong》目前已登陆PlayStation 5、Xbox Series、Switch 2、PlayStation 4、Xbox One、Switch和PC，PC版通过Steam、GOG、Humble Store与Microsoft Store发售。

Team Cherry launches publishing brand Presents, announces two new PC games

Team Cherry设立发行品牌Presents，公布两款PC新作

11月27日 23:10重要可靠性 B单一来源报道

据Gematsu报道，Team Cherry设立新发行品牌Team Cherry Presents，并公布两款PC新作：Makoto Koji开发的植物解谜冒险游戏《The Botanist and the Translator》，以及Studio Stobie开发的平台冒险游戏《Radio Rain》。 两款游戏都将通过Steam登陆PC，发售日期均未公布。

《The Botanist and the Translator》让植物学家Hatti与翻译Oliver调查一颗被黏稠花粉覆盖的星球，用植物学技能解决问题，并配有Yunomi的管弦乐。 《Radio Rain》讲述女孩Cass被传送到风暴笼罩的陌生世界，释放火焰精灵Ember后与其一同寻找回家之路，并配有lofi电台音乐。

According to Gematsu, Team Cherry has established a new publishing label, Team Cherry Presents, and announced two new PC games: the plant puzzle adventure game The Botanist and the Translator, developed by Makoto Koji, and the platform adventure game Radio Rain, developed by Studio Stobie. Both games will launch on PC via Steam; release dates for both have not been announced.

据Gematsu报道，Team Cherry设立新发行品牌Team Cherry Presents，并公布两款PC新作：Makoto Koji开发的植物解谜冒险游戏《The Botanist and the Translator》，以及Studio Stobie开发的平台冒险游戏《Radio Rain》。 两款游戏都将通过Steam登陆PC，发售日期均未公布。

The Botanist and the Translator follows botanist Hatti and translator Oliver as they investigate a planet covered in sticky pollen, using botanical skills to solve problems, and features orchestral music by Yunomi. Radio Rain follows a girl named Cass, who is transported to a strange, storm-shrouded world and, after releasing the fire spirit Ember, searches with Ember for a way home, and features lofi radio music.

《The Botanist and the Translator》让植物学家Hatti与翻译Oliver调查一颗被黏稠花粉覆盖的星球，用植物学技能解决问题，并配有Yunomi的管弦乐。 《Radio Rain》讲述女孩Cass被传送到风暴笼罩的陌生世界，释放火焰精灵Ember后与其一同寻找回家之路，并配有lofi电台音乐。

"The Streamer’s Alt Account Labyrinth" Sets January 29, 2027 PC Release

《The Streamer’s Alt Account Labyrinth》定档2027年1月29日登陆PC

11月27日 20:15重要可靠性 B单一来源报道

据Gematsu报道，发行商G-MODE与开发商Acacia宣布，地牢RPG与视觉小说结合的《The Streamer’s Alt Account Labyrinth》将于2027年1月29日通过Steam登陆PC，Switch版稍后推出。

游戏支持英语、日语、繁体中文和简体中文。

故事围绕全女子乐队Rag Drops被指控犯罪后，成员进入社交媒体阴暗面具象化的异世界UraU；玩法分为地牢探索与充满秘密的冒险段落，玩家要在迷宫中追查“里账号”的主人。

According to Gematsu, publisher G-MODE and developer Acacia have announced that "The Streamer’s Alt Account Labyrinth," a blend of dungeon RPG and visual novel, will launch on PC via Steam on January 29, 2027, with a Switch version to follow later.

据Gematsu报道，发行商G-MODE与开发商Acacia宣布，地牢RPG与视觉小说结合的《The Streamer’s Alt Account Labyrinth》将于2027年1月29日通过Steam登陆PC，Switch版稍后推出。

The game will support English, Japanese, Traditional Chinese, and Simplified Chinese.

游戏支持英语、日语、繁体中文和简体中文。

The story centers on the all-female band Rag Drops, whose members, after being accused of a crime, enter UraU, an otherworld where the dark side of social media is made manifest; the gameplay is divided into dungeon exploration and adventure segments full of secrets, with players tasked with tracking down the owner of the "alt account" in the labyrinth.

Windrose’s Major Ashlands Update to Launch This Winter, Unveiling New Land and Naval Combat Features

《Windrose》大型更新Ashlands今冬上线，公开陆战与海战新要素

11月27日 23:24重要可靠性 B单一来源报道

据4Gamer报道，Pocketpair于11月27日公开《Windrose》大型更新“Ashlands”的开发博客第6弹，更新计划于今年冬季上线。

新区域Ashlands的陆战会出现为财宝而来的英国士兵和拥有棘手能力的骷髅。部分骷髅被击倒后会重新拼合散落的骨头站起，重装骷髅则需先破坏其体干、再剥下防具。

海战中，全海域现有敌船会按战斗角色使用特殊能力，包括在常规火炮射程外炮击、冲撞、召唤援军、投下火药桶，以及接近后爆炸的小型船；除自爆小船外，敌船角色不按船种固定。

According to 4Gamer, Pocketpair published the sixth development blog post for Windrose’s major “Ashlands” update on November 27, and the update is scheduled to launch this winter.

据4Gamer报道，Pocketpair于11月27日公开《Windrose》大型更新“Ashlands”的开发博客第6弹，更新计划于今年冬季上线。

Land combat in the new Ashlands region will feature British soldiers drawn by treasure and skeletons with troublesome abilities. Some skeletons will reassemble their scattered bones and rise after being knocked down, while heavily armored skeletons must have their torsos destroyed first before their armor can be stripped off.

新区域Ashlands的陆战会出现为财宝而来的英国士兵和拥有棘手能力的骷髅。部分骷髅被击倒后会重新拼合散落的骨头站起，重装骷髅则需先破坏其体干、再剥下防具。

In naval combat, existing enemy ships across all sea areas will use special abilities according to their combat roles, including bombarding from beyond conventional cannon range, ramming, summoning reinforcements, dropping gunpowder barrels, and small boats that explode after closing in; except for the self-detonating small boats, enemy ship roles are not fixed by ship type.

海战中，全海域现有敌船会按战斗角色使用特殊能力，包括在常规火炮射程外炮击、冲撞、召唤援军、投下火药桶，以及接近后爆炸的小型船；除自爆小船外，敌船角色不按船种固定。
