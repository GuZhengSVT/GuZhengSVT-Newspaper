OpenAI Countersues Apple, Alleging Trade Secret Theft

OpenAI反诉Apple，指控窃取商业秘密

11月28日 03:44重要可靠性 B单一来源报道

据Engadget 11月27日报道，OpenAI已对Apple提起反诉，指控Apple窃取商业秘密，并称Apple“胁迫和威胁”同时与两家公司合作的供应商。该报道称相关信息来自《金融时报》。

报道称，这起诉讼是对Apple此前起诉OpenAI的回应。Apple在OpenAI收购由前Apple设计主管Jony Ive领导的io后提起诉讼，指控OpenAI窃取秘密信息。

报道还称，OpenAI指控一名前员工返回Apple后分享了产品计划和供应商信息。Engadget称Apple尚未对此置评。

According to an Engadget report on November 27, OpenAI has filed a counterclaim against Apple, alleging that Apple stole trade secrets and saying that Apple “coerced and threatened” suppliers that work with both companies. The report said the information came from the Financial Times.

据Engadget 11月27日报道，OpenAI已对Apple提起反诉，指控Apple窃取商业秘密，并称Apple“胁迫和威胁”同时与两家公司合作的供应商。该报道称相关信息来自《金融时报》。

The report said the lawsuit was a response to Apple’s earlier lawsuit against OpenAI. Apple filed the lawsuit after OpenAI acquired io, which was led by former Apple design chief Jony Ive, alleging that OpenAI stole confidential information.

报道称，这起诉讼是对Apple此前起诉OpenAI的回应。Apple在OpenAI收购由前Apple设计主管Jony Ive领导的io后提起诉讼，指控OpenAI窃取秘密信息。

The report also said that OpenAI alleged that a former employee shared product plans and supplier information after returning to Apple. Engadget said Apple had not yet commented.

报道还称，OpenAI指控一名前员工返回Apple后分享了产品计划和供应商信息。Engadget称Apple尚未对此置评。

China Successfully Launches Yaogan-50 04 Satellite

我国成功发射遥感五十号04星

11月28日 08:21重要可靠性 B单一来源报道

央视新闻客户端报道，北京时间11月27日21时05分，我国在太原卫星发射中心使用长征六号改运载火箭，成功将遥感五十号04星发射升空，卫星顺利进入预定轨道，发射任务取得圆满成功。

该报道称，该卫星主要用于国土普查、农作物估产和防灾减灾等领域。

此次任务是长征系列运载火箭的第682次飞行。

CCTV News app reported that at 21:05 Beijing time on November 27, China successfully launched the Yaogan-50 04 satellite into space using a Long March 6A carrier rocket at the Taiyuan Satellite Launch Center; the satellite smoothly entered its planned orbit, and the launch mission achieved complete success.

央视新闻客户端报道，北京时间11月27日21时05分，我国在太原卫星发射中心使用长征六号改运载火箭，成功将遥感五十号04星发射升空，卫星顺利进入预定轨道，发射任务取得圆满成功。

The report said the satellite is mainly used for land surveys, crop yield estimation, and disaster prevention and mitigation, among other fields.

该报道称，该卫星主要用于国土普查、农作物估产和防灾减灾等领域。

This mission was the 682nd flight of the Long March series of carrier rockets.

此次任务是长征系列运载火箭的第682次飞行。

EE Times: Huawei to Disclose Custom HBM Design at ISSCC 2027

EE Times：华为将在ISSCC 2027披露定制HBM设计

11月27日 16:56重要可靠性 B单一来源报道

据EE Times 11月27日报道，华为将在明年年初于旧金山举行的ISSCC 2027上介绍其定制高带宽存储器（HBM）设计。报道称，华为本月早些时候向潜在客户展示昇腾960DT AI加速器时，要求客户自行采购HBM。

报道还称，HBM供应目前由三星电子和SK海力士主导。

According to a November 27 report by EE Times, Huawei will present its custom high-bandwidth memory (HBM) design at ISSCC 2027 in San Francisco early next year. The report said that when Huawei showed its Ascend 960DT AI accelerator to potential customers earlier this month, it asked customers to source HBM themselves.

据EE Times 11月27日报道，华为将在明年年初于旧金山举行的ISSCC 2027上介绍其定制高带宽存储器（HBM）设计。报道称，华为本月早些时候向潜在客户展示昇腾960DT AI加速器时，要求客户自行采购HBM。

The report also said that HBM supply is currently dominated by Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix.

报道还称，HBM供应目前由三星电子和SK海力士主导。
