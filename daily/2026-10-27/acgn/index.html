Auroch Digital Confirms Roughly 25% Layoffs, Warhammer Support to Continue

Auroch Digital 确认裁员约 25%，Warhammer 系列后续支持继续

10月27日 02:29重要可靠性 B

《Warhammer 40,000: Boltgun II》与《Warhammer Survivors》开发商 Auroch Digital 在社交媒体发布声明，确认已启动裁员咨询程序，影响约四分之一岗位。公司称，尽管过去一年推出了 Boltgun II 和《Starship Troopers: Ultimate Bug War!》等作品，但行业市场环境已波及自身，因此决定重组。

Auroch Digital 表示，Warhammer Survivors 和 Warhammer 40,000: Boltgun II 的开发与发售后支持将继续进行，并呼吁有招聘需求的同行考虑接收受影响员工。Rock Paper Shotgun 报道称，该工作室发布的相关帖子已被删除。

Auroch Digital, the developer of "Warhammer 40,000: Boltgun II" and "Warhammer Survivors," said in a statement on social media that it has begun a redundancy consultation process affecting about a quarter of its positions. The company said that although it released titles including Boltgun II and "Starship Troopers: Ultimate Bug War!" over the past year, industry market conditions have affected it as well, prompting the decision to restructure.

《Warhammer 40,000: Boltgun II》与《Warhammer Survivors》开发商 Auroch Digital 在社交媒体发布声明，确认已启动裁员咨询程序，影响约四分之一岗位。公司称，尽管过去一年推出了 Boltgun II 和《Starship Troopers: Ultimate Bug War!》等作品，但行业市场环境已波及自身，因此决定重组。

Auroch Digital said development and post-launch support for Warhammer Survivors and Warhammer 40,000: Boltgun II will continue, and called on peers with hiring needs to consider taking on affected employees. Rock Paper Shotgun reported that the studio's related post has been deleted.

Auroch Digital 表示，Warhammer Survivors 和 Warhammer 40,000: Boltgun II 的开发与发售后支持将继续进行，并呼吁有招聘需求的同行考虑接收受影响员工。Rock Paper Shotgun 报道称，该工作室发布的相关帖子已被删除。

Sucker Punch Productions Reportedly Lays Off 26 Employees

据报 Sucker Punch Productions 裁员 26 人

10月27日 02:10重要可靠性 B

据 Aftermath 援引一名前员工的报道，索尼互动娱乐旗下工作室 Sucker Punch Productions 已裁员 26 人，该工作室员工总数不足 200 人。Sucker Punch 曾开发《对马岛之魂》，最新作品《羊蹄山之魂》于 2025 年 10 月 2 日登陆 PlayStation 5。

Aftermath 查阅的工作室内部邮件称，公司在观察市场挑战并审视下一项目团队需求后决定裁员，受影响岗位涉及多个部门，并称下周将公布更多未来细节。该邮件内容由 Gematsu 转述，索尼与 Sucker Punch 尚未公开发布确认。

According to a report by Aftermath citing a former employee, Sucker Punch Productions, a studio under Sony Interactive Entertainment, has laid off 26 people, and the studio's total workforce is fewer than 200. Sucker Punch developed Ghost of Tsushima, and its latest title, Ghost of Yotei, launched on PlayStation 5 on October 2, 2025.

据 Aftermath 援引一名前员工的报道，索尼互动娱乐旗下工作室 Sucker Punch Productions 已裁员 26 人，该工作室员工总数不足 200 人。Sucker Punch 曾开发《对马岛之魂》，最新作品《羊蹄山之魂》于 2025 年 10 月 2 日登陆 PlayStation 5。

An internal studio email reviewed by Aftermath said the company decided to lay off staff after observing market challenges and reviewing the team needs for its next project, that the affected positions span multiple departments, and that more details about the future will be announced next week. The contents of the email were relayed by Gematsu, and Sony and Sucker Punch have not yet issued a public confirmation.

Aftermath 查阅的工作室内部邮件称，公司在观察市场挑战并审视下一项目团队需求后决定裁员，受影响岗位涉及多个部门，并称下周将公布更多未来细节。该邮件内容由 Gematsu 转述，索尼与 Sucker Punch 尚未公开发布确认。

Assassin's Creed IV Black Flag Resynced New Game+ Update Launches October 27

《刺客信条：黑旗 重同步》New Game+ 更新 10 月 27 日上线

10月27日 01:16重要可靠性 B

育碧宣布，《刺客信条：黑旗 重同步》的最终大型内容更新将于 10 月 27 日上线，加入 New Game+ 模式。玩家可继承爱德华的全部工具、升级、已解锁武器和饰品，以及寒鸦号的升级、官员能力和宠物；金钱、材料、贸易货物、服装、藏身处进度和船队船只也将保留。

New Game+ 中，要塞需重新占领，地图迷雾重置，官员需重新招募；每次通关后海上威胁提升，精英士兵出现更频繁，最高等级船只开始巡逻，难度可累计至 New Game+++++（五周目）。更新还加入 New Game+ 专属货币、新处决动作和服装。

Ubisoft has announced that the final major content update for Assassin's Creed IV Black Flag Resynced will launch on October 27, adding a New Game+ mode. Players can carry over all of Edward's tools, upgrades, unlocked weapons and accessories, as well as the Jackdaw's upgrades, officer abilities and pets; money, materials, trade goods, outfits, hideout progress and fleet ships will also be retained.

育碧宣布，《刺客信条：黑旗 重同步》的最终大型内容更新将于 10 月 27 日上线，加入 New Game+ 模式。玩家可继承爱德华的全部工具、升级、已解锁武器和饰品，以及寒鸦号的升级、官员能力和宠物；金钱、材料、贸易货物、服装、藏身处进度和船队船只也将保留。

In New Game+, forts must be recaptured, the map fog resets and officers must be recruited again; after each playthrough, maritime threats increase, elite soldiers appear more frequently, top-level ships begin patrolling, and difficulty can stack up to New Game+++++ (five playthroughs). The update also adds New Game+-exclusive currency, new execution animations and outfits.

New Game+ 中，要塞需重新占领，地图迷雾重置，官员需重新招募；每次通关后海上威胁提升，精英士兵出现更频繁，最高等级船只开始巡逻，难度可累计至 New Game+++++（五周目）。更新还加入 New Game+ 专属货币、新处决动作和服装。

Marvel Cosmic Invasion Second DLC "Castle Doom Siege" Reveals Castle Mode Gameplay

《漫威宇宙入侵》第二弹 DLC“末日城堡围攻”公布城堡模式玩法

10月27日 00:04重要可靠性 B

发行商 Dotemu 与开发商 Tribute Games 公布《漫威宇宙入侵》付费 DLC“末日城堡围攻”的新模式“Castle Doom”。该模式以毁灭博士的末日城堡为舞台，围攻槽会随英雄被击败而积累，玩家需收集电池为稳定器充能、完成挑战获取防御、伤害或战术升级，并在围攻槽蓄满前抵达毁灭博士处。

据 Dotemu 公告，该模式的设计围绕里德·理查兹与毁灭博士的对立展开，每次通关可解锁新装备、事件和修正项，并解锁角色新配色。DLC 将于 11 月 10 日发售，新增隐形女侠与毁灭博士两名可玩角色；《漫威宇宙入侵》本体已登陆 PS5、Xbox Series、Switch 2、PS4、Xbox One、Switch 和 PC。

Publisher Dotemu and developer Tribute Games have announced "Castle Doom," a new mode for Marvel Cosmic Invasion's paid DLC "Castle Doom Siege." Set in Doctor Doom's Castle Doom, the mode fills a siege gauge as heroes are defeated, requiring players to collect batteries to charge stabilizers, complete challenges to earn defensive, damage, or tactical upgrades, and reach Doctor Doom before the siege gauge fills up.

发行商 Dotemu 与开发商 Tribute Games 公布《漫威宇宙入侵》付费 DLC“末日城堡围攻”的新模式“Castle Doom”。该模式以毁灭博士的末日城堡为舞台，围攻槽会随英雄被击败而积累，玩家需收集电池为稳定器充能、完成挑战获取防御、伤害或战术升级，并在围攻槽蓄满前抵达毁灭博士处。

According to Dotemu's announcement, the mode's design centers on the conflict between Reed Richards and Doctor Doom, and each clear unlocks new equipment, events, and modifiers, as well as new color schemes for characters. The DLC launches on November 10 and adds Invisible Woman and Doctor Doom as playable characters; Marvel Cosmic Invasion is already available on PS5, Xbox Series, Switch 2, PS4, Xbox One, Switch, and PC.

据 Dotemu 公告，该模式的设计围绕里德·理查兹与毁灭博士的对立展开，每次通关可解锁新装备、事件和修正项，并解锁角色新配色。DLC 将于 11 月 10 日发售，新增隐形女侠与毁灭博士两名可玩角色；《漫威宇宙入侵》本体已登陆 PS5、Xbox Series、Switch 2、PS4、Xbox One、Switch 和 PC。
