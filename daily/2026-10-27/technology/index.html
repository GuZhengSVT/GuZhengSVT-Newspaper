Apple unveils iPad mini with A20 Pro, pre-orders open October 28

苹果发布搭载A20 Pro的iPad mini，10月28日预售

10月26日 21:00重要可靠性 A进展

苹果10月26日发布新款iPad mini，称这是该产品线迄今最大幅度更新。新机采用8.5英寸OLED屏幕，峰值亮度1200尼特，机身厚度5.4毫米，首次具备IP67防尘防水能力，并提供纳米纹理屏幕选项。

新款iPad mini搭载采用2纳米工艺的A20 Pro芯片，7核GPU每个核心内置神经加速器，苹果称AI性能最高提升4.5倍、图形性能提升2.7倍。新机10月28日开启预售，11月6日上市。

Apple released the new iPad mini on October 26, calling it the biggest update to the product line to date. The new model features an 8.5-inch OLED display with a peak brightness of 1,200 nits, a 5.4mm-thick body, IP67 dust and water resistance for the first time, and an optional nano-texture display.

苹果10月26日发布新款iPad mini，称这是该产品线迄今最大幅度更新。新机采用8.5英寸OLED屏幕，峰值亮度1200尼特，机身厚度5.4毫米，首次具备IP67防尘防水能力，并提供纳米纹理屏幕选项。

The new iPad mini is powered by the A20 Pro chip built on a 2nm process, with a 7-core GPU that has a neural accelerator built into each core; Apple says AI performance is up to 4.5 times faster and graphics performance up to 2.7 times faster. Pre-orders for the new model open on October 28, and it goes on sale on November 6.

新款iPad mini搭载采用2纳米工艺的A20 Pro芯片，7核GPU每个核心内置神经加速器，苹果称AI性能最高提升4.5倍、图形性能提升2.7倍。新机10月28日开启预售，11月6日上市。

iOS 27.2 Adds Apple Pencil Support to iPhone Duo

iOS 27.2为iPhone Duo增加Apple Pencil支持

10月27日 02:51重要可靠性 B

据The Verge 10月26日报道，苹果发布iOS 27.2，为iPhone Duo折叠屏手机增加Apple Pencil支持，并新增“Edge Light”功能，在视频通话时为面部补光。苹果此前称该手写笔支持将在今年晚些时候推出。

iOS 27.2还允许Siri AI在信息应用中执行建议操作，并新增法语、日语、韩语、葡萄牙语和西班牙语支持；照片和Image Playground加入谷歌SynthID水印支持。FaceTime群聊新增双摄像头同时拍摄功能。

According to a report by The Verge on October 26, Apple released iOS 27.2, adding Apple Pencil support to the iPhone Duo foldable phone and introducing a new "Edge Light" feature that lights up the face during video calls. Apple previously said that support for the stylus would arrive later this year.

据The Verge 10月26日报道，苹果发布iOS 27.2，为iPhone Duo折叠屏手机增加Apple Pencil支持，并新增“Edge Light”功能，在视频通话时为面部补光。苹果此前称该手写笔支持将在今年晚些时候推出。

iOS 27.2 also allows Siri AI to perform suggested actions in the Messages app and adds support for French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese and Spanish; Photos and Image Playground gain support for Google's SynthID watermarking. FaceTime group chats add the ability to capture with both cameras simultaneously.

iOS 27.2还允许Siri AI在信息应用中执行建议操作，并新增法语、日语、韩语、葡萄牙语和西班牙语支持；照片和Image Playground加入谷歌SynthID水印支持。FaceTime群聊新增双摄像头同时拍摄功能。

Apple Siri AI Adds Support for Five More Languages Including French and Japanese

苹果Siri AI新增法语、日语等五种语言支持

10月27日 02:37重要可靠性 A

苹果10月26日宣布，随iOS 27.2、iPadOS 27.2、macOS 27.2、watchOS 27.2和visionOS 27.2发布，Siri AI新增支持法语、日语、韩语、葡萄牙语和西班牙语。苹果称繁体中文、挪威语、土耳其语和越南语支持将在年底前推出。

Siri AI目前为测试版，需要支持Apple Intelligence的设备并设置为受支持语言。苹果称，Siri AI目前在欧盟的iOS、iPadOS和watchOS上不可用，公司正与欧盟沟通寻求解决方案。部分依赖服务器模型的功能受每日使用限制，未来扩展访问将收费。

On October 26, Apple announced that with the release of iOS 27.2, iPadOS 27.2, macOS 27.2, watchOS 27.2 and visionOS 27.2, Siri AI now supports French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese and Spanish. Apple said support for Traditional Chinese, Norwegian, Turkish and Vietnamese will roll out before the end of the year.

苹果10月26日宣布，随iOS 27.2、iPadOS 27.2、macOS 27.2、watchOS 27.2和visionOS 27.2发布，Siri AI新增支持法语、日语、韩语、葡萄牙语和西班牙语。苹果称繁体中文、挪威语、土耳其语和越南语支持将在年底前推出。

Siri AI is currently in beta and requires a device that supports Apple Intelligence set to a supported language. Apple said Siri AI is currently unavailable on iOS, iPadOS and watchOS in the EU, and the company is in talks with the EU to seek a solution. Some features that rely on server models are subject to daily usage limits, and expanded access will be charged in the future.

Siri AI目前为测试版，需要支持Apple Intelligence的设备并设置为受支持语言。苹果称，Siri AI目前在欧盟的iOS、iPadOS和watchOS上不可用，公司正与欧盟沟通寻求解决方案。部分依赖服务器模型的功能受每日使用限制，未来扩展访问将收费。
