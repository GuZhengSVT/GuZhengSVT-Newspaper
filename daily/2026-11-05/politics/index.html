Top procuratorate announces public prosecution of former Politburo member Ma Xingrui in bribery case

最高检通报马兴瑞涉嫌受贿案已提起公诉

11月05日 11:40重大可靠性 B进展

中新社报道，最高人民检察院11月5日通报，中共中央政治局原委员马兴瑞涉嫌受贿案已由国家监察委员会调查终结，移送检察机关审查起诉；最高检以涉嫌受贿罪对其作出逮捕决定，并指定湖北省宜昌市人民检察院审查起诉。

宜昌市人民检察院近日已向宜昌市中级人民法院提起公诉。起诉指控马兴瑞利用在航天科技集团、广东省委、深圳市委、广东省政府及中央政治局等任职便利，为他人谋利，非法收受财物数额特别巨大。

China News Service reported that the Supreme People's Procuratorate said on November 5 that the investigation into the suspected bribery case against Ma Xingrui, a former member of the Political Bureau of the Communist Party of China Central Committee, had been concluded by the National Commission of Supervision and transferred to procuratorial organs for examination and prosecution; the Supreme People's Procuratorate decided to arrest him on suspicion of accepting bribes and designated the Yichang Municipal People's Procuratorate in Hubei Province to examine and prosecute the case.

中新社报道，最高人民检察院11月5日通报，中共中央政治局原委员马兴瑞涉嫌受贿案已由国家监察委员会调查终结，移送检察机关审查起诉；最高检以涉嫌受贿罪对其作出逮捕决定，并指定湖北省宜昌市人民检察院审查起诉。

The Yichang Municipal People's Procuratorate recently filed a public prosecution with the Yichang Intermediate People's Court. The prosecution alleges that Ma took advantage of his positions at China Aerospace Science and Technology Corporation, in the CPC Guangdong Provincial Committee, the CPC Shenzhen Municipal Committee and the Guangdong Provincial Government, and in the Political Bureau, among others, to seek benefits for others and illegally accepted an especially huge amount of property.

Joshua Wong Sentenced to Ten Years in National Security Law Case

黄之锋因国安法案件被判十年监禁

11月05日 11:08重大可靠性 B进展

BBC报道，香港活动人士黄之锋因国安法下“勾结外国势力”罪被判十年监禁。判决于11月5日作出；他此前因颠覆罪服刑，原定明年1月获释，新刑期中2个月2天与现有刑期同期执行。

控方称，黄之锋2020年7月至11月与罗冠聪等人串谋，游说外国对北京实施制裁和封锁；他已于9月认罪。

The BBC reported that Hong Kong activist Joshua Wong was sentenced to ten years in prison on a charge of "colluding with foreign forces" under the national security law. The sentence was handed down on November 5; he had been serving a term for subversion and had been due for release in January next year, with two months and two days of the new term to run concurrently with his existing sentence.

BBC报道，香港活动人士黄之锋因国安法下“勾结外国势力”罪被判十年监禁。判决于11月5日作出；他此前因颠覆罪服刑，原定明年1月获释，新刑期中2个月2天与现有刑期同期执行。

Prosecutors said Wong conspired with Nathan Law and others between July and November 2020 to lobby foreign countries to impose sanctions and a blockade on Beijing; he pleaded guilty in September.

控方称，黄之锋2020年7月至11月与罗冠聪等人串谋，游说外国对北京实施制裁和封锁；他已于9月认罪。

Brazil Senate Removes Bill to Lower Criminal Responsibility Age From Agenda

巴西参议院将降低刑事责任年龄提案移出议程

11月05日 10:54重要可靠性 B进展

巴西通讯社报道，巴西参议院议长阿尔科伦布雷11月4日将一项把刑事责任年龄从18岁降至16岁的宪法修正案提案移出当天议程，称应众议院议长莫塔请求，将等待众议院在特别委员会讨论，并承诺11月内表决。此前参议院3日通过公共安全宪法修正案，未包含降低刑责年龄内容。

Brazil's Senate President Davi Alcolumbre removed from that day's agenda on November 4 a proposed constitutional amendment to lower the age of criminal responsibility from 18 to 16, saying at the request of Chamber of Deputies Speaker Arthur Lira that it would wait for the Chamber to discuss it in a special committee and pledging a vote within November, Agência Brasil reported. The Senate had previously passed a public security constitutional amendment on November 3 that did not include provisions to lower the age of criminal responsibility.
