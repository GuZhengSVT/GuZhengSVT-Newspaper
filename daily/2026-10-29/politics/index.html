Israel coalition talks near after election, with two main blocs expected to fall short of majority

以色列选举后组阁谈判临近，两大阵营预计均未获多数

10月29日 03:58重大可靠性 B进展

According to The Guardian, as coalition talks approach following Israel's parliamentary election, the camps led by Prime Minister Netanyahu and former military chief of staff Gadi Eisenkot are both expected to fail to secure a parliamentary majority.
