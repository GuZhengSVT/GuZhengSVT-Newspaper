NVIDIA Releases Open-Weight Speech Model Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe, Combining Transcription, Speaker Diarization and Translation

NVIDIA开放语音模型Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe，整合转写、说话人分离与翻译

10月28日 22:58重要可靠性 A进展

NVIDIA在Hugging Face发布开放权重语音模型Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe，整合转写、说话人分离、翻译和音频问答，支持40种语言转写及31种语言与英语互译。

NVIDIA称该模型在Hugging Face开放ASR英文榜以5.33%平均词错误率居开放权重模型首位，在开放权重模型中AA-WER指数以2.8%并列第一。

同步开放Granary v2数据集与整理管线及NeMo Speech微调工具；NVIDIA称医疗场景微调使留出说话人词错误率较最佳提示降低45.8%，量化版未发布。

NVIDIA released the open-weight speech model Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe on Hugging Face, integrating transcription, speaker diarization, translation and audio question answering, with support for transcription in 40 languages and translation between 31 languages and English.

NVIDIA在Hugging Face发布开放权重语音模型Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe，整合转写、说话人分离、翻译和音频问答，支持40种语言转写及31种语言与英语互译。

NVIDIA says the model ranks first among open-weight models on the Hugging Face Open ASR English leaderboard with an average word error rate of 5.33%, and is tied for first among open-weight models with an AA-WER index of 2.8%.

NVIDIA称该模型在Hugging Face开放ASR英文榜以5.33%平均词错误率居开放权重模型首位，在开放权重模型中AA-WER指数以2.8%并列第一。

It also released the Granary v2 dataset and curation pipeline and the NeMo Speech fine-tuning tools; NVIDIA says fine-tuning for medical scenarios reduced the word error rate on held-out speakers by 45.8% compared with the best prompt, and a quantized version has not been released.

同步开放Granary v2数据集与整理管线及NeMo Speech微调工具；NVIDIA称医疗场景微调使留出说话人词错误率较最佳提示降低45.8%，量化版未发布。

来源：huggingface.co

NASA says Artemis III SLS core stage and boosters fully mated

NASA称阿耳忒弥斯三号SLS核心级与助推器完成对接

10月29日 02:59重要可靠性 A进展

NASA 10月28日发布消息，阿耳忒弥斯三号（Artemis III）任务的 SLS 核心级已在肯尼迪航天中心与助推器完成对接。这是该任务硬件总装的一项进展。

NASA announced on October 28 that the SLS core stage for the Artemis III mission has been mated with its boosters at Kennedy Space Center. This marks a step forward in the mission's hardware integration.

NASA 10月28日发布消息，阿耳忒弥斯三号（Artemis III）任务的 SLS 核心级已在肯尼迪航天中心与助推器完成对接。这是该任务硬件总装的一项进展。

Apple launches first touch-enabled MacBook Pro with tandem OLED PROLED XDR display

苹果发布首款触控MacBook Pro，采用串联OLED的PROLED XDR屏

10月29日 01:09重大可靠性 A进展

苹果10月28日发布新款 MacBook Pro，提供14英寸和16英寸机型，采用串联OLED面板，命名为 PROLED XDR：峰值亮度2000尼特（HDR与SDR）、对比度1000万:1、120Hz自适应刷新率，色域同时覆盖P3与Adobe RGB。

新机首次为 Mac 引入触控操作，灵动岛也出现在 Mac 上；搭载 M5 Pro 与 M5 Max，最高128GB统一内存和16TB存储。苹果称机身更薄更轻，提供3磅与4磅两种重量，续航最长24小时。

苹果称新机10月28日起接受预订，11月6日开始发售。Engadget 报道指出，其起售价较前代高400美元，新屏幕对以内容创作为主要收入的用户更有吸引力。

On October 28, Apple announced the new MacBook Pro, available in 14-inch and 16-inch models, featuring tandem OLED panels named PROLED XDR: peak brightness of 2,000 nits (HDR and SDR), a 10,000,000:1 contrast ratio, a 120Hz adaptive refresh rate, and color gamut coverage of both P3 and Adobe RGB.

苹果10月28日发布新款 MacBook Pro，提供14英寸和16英寸机型，采用串联OLED面板，命名为 PROLED XDR：峰值亮度2000尼特（HDR与SDR）、对比度1000万:1、120Hz自适应刷新率，色域同时覆盖P3与Adobe RGB。

The new machines bring touch input to the Mac for the first time, and the Dynamic Island also comes to the Mac; they are powered by M5 Pro and M5 Max, with up to 128GB of unified memory and 16TB of storage. Apple says the chassis is thinner and lighter, comes in two weights of 3 pounds and 4 pounds, and offers up to 24 hours of battery life.

新机首次为 Mac 引入触控操作，灵动岛也出现在 Mac 上；搭载 M5 Pro 与 M5 Max，最高128GB统一内存和16TB存储。苹果称机身更薄更轻，提供3磅与4磅两种重量，续航最长24小时。

Apple says the new models will be available for pre-order starting October 28 and will go on sale starting November 6. Engadget reported that its starting price is $400 higher than the previous generation and that the new display is more appealing to users whose main source of income is content creation.

苹果称新机10月28日起接受预订，11月6日开始发售。Engadget 报道指出，其起售价较前代高400美元，新屏幕对以内容创作为主要收入的用户更有吸引力。

来源：apple.com · engadget.com
