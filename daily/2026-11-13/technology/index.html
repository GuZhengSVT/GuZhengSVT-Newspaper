IBM releases Granite 4.1 LLMON preview models, using a native markup language to separate instructions from data

IBM发布Granite 4.1 LLMON预览模型，用原生标记语言隔离指令与数据

11月13日 03:07重要可靠性 A

IBM Granite团队在Hugging Face发文，发布两个实验性预览模型granite-4.1-3b-LLMON-preview和granite-4.1-8b-LLMON-preview，基于Granite 4.1基座，训练目标是读取和生成LLMON标记文本；团队同时发布GitHub工具包。

LLMON全称LLM Object Notation，是一种面向大模型的原生标记语言。它用保留的特殊token作为结构分隔符，给指令、数据和约束打标签，并用唯一ID把指令与作用数据绑定。团队称，这样模型不必像处理JSON那样从普通字符推断嵌套和转义；JSON面向解析器设计，不是为因果语言模型设计。

IBM称，在部分基准上，隐藏在用户数据中的间接提示注入成功率降至接近零，而常规指令微调模型有较高概率被劫持；同时相对Granite 4.1 Instruct保持通用能力。

The IBM Granite team published a post on Hugging Face releasing two experimental preview models, granite-4.1-3b-LLMON-preview and granite-4.1-8b-LLMON-preview, built on the Granite 4.1 base, with the training goal of reading and generating LLMON-marked text; the team also released a GitHub toolkit.

IBM Granite团队在Hugging Face发文，发布两个实验性预览模型granite-4.1-3b-LLMON-preview和granite-4.1-8b-LLMON-preview，基于Granite 4.1基座，训练目标是读取和生成LLMON标记文本；团队同时发布GitHub工具包。

LLMON stands for LLM Object Notation, a native markup language for large models. It uses reserved special tokens as structural delimiters, labels instructions, data, and constraints, and binds instructions to the data they act on using unique IDs. The team says this way the model does not have to infer nesting and escaping from ordinary characters as it does when processing JSON; JSON is designed for parsers, not for causal language models.

LLMON全称LLM Object Notation，是一种面向大模型的原生标记语言。它用保留的特殊token作为结构分隔符，给指令、数据和约束打标签，并用唯一ID把指令与作用数据绑定。团队称，这样模型不必像处理JSON那样从普通字符推断嵌套和转义；JSON面向解析器设计，不是为因果语言模型设计。

IBM says that on some benchmarks, the success rate of indirect prompt injection hidden in user data dropped to near zero, whereas conventionally instruction-tuned models have a high probability of being hijacked, and that the models maintain general capabilities relative to Granite 4.1 Instruct.

IBM称，在部分基准上，隐藏在用户数据中的间接提示注入成功率降至接近零，而常规指令微调模型有较高概率被劫持；同时相对Granite 4.1 Instruct保持通用能力。

UARX Space Says OSSIE-D Orbital Transfer Vehicle Completed First Flight Deployment

UARX Space称OSSIE-D轨道转移飞行器完成首飞部署

11月13日 04:00重要可靠性 B

据UARX Space发布的新闻稿，西班牙公司UARX Space的轨道转移飞行器OSSIE-D已由SpaceX Falcon 9发射，并使用该公司SAU&RON 24分离系统完成部署。公司称OSSIE-D完全在西班牙设计制造，75%组件由公司内部开发。

DHV Technology提供的完整电源子系统在入轨后工作正常，包括太阳能阵列、PCDU和电池的初始遥测符合预期。UARX称，这是首次由同一家公司同时提供轨道转移飞行器和分离系统的任务。新闻稿没有给出具体发射日期。

According to a press release issued by UARX Space, the Spanish company's OSSIE-D orbital transfer vehicle has been launched by a SpaceX Falcon 9 and deployed using the company's SAU&RON 24 separation system. The company says OSSIE-D was fully designed and manufactured in Spain, with 75% of its components developed in-house.

据UARX Space发布的新闻稿，西班牙公司UARX Space的轨道转移飞行器OSSIE-D已由SpaceX Falcon 9发射，并使用该公司SAU&RON 24分离系统完成部署。公司称OSSIE-D完全在西班牙设计制造，75%组件由公司内部开发。

The complete power subsystem provided by DHV Technology is functioning properly after orbital insertion, with initial telemetry for the solar arrays, PCDU and batteries meeting expectations. UARX says this is the first mission in which a single company provided both the orbital transfer vehicle and the separation system. The press release did not provide a specific launch date.

DHV Technology提供的完整电源子系统在入轨后工作正常，包括太阳能阵列、PCDU和电池的初始遥测符合预期。UARX称，这是首次由同一家公司同时提供轨道转移飞行器和分离系统的任务。新闻稿没有给出具体发射日期。

Kioxia and Dell Build 157PB Single-Rack All-Flash Configuration

Kioxia与Dell构建157PB单机架全闪存配置

11月13日 09:15重要可靠性 B

TechPowerUp报道，Kioxia与Dell在单个42U机架中构建了157PB原始闪存容量的配置，使用16台Dell PowerEdge R7725xd服务器、640块Kioxia 245.76TB LC9系列SSD，运行Dell ObjectScale对象存储平台。每台2U服务器装40块E3.L规格硬盘，16台占32U，剩余10U。

LC9为PCIe 5.0 NVMe硬盘，基于Kioxia第八代BiCS QLC和32 die堆叠。Kioxia称这是业界首个157PB单机架对象存储，依据是自家市场调查；报道指出，公告没有给出价格或可用时间。

TechPowerUp reports that Kioxia and Dell built a configuration with 157PB of raw flash capacity in a single 42U rack, using 16 Dell PowerEdge R7725xd servers and 640 Kioxia 245.76TB LC9 series SSDs, running the Dell ObjectScale object storage platform. Each 2U server holds 40 E3.L form-factor drives; the 16 servers occupy 32U, leaving 10U.

TechPowerUp报道，Kioxia与Dell在单个42U机架中构建了157PB原始闪存容量的配置，使用16台Dell PowerEdge R7725xd服务器、640块Kioxia 245.76TB LC9系列SSD，运行Dell ObjectScale对象存储平台。每台2U服务器装40块E3.L规格硬盘，16台占32U，剩余10U。

The LC9 is a PCIe 5.0 NVMe drive based on Kioxia's eighth-generation BiCS QLC and 32-die stacking. Kioxia says this is the industry's first 157PB single-rack object storage, based on its own market survey; the report notes that the announcement did not provide pricing or availability.

LC9为PCIe 5.0 NVMe硬盘，基于Kioxia第八代BiCS QLC和32 die堆叠。Kioxia称这是业界首个157PB单机架对象存储，依据是自家市场调查；报道指出，公告没有给出价格或可用时间。

Samsung Smart Glasses to Go on Sale in the U.S. and South Korea on December 10, With Built-in Gemini

三星智能眼镜将于12月10日在美韩开售，内置Gemini

11月12日 22:00重要可靠性 A进展

三星11月12日宣布，其智能眼镜全系将于12月10日在美国和韩国发售，预售从当天开始。产品与Gentle Monster和Warby Parker合作，共30款设计；三星称整机约41克，内置Google Gemini，提供免手操作协助，可帮助用户理解周围环境并进行跨语言交流。

硬件包括1200万像素HDR相机、开放式扬声器和四麦克风阵列，搭载高通Snapdragon AR1 Gen 1平台，单次充电续航最长9小时，搭配充电盒最长72小时。产品计划12月10日发售，尚未上市。

Samsung announced on November 12 that its entire smart glasses lineup will go on sale in the United States and South Korea on December 10, with pre-orders beginning that day. The product was developed in collaboration with Gentle Monster and Warby Parker and offers 30 designs in total; Samsung says the glasses weigh about 41 grams, feature built-in Google Gemini, provide hands-free assistance, and can help users understand their surroundings and communicate across languages.

三星11月12日宣布，其智能眼镜全系将于12月10日在美国和韩国发售，预售从当天开始。产品与Gentle Monster和Warby Parker合作，共30款设计；三星称整机约41克，内置Google Gemini，提供免手操作协助，可帮助用户理解周围环境并进行跨语言交流。

The hardware includes a 12-megapixel HDR camera, open-ear speakers, and a four-microphone array, is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon AR1 Gen 1 platform, and offers up to 9 hours of battery life on a single charge and up to 72 hours with the charging case. The product is planned for release on December 10 and has not yet gone on sale.

硬件包括1200万像素HDR相机、开放式扬声器和四麦克风阵列，搭载高通Snapdragon AR1 Gen 1平台，单次充电续航最长9小时，搭配充电盒最长72小时。产品计划12月10日发售，尚未上市。
