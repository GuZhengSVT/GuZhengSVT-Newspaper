OpenAI, Google and Others Back Shared robots.txt Opt-Out Signal

OpenAI、Google等支持共享robots.txt退出信号

10月31日 04:26重要可靠性 B进展

据The Verge报道，OpenAI、Google、Microsoft和Anthropic表示将采用一项新的共享信号，让发布者可以在robots.txt中一次性表明是否允许AI抓取，而不必逐家设置。该信号由Anthropic标准技术负责人Kevin Kelley在IETF提出，被称为“policy token”。

Meta传播负责人Andy Stone表示支持这一共同信号，但没有说明Meta是否会遵守。报道称，发布者目前仍需向每家AI公司分别表达训练偏好，新方案试图把这一选择集中到网站已有的robots.txt中。

According to The Verge, OpenAI, Google, Microsoft and Anthropic said they would adopt a new shared signal that lets publishers indicate in robots.txt, in one place, whether they allow AI crawling, rather than setting it separately for each company. The signal was proposed at the IETF by Kevin Kelley, Anthropic's standards technical lead, and is called a "policy token."

据The Verge报道，OpenAI、Google、Microsoft和Anthropic表示将采用一项新的共享信号，让发布者可以在robots.txt中一次性表明是否允许AI抓取，而不必逐家设置。该信号由Anthropic标准技术负责人Kevin Kelley在IETF提出，被称为“policy token”。

Andy Stone, Meta's communications lead, said he supports this common signal but did not say whether Meta would comply. The report said publishers currently still need to express training preferences to each AI company separately, and the new proposal seeks to consolidate this choice into the robots.txt file already on websites.

Meta传播负责人Andy Stone表示支持这一共同信号，但没有说明Meta是否会遵守。报道称，发布者目前仍需向每家AI公司分别表达训练偏好，新方案试图把这一选择集中到网站已有的robots.txt中。

NVIDIA and MIT Team Open-Source Video Enhancement Solution Based on MiniMax H3

NVIDIA与MIT团队开源基于MiniMax H3的视频增强方案

10月30日 15:31重要可靠性 B

据量子位报道，由NVIDIA与MIT研究人员组成的Efficient-Large-Model团队在Hugging Face发布了视频增强项目Open-H3-Regenerate-2K的模型权重，配套推理代码已在NVlabs/Sana仓库开放。该方案基于MiniMax H3，把低分辨率视频草稿重生成到2560×1440，最高支持3840×2160。

项目团队公布的测试显示，在单张NVIDIA B200 GPU、预热条件下，约5秒视频的潜空间放大、三步细化和2K解码合计耗时从87.26秒降至22.45秒，约3.9倍；在RefinerBench-150上，该方案UniPercept平均分60.0，官方H3-Regenerate-2K API为53.4。上述数据来自团队公布。

According to QbitAI, the Efficient-Large-Model team, composed of researchers from NVIDIA and MIT, released on Hugging Face the model weights for the video enhancement project Open-H3-Regenerate-2K, with accompanying inference code already available in the NVlabs/Sana repository. The solution is based on MiniMax H3 and regenerates low-resolution video drafts to 2560×1440, with support for up to 3840×2160.

据量子位报道，由NVIDIA与MIT研究人员组成的Efficient-Large-Model团队在Hugging Face发布了视频增强项目Open-H3-Regenerate-2K的模型权重，配套推理代码已在NVlabs/Sana仓库开放。该方案基于MiniMax H3，把低分辨率视频草稿重生成到2560×1440，最高支持3840×2160。

Tests published by the project team show that, on a single NVIDIA B200 GPU under warm-up conditions, the combined time for latent-space upscaling, three-step refinement, and 2K decoding of an approximately 5-second video fell from 87.26 seconds to 22.45 seconds, about 3.9×; on RefinerBench-150, the solution's UniPercept average score was 60.0, compared with 53.4 for the official H3-Regenerate-2K API. The above figures were published by the team.

项目团队公布的测试显示，在单张NVIDIA B200 GPU、预热条件下，约5秒视频的潜空间放大、三步细化和2K解码合计耗时从87.26秒降至22.45秒，约3.9倍；在RefinerBench-150上，该方案UniPercept平均分60.0，官方H3-Regenerate-2K API为53.4。上述数据来自团队公布。

SoMark Team Open-Sources OXR, a 1.3B Document Parsing Model, Claiming Top Rankings on Three Benchmarks

SoMark团队开源1.3B文档解析模型OXR，称三项基准登顶

10月30日 13:52重要可靠性 B

据量子位报道，SoMark团队开源了1.3B参数的文档解析模型OXR，称其在OmniDocBench、olmOCR-Bench和DocParsingBench三项基准上排名第一，对应分数分别为97.18、87.84和87.76。模型代码、权重和评测复现代码已公开。

OXR可一次解析表格、公式、代码块、图片、化学方程式等13类文档元素，报道称单张RTX PRO 6000解析100页文档耗时101秒。团队采用递归自我改进后训练，让模型自行找错、归因并生成下一轮训练数据。

According to QbitAI, the SoMark team has open-sourced OXR, a 1.3B-parameter document parsing model, which it says ranks first on three benchmarks—OmniDocBench, olmOCR-Bench, and DocParsingBench—with scores of 97.18, 87.84, and 87.76, respectively. The model's code, weights, and evaluation reproduction code have been made public.

据量子位报道，SoMark团队开源了1.3B参数的文档解析模型OXR，称其在OmniDocBench、olmOCR-Bench和DocParsingBench三项基准上排名第一，对应分数分别为97.18、87.84和87.76。模型代码、权重和评测复现代码已公开。

OXR can parse 13 categories of document elements—including tables, formulas, code blocks, images, and chemical equations—in a single pass, and the report says parsing a 100-page document takes 101 seconds on a single RTX PRO 6000. The team used recursive self-improvement post-training, allowing the model to find its own errors, identify their causes, and generate training data for the next round.

OXR可一次解析表格、公式、代码块、图片、化学方程式等13类文档元素，报道称单张RTX PRO 6000解析100页文档耗时101秒。团队采用递归自我改进后训练，让模型自行找错、归因并生成下一轮训练数据。

Google Says Suncatcher Satellite TPU Starts Up in Orbit; Gemma 4 Generates First Response

Google称Suncatcher卫星TPU在轨启动，Gemma 4生成首条回应

10月31日 02:21重大可靠性 B进展

据The Verge报道，Google称其搭载AI芯片的卫星已成功启动Tensor Processing Unit（TPU），使星载Gemma 4模型能够执行提示，生成一首关于身处太空的俳句并传回地球。Google还测试了其他太空主题的短诗和遥测提示。

这是Google实验项目Project Suncatcher的一部分，该项目设想最终把AI数据中心送入太空。Google表示，接下来将评估TPU在轨道热应力和辐射暴露下的表现，并称验证TPU能在太空可靠执行工作负载，是2027年双星互联目标的前提。

According to The Verge, Google said that its satellite carrying AI chips had successfully started up a Tensor Processing Unit (TPU), enabling the onboard Gemma 4 model to execute prompts, generate a haiku about being in space, and send it back to Earth. Google also tested other space-themed short poems and telemetry prompts.

据The Verge报道，Google称其搭载AI芯片的卫星已成功启动Tensor Processing Unit（TPU），使星载Gemma 4模型能够执行提示，生成一首关于身处太空的俳句并传回地球。Google还测试了其他太空主题的短诗和遥测提示。

This is part of Google's experimental Project Suncatcher, which envisions eventually sending AI data centers into space. Google said it would next assess how the TPU performs under orbital thermal stress and radiation exposure, and said that verifying the TPU can reliably execute workloads in space is a prerequisite for the 2027 goal of interconnecting two satellites.

这是Google实验项目Project Suncatcher的一部分，该项目设想最终把AI数据中心送入太空。Google表示，接下来将评估TPU在轨道热应力和辐射暴露下的表现，并称验证TPU能在太空可靠执行工作负载，是2027年双星互联目标的前提。

AUO Board Approves NT$38.8 Billion Sale of Taichung Phase 2 Plant to TSMC

友达董事会批准以388亿新台币向台积电出售台中二期厂

10月30日 16:30重要可靠性 B进展

据TrendForce报道，友达（AUO）董事会批准将台中二期厂及相关基础设施出售给台积电，交易金额388亿新台币（约12亿美元），涵盖建筑、洁净室和配套设备，登记楼面面积约42.9万平方米。友达预计认列约185亿新台币处置利益。

友达原计划将华亚厂出售给广达，但因双方未就工厂规格和交付时程达成一致而取消。台积电正在台中扩建，计划投资约1.5万亿新台币建设四座1.4nm厂，预计2027年起分期完成；TrendForce引述产业消息称，取得现有厂房可能有助于其在台中整合先进制造与封装。

According to TrendForce, AUO's board approved the sale of its Taichung Phase 2 plant and related infrastructure to TSMC for NT$38.8 billion (approximately US$1.2 billion), covering buildings, cleanrooms and ancillary equipment, with a registered floor area of about 429,000 square meters. AUO expects to recognize a disposal gain of about NT$18.5 billion.

据TrendForce报道，友达（AUO）董事会批准将台中二期厂及相关基础设施出售给台积电，交易金额388亿新台币（约12亿美元），涵盖建筑、洁净室和配套设备，登记楼面面积约42.9万平方米。友达预计认列约185亿新台币处置利益。

AUO originally planned to sell its Hwa Ya plant to Quanta Computer, but the deal was canceled because the two sides failed to agree on plant specifications and the delivery schedule. TSMC is expanding in Taichung and plans to invest about NT$1.5 trillion to build four 1.4nm fabs, which are expected to be completed in phases starting in 2027; TrendForce, citing industry sources, said acquiring the existing plant may help TSMC integrate advanced manufacturing and packaging in Taichung.

友达原计划将华亚厂出售给广达，但因双方未就工厂规格和交付时程达成一致而取消。台积电正在台中扩建，计划投资约1.5万亿新台币建设四座1.4nm厂，预计2027年起分期完成；TrendForce引述产业消息称，取得现有厂房可能有助于其在台中整合先进制造与封装。
