Large Free WORLD OF HORROR Update Now Live on PC

WORLD OF HORROR大型免费更新已在PC上线

10月30日 22:12重要可靠性 B

发行商Ysbryd Games与开发者Panstasz为《WORLD OF HORROR》推出大型免费更新“AS BELOW SO ABOVE”，PC版现已上线，PS4和Switch版计划2027年推出。

据Gematsu报道，这次更新为游戏增加约20%内容，加入新事件、敌人、谜团、角色、物品、法术、诅咒和伤病，并改善体验、修复问题。更新新增两个古神显现、三名新可玩角色和Scenario Mode。

Publisher Ysbryd Games and developer Panstasz have released the large free update “AS BELOW SO ABOVE” for WORLD OF HORROR; the PC version is out now, with PS4 and Switch versions planned for release in 2027.

发行商Ysbryd Games与开发者Panstasz为《WORLD OF HORROR》推出大型免费更新“AS BELOW SO ABOVE”，PC版现已上线，PS4和Switch版计划2027年推出。

According to Gematsu, the update adds about 20% more content to the game, including new events, enemies, mysteries, characters, items, spells, curses, and injuries and ailments, and improves the experience and fixes issues. The update adds two Old God manifestations, three new playable characters, and Scenario Mode.

据Gematsu报道，这次更新为游戏增加约20%内容，加入新事件、敌人、谜团、角色、物品、法术、诅咒和伤病，并改善体验、修复问题。更新新增两个古神显现、三名新可玩角色和Scenario Mode。

Avatar Legends: The Fighting Game Launches Major “Avatar State” Update

Avatar Legends格斗游戏推出Avatar State大型更新

10月31日 02:00重要可靠性 A

微软Xbox Wire宣布，《Avatar Legends: The Fighting Game》的“Avatar State”大型更新已于10月30日上线Xbox Series X|S。更新重做安昂与Nightmare Korra，重新平衡全部角色，并加入Ranked Mode 2.0、故事模式+、新教程和连招挑战。

官方称这是游戏迄今最大更新，菜单与对战界面也重新设计。标准版售价29.99美元，含Year 1 Pass的豪华版49.99美元；官方还开放新角色投票，候选包括Tenzin、Kuvira、Amon、King Bumi和Asami Sato。

Microsoft's Xbox Wire announced that the major “Avatar State” update for Avatar Legends: The Fighting Game launched on Xbox Series X|S on October 30. The update reworks Aang and Nightmare Korra, rebalances all characters, and adds Ranked Mode 2.0, Story Mode+, a new tutorial, and combo challenges.

微软Xbox Wire宣布，《Avatar Legends: The Fighting Game》的“Avatar State”大型更新已于10月30日上线Xbox Series X|S。更新重做安昂与Nightmare Korra，重新平衡全部角色，并加入Ranked Mode 2.0、故事模式+、新教程和连招挑战。

The official announcement called it the game's largest update to date, and the menus and battle interface have also been redesigned. The Standard Edition costs $29.99 and the Deluxe Edition, which includes the Year 1 Pass, costs $49.99; officials also opened voting for a new character, with candidates including Tenzin, Kuvira, Amon, King Bumi and Asami Sato.

官方称这是游戏迄今最大更新，菜单与对战界面也重新设计。标准版售价29.99美元，含Year 1 Pass的豪华版49.99美元；官方还开放新角色投票，候选包括Tenzin、Kuvira、Amon、King Bumi和Asami Sato。
