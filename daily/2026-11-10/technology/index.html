Tencent Hunyuan Releases Hy4 With 770B Parameters and Computer-Operation Support, Plans to Open-Source Weights Soon

腾讯混元发布Hy4：770B参数、支持电脑操作，权重计划近期开源

11月09日 16:44重要可靠性 B进展

据量子位11月9日报道，腾讯混元Hy4正式发布，总参数770B、激活参数49B，首次引入原生视觉理解并支持电脑操作。

该报道称，Hy4已在腾讯云TokenHub、WorkBuddy/CodeBuddy、元宝、企业微信智能助理等产品上线，API接入OpenRouter、Hermes、OpenCode等平台；模型权重将于近期开源。

上述参数与体验来自量子位的一手实测报道，权重尚未开源。

According to a November 9 report by QbitAI, Tencent Hunyuan Hy4 was officially released with 770B total parameters and 49B activated parameters, introducing native visual understanding for the first time and supporting computer operation.

据量子位11月9日报道，腾讯混元Hy4正式发布，总参数770B、激活参数49B，首次引入原生视觉理解并支持电脑操作。

The report said that Hy4 is already available in products including Tencent Cloud TokenHub, WorkBuddy/CodeBuddy, Yuanbao, and the WeCom intelligent assistant, and that its API is available through platforms such as OpenRouter, Hermes, and OpenCode; the model weights will be open-sourced in the near future.

该报道称，Hy4已在腾讯云TokenHub、WorkBuddy/CodeBuddy、元宝、企业微信智能助理等产品上线，API接入OpenRouter、Hermes、OpenCode等平台；模型权重将于近期开源。

The above parameters and hands-on experience come from QbitAI's first-hand test report, and the weights have not yet been open-sourced.

上述参数与体验来自量子位的一手实测报道，权重尚未开源。

DeepX Tapes Out Second-Generation AI Accelerator DX-M2; Simulation Shows 6.7x Throughput Gain

DeepX流片第二代AI加速器DX-M2，模拟显示6.7倍吞吐提升

11月09日 14:47重要可靠性 B进展

韩国边缘AI芯片公司DeepX已流片第二代AI加速器DX-M2，采用三星LPDDR5x-PIM。公司称该设计可在5W以下推理最高1000亿参数模型。

DeepX的模拟显示，把GEMV运算卸载到LPDDR5x-PIM后，Llama3.1-8B的token吞吐可达单独DX-M2的6.7倍，但整体功耗约为2.7倍。

公司已收到PIM样品并在内部测试，目前处于流片后的测试阶段。

South Korean edge AI chip company DeepX has taped out its second-generation AI accelerator DX-M2, which uses Samsung LPDDR5x-PIM. The company says the design can run inference on models with up to 100 billion parameters at under 5W.

韩国边缘AI芯片公司DeepX已流片第二代AI加速器DX-M2，采用三星LPDDR5x-PIM。公司称该设计可在5W以下推理最高1000亿参数模型。

DeepX's simulation shows that after offloading GEMV operations to LPDDR5x-PIM, Llama3.1-8B token throughput can reach 6.7 times that of DX-M2 alone, while overall power consumption is about 2.7 times.

DeepX的模拟显示，把GEMV运算卸载到LPDDR5x-PIM后，Llama3.1-8B的token吞吐可达单独DX-M2的6.7倍，但整体功耗约为2.7倍。

The company has received PIM samples and is testing them internally; it is currently in the post-tapeout testing phase.

公司已收到PIM样品并在内部测试，目前处于流片后的测试阶段。

来源：eetimes.com

NVIDIA Partners with Rebellions and SK Telecom to Advance South Korea's Sovereign AI Infrastructure

NVIDIA与Rebellions、SK电讯合作，推进韩国主权AI基础设施

11月09日 13:00重要可靠性 A进展

NVIDIA新闻稿宣布，NVIDIA、Rebellions与SK电讯合作推进韩国主权AI基础设施，项目获韩国科学技术信息通信部支持。

Rebellions计划用NVLink Fusion把R200 NPU接入NVIDIA平台，并采用MGX机架架构；SK电讯计划在AI数据中心部署这些系统。

三方还计划让GPU承担prefill、NPU承担decode，以分离式推理提升吞吐和能效。上述均为计划。

An NVIDIA press release announced that NVIDIA, Rebellions, and SK Telecom are collaborating to advance South Korea's sovereign AI infrastructure, with the project supported by South Korea's Ministry of Science and ICT.

NVIDIA新闻稿宣布，NVIDIA、Rebellions与SK电讯合作推进韩国主权AI基础设施，项目获韩国科学技术信息通信部支持。

Rebellions plans to use NVLink Fusion to connect the R200 NPU to NVIDIA's platform and adopt the MGX rack architecture; SK Telecom plans to deploy these systems in AI data centers.

Rebellions计划用NVLink Fusion把R200 NPU接入NVIDIA平台，并采用MGX机架架构；SK电讯计划在AI数据中心部署这些系统。

The three parties also plan to have GPUs handle prefill and NPUs handle decode, using disaggregated inference to improve throughput and energy efficiency. All of the above are plans.

三方还计划让GPU承担prefill、NPU承担decode，以分离式推理提升吞吐和能效。上述均为计划。

来源：nvidianews.nvidia.com
