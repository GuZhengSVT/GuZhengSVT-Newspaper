Warhammer 40,000: Dark Heresy Set to Launch on January 28, 2027

《Warhammer 40,000: Dark Heresy》定于2027年1月28日发售

11月10日 00:13重要可靠性 B进展

Owlcat Games宣布，《Warhammer 40,000: Dark Heresy》将于2027年1月28日登陆PS5、Xbox Series和PC，PC版通过Steam、Epic Games Store与GOG发行，Switch 2版后续推出。

本作是队伍制、剧情驱动的cRPG。玩家从审判庭侍僧起步，组建由狂热者与弃儿组成的战团调查犯罪和叛教，可招募亚人类、危险灵能者、无魂者乃至艾达灵族海盗、食肉克鲁特等异形同伴，并在调查与战术战斗中使用其独特技能。

Founder’s Packs销售将持续到1月初，面向所有创始人的Beta测试于1月7日关闭，为正式发售做准备。

Owlcat Games announced that Warhammer 40,000: Dark Heresy will launch on January 28, 2027, for PS5, Xbox Series, and PC, with the PC version available via Steam, Epic Games Store, and GOG, and a Switch 2 version to follow.

Owlcat Games宣布，《Warhammer 40,000: Dark Heresy》将于2027年1月28日登陆PS5、Xbox Series和PC，PC版通过Steam、Epic Games Store与GOG发行，Switch 2版后续推出。

This title is a party-based, story-driven cRPG. Players begin as an Acolyte of the Inquisition and assemble a warband of zealots and outcasts to investigate crimes and apostasy; they can recruit subhumans, dangerous psykers, blanks, and even xenos companions such as Aeldari pirates and carnivorous Kroot, and use their unique skills in investigations and tactical combat.

本作是队伍制、剧情驱动的cRPG。玩家从审判庭侍僧起步，组建由狂热者与弃儿组成的战团调查犯罪和叛教，可招募亚人类、危险灵能者、无魂者乃至艾达灵族海盗、食肉克鲁特等异形同伴，并在调查与战术战斗中使用其独特技能。

Sales of Founder’s Packs will continue until early January, and the Beta test for all founders closes on January 7 in preparation for the official release.

Founder’s Packs销售将持续到1月初，面向所有创始人的Beta测试于1月7日关闭，为正式发售做准备。

FragPunk Launches Farlands Into Global Early Access on PC and Consoles

《FragPunk》推出Farlands，已在PC和主机开启全球Early Access

11月10日 00:00重要可靠性 A进展

Bad Guitar Studio宣布，《FragPunk》迄今规模最大的新章节Farlands已在PC和主机开启全球Early Access。

Farlands是永久性多人送货PvPvE冒险，数十名玩家共享一个持续演进的世界。每个服务器以五天为周期持续运行，玩家的战斗和采集会影响服务器内阵营等级，改变敌对势力的强度、数量和分布；高温、寒冷和降雨会加速生命流失并影响运货。

玩家可通过全球语音和表情系统临时结盟护送高价货物，也可能在关键时刻反目；成功运送获得的资源可用于强化个人装备和基地。

Bad Guitar Studio has announced that Farlands, the largest new chapter in FragPunk to date, has entered global Early Access on PC and consoles.

Bad Guitar Studio宣布，《FragPunk》迄今规模最大的新章节Farlands已在PC和主机开启全球Early Access。

Farlands is a persistent multiplayer delivery PvPvE adventure in which dozens of players share a continuously evolving world. Each server runs on a five-day cycle, and players' combat and gathering affect faction levels on that server, changing the strength, numbers, and distribution of hostile forces; high heat, cold, and rain accelerate health drain and affect cargo delivery.

Farlands是永久性多人送货PvPvE冒险，数十名玩家共享一个持续演进的世界。每个服务器以五天为周期持续运行，玩家的战斗和采集会影响服务器内阵营等级，改变敌对势力的强度、数量和分布；高温、寒冷和降雨会加速生命流失并影响运货。

Players can use global voice and emote systems to temporarily form alliances to escort high-value cargo and may also turn against each other at critical moments; resources gained from successful deliveries can be used to upgrade personal equipment and bases.

玩家可通过全球语音和表情系统临时结盟护送高价货物，也可能在关键时刻反目；成功运送获得的资源可用于强化个人装备和基地。

Medabots Classics Plus to Launch on Steam in December With 8 Classic Titles

《Medabots Classics Plus》12月登陆Steam，收录8款旧作

11月10日 10:32重要可靠性 B进展

Imagineer与开发商SUCCESS Corporation宣布，《Medabots Classics Plus》将于2026年12月在Steam发售，分为Kabuto Ver.和Kuwagata Ver.，日本各售5478日元。 合集收录8款系列旧作，包括《Medabots》至《Medabots 5》以及《Medabots Navi》《Medabots G》《Medabots Two: CORE》。

合集加入随时存档读档、战斗跳过和最高8倍速等功能。 卡布托版与锹形虫版之间支持在线对战和零件交换。

Imagineer and developer SUCCESS Corporation have announced that Medabots Classics Plus will be released on Steam in December 2026 in Kabuto Ver. and Kuwagata Ver., priced at 5,478 yen each in Japan. The collection includes eight past titles in the series, including Medabots through Medabots 5, as well as Medabots Navi, Medabots G, and Medabots Two: CORE.

Imagineer与开发商SUCCESS Corporation宣布，《Medabots Classics Plus》将于2026年12月在Steam发售，分为Kabuto Ver.和Kuwagata Ver.，日本各售5478日元。 合集收录8款系列旧作，包括《Medabots》至《Medabots 5》以及《Medabots Navi》《Medabots G》《Medabots Two: CORE》。

The collection adds features such as saving and loading at any time, battle skip, and up to 8x speed. Online battles and parts exchange are supported between the Kabuto Ver. and Kuwagata Ver.

合集加入随时存档读档、战斗跳过和最高8倍速等功能。 卡布托版与锹形虫版之间支持在线对战和零件交换。

Sea of Remnants' Dawnbreaker Closed Beta Opens December 3

《Sea of Remnants》Dawnbreaker封闭测试12月3日开启

11月09日 22:30重要可靠性 B进展

网易游戏与Joker Studio宣布，免费海洋冒险RPG《Sea of Remnants》的Dawnbreaker封闭测试将于12月3日开始，覆盖PS5、Xbox Series、PC（专用启动器）、iOS（TestFlight）和Android。预下载12月1日开放，测试资格有限，按先到先得发放。

测试提供21名可玩角色，开放白海和桥海两个新区域及新任务，新增两艘初始船可选，并加入高难度挑战Peril Theatre。

游戏计划2027年初正式上线PS5、Xbox Series、PC（Steam）、iOS和Android；完全共享世界的多人冒险会在上线后逐步实装。

NetEase Games and Joker Studio have announced that the Dawnbreaker closed beta test for the free-to-play ocean adventure RPG Sea of Remnants will begin on December 3 on PS5, Xbox Series, PC (via a dedicated launcher), iOS (via TestFlight), and Android. Pre-downloads will open on December 1, with a limited number of test slots allocated on a first-come, first-served basis.

网易游戏与Joker Studio宣布，免费海洋冒险RPG《Sea of Remnants》的Dawnbreaker封闭测试将于12月3日开始，覆盖PS5、Xbox Series、PC（专用启动器）、iOS（TestFlight）和Android。预下载12月1日开放，测试资格有限，按先到先得发放。

The test will offer 21 playable characters, open two new regions—the White Sea and the Bridge Sea—and new quests, add two selectable starting ships, and introduce the high-difficulty challenge Peril Theatre.

测试提供21名可玩角色，开放白海和桥海两个新区域及新任务，新增两艘初始船可选，并加入高难度挑战Peril Theatre。

The game is planned for official launch in early 2027 on PS5, Xbox Series, PC (Steam), iOS, and Android; its fully shared-world multiplayer adventure will be implemented gradually after launch.

游戏计划2027年初正式上线PS5、Xbox Series、PC（Steam）、iOS和Android；完全共享世界的多人冒险会在上线后逐步实装。

Dragon Quest RPG: Makai-ju to Michibikareshi Shima Announced for Global Simultaneous Release in 2027

《ドラゴンクエストRPG 魔界樹と導かれし島》公布，计划2027年全球同步发行

11月10日 11:28重要可靠性 B进展

GREE Holdings于11月10日公开IR信息，旗下WFS与Square Enix共同开发的手机指令RPG《ドラゴンクエストRPG 魔界樹と導かれし島》计划2027年全球同步发行（部分地区除外），登陆iOS和Android，采用基本免费加道具收费。

开发由曾制作《Heaven Burns Red》的WFS负责，Square Enix负责企划与制作；堀井雄二任总导演，鸟山明负责角色设计，すぎやまこういち负责音乐。

官方计划11月10日20:00在YouTube首播公布预告片。

GREE Holdings disclosed IR information on November 10, stating that the mobile command RPG Dragon Quest RPG: Makai-ju to Michibikareshi Shima, jointly developed by its WFS unit and Square Enix, is planned for a simultaneous global release in 2027 (excluding some regions), will launch on iOS and Android, and will adopt a free-to-play model with item purchases.

GREE Holdings于11月10日公开IR信息，旗下WFS与Square Enix共同开发的手机指令RPG《ドラゴンクエストRPG 魔界樹と導かれし島》计划2027年全球同步发行（部分地区除外），登陆iOS和Android，采用基本免费加道具收费。

Development is handled by WFS, which produced Heaven Burns Red, while Square Enix is responsible for planning and production; Yuji Horii serves as general director, Akira Toriyama is responsible for character design, and Koichi Sugiyama is responsible for music.

开发由曾制作《Heaven Burns Red》的WFS负责，Square Enix负责企划与制作；堀井雄二任总导演，鸟山明负责角色设计，すぎやまこういち负责音乐。

The official plan is to premiere the announcement trailer on YouTube at 20:00 on November 10.

官方计划11月10日20:00在YouTube首播公布预告片。
