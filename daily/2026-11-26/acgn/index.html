Azur Promilia Officially Launches: Free-to-Play with In-App Purchases, Launch Events Offer Over 40 Free Draws

《Azur Promilia》正式开服：基本免费加道具收费，开服活动可免费抽卡40次以上

11月26日 11:59重大可靠性 B单一来源报道

据4Gamer报道，Yostar与Manjuu Games共同发行的幻想RPG《Azur Promilia》（アズールプロミリア）于11月26日正式开始服务，登陆iOS、Android和PC，采用基本免费加道具收费的模式。

游戏以幻想生物“キボ”栖息的プロミリア大陆为舞台，玩家扮演预言中的“星臨者”，与个性各异的伙伴共同对抗威胁世界的敌人，并逐步解开大陆之谜。

开服纪念活动中，首次登录可领取★4角色“アニス”和20个抽卡道具“無垢な痕跡”，加上登录与任务奖励，累计可免费抽卡40次以上；新手卡池“始源の観測”十连消耗8个道具，30次以内必出★5。限定角色“塗山小玉”及其专属“アニマ”的卡池持续至12月17日4时59分，分别在最多90抽和80抽内确定，相关剧情活动持续至2027年1月5日4时59分。

According to 4Gamer, the fantasy RPG Azur Promilia (アズールプロミリア), jointly published by Yostar and Manjuu Games, officially began service on November 26 for iOS, Android, and PC under a free-to-play model with in-app purchases.

据4Gamer报道，Yostar与Manjuu Games共同发行的幻想RPG《Azur Promilia》（アズールプロミリア）于11月26日正式开始服务，登陆iOS、Android和PC，采用基本免费加道具收费的模式。

Set on the Promilia continent, where fantasy creatures called 'Kibo' dwell, the game has players take on the role of the prophesied 'Xinglinzhe,' fighting alongside companions of diverse personalities against enemies threatening the world and gradually unraveling the continent's mysteries.

游戏以幻想生物“キボ”栖息的プロミリア大陆为舞台，玩家扮演预言中的“星臨者”，与个性各异的伙伴共同对抗威胁世界的敌人，并逐步解开大陆之谜。

During the launch celebration, a first login grants the ★4 character 'Anis' and 20 gacha items called 'Innocent Traces,' and together with login and quest rewards, players can make more than 40 free draws in total; a ten-pull on the beginner banner 'Observation of the Origin' costs 8 items, with a ★5 guaranteed within 30 pulls. The gacha banners for the limited character 'Tushan Xiaoyu' and her exclusive 'Anima' run until December 17 at 4:59, with the former guaranteed within at most 90 pulls and the latter within 80, while the related story event runs until January 5, 2027, at 4:59.

Arknights: Endfield New Version Launches, Introducing New ★6 'Ke-chan'

《Arknights: Endfield》新版本配信，新★6“ケーちゃん”登场

11月25日 17:55重要可靠性 B单一来源报道

据Inside报道，GRYPHLINE宣布由Hypergryph开发、GRYPHLINE发行的3D实时战略RPG《Arknights: Endfield》新版本“キノコ菌グ！？君との大冒険！”于11月25日开始配信，平台为PS5、PC（Steam）、iOS和Android，基本免费加道具收费。

版本新增★6干员“ケーちゃん”（CV瀬戸麻沙美，自然属性），其特别寻访持续至12月23日11时59分（服务器时间）；★5干员“ミミノ”（CV菱川花菜，物理属性）与★5武器“ライジングスター”则从12月16日12时起至下次版本升级维护开始前，通过登录免费发放。

版本同时追加常设内容“封鎖区域再建”，玩家推进任务并生产物资完成建设任务后可获得“赤晶玉”等奖励；另有含两种多人卡牌游戏的休闲活动“ラッキーキャロット”陆续开放。

According to Inside, GRYPHLINE announced that the new version 'Kinoko-kin Gu!? Adventure with You!' of the 3D real-time strategy RPG Arknights: Endfield, developed by Hypergryph and published by GRYPHLINE, began distribution on November 25 for PS5, PC (Steam), iOS, and Android, as a free-to-play title with in-app purchases.

据Inside报道，GRYPHLINE宣布由Hypergryph开发、GRYPHLINE发行的3D实时战略RPG《Arknights: Endfield》新版本“キノコ菌グ！？君との大冒険！”于11月25日开始配信，平台为PS5、PC（Steam）、iOS和Android，基本免费加道具收费。

The version adds ★6 operator 'Ke-chan' (CV: Asami Seto, Nature attribute), whose special Headhunting runs until 11:59 on December 23 (server time); ★5 operator 'Mimino' (CV: Hana Hishikawa, Physical attribute) and the ★5 weapon 'Rising Star' will be distributed free via login from 12:00 on December 16 until the start of maintenance for the next version update.

版本新增★6干员“ケーちゃん”（CV瀬戸麻沙美，自然属性），其特别寻访持续至12月23日11时59分（服务器时间）；★5干员“ミミノ”（CV菱川花菜，物理属性）与★5武器“ライジングスター”则从12月16日12时起至下次版本升级维护开始前，通过登录免费发放。

The version also adds the permanent content 'Blockaded Area Reconstruction,' in which players can obtain rewards such as 'Red Crystal Jade' after advancing missions and producing materials to complete construction tasks; a casual event 'Lucky Carrot' featuring two multiplayer card games will also open gradually.

版本同时追加常设内容“封鎖区域再建”，玩家推进任务并生产物资完成建设任务后可获得“赤晶玉”等奖励；另有含两种多人卡牌游戏的休闲活动“ラッキーキャロット”陆续开放。

GTA 6 Street Flag Wall Found to Include Republic of China Flag, Sparking Community Discussion

《GTA 6》街景国旗墙被玩家发现含中华民国国旗，引发社群讨论

11月25日 19:12重要可靠性 B独立多源核实

联合新闻网与自由时报11月25日报道，多名玩家在Threads分享《GTA 6》游戏画面，称一处仓库外墙并排绘有阿根廷、哥伦比亚、法国、委内瑞拉、巴西、加拿大等国国旗，其中包含中华民国国旗，未见中华人民共和国国旗。据自由时报，该处位于游戏地图“艾米里歐車坊加油站”附近、外墙标有“EMILIO'S AUTO CENTER”字样的建筑。

相关贴文引发讨论，有网友以“辱华”调侃，也有人表示要前往该处拍照留念。

On November 25, UDN News and Liberty Times reported that multiple players shared GTA 6 game footage on Threads, saying that the exterior wall of a warehouse had the national flags of Argentina, Colombia, France, Venezuela, Brazil, and Canada painted side by side, including the flag of the Republic of China but no flag of the People's Republic of China. According to Liberty Times, the location is near "Emilio's Auto Center Gas Station" on the game map, at a building with "EMILIO'S AUTO CENTER" on its exterior wall.

联合新闻网与自由时报11月25日报道，多名玩家在Threads分享《GTA 6》游戏画面，称一处仓库外墙并排绘有阿根廷、哥伦比亚、法国、委内瑞拉、巴西、加拿大等国国旗，其中包含中华民国国旗，未见中华人民共和国国旗。据自由时报，该处位于游戏地图“艾米里歐車坊加油站”附近、外墙标有“EMILIO'S AUTO CENTER”字样的建筑。

Related posts sparked discussion, with some netizens joking about it as "insulting China," while others said they wanted to go to the location to take photos as a memento.

Kindergarten WARS TV Anime Set for April 2027, Ado to Perform Opening Theme

《幼稚園WARS》电视动画定档2027年4月，Ado演唱片头曲

11月25日 17:00重要可靠性 B单一来源报道

据Famitsu报道，动画官方新闻稿宣布，漫画《幼稚園WARS》的电视动画将于2027年4月在东京电视台系6局网络播出，第1弹PV、主视觉与主要制作阵容同时公开。原作由千葉侑生在《少年Jump+》连载，单行本发行量突破200万部，类型为暴力与恋爱喜剧。 动画由サンライズFelixFilm制作，监督为五味伸介，系列构成与脚本为金﨑貴臣，角色设计为山中純子。片头曲为Ado演唱的《ビバヤタラメッタ》，由Vocaloid P和田たけあき创作。

故事舞台是世界各国要人的孩子就读的“黑色幼儿园”，原杀手等罪犯以“特殊教师”身份保护园童、与袭击者战斗，主角是原杀手リタ。 Famitsu的报道列出上述制作信息，未提供社区讨论数据。

According to Famitsu, the anime's official press release announced that the TV anime of the manga Kindergarten WARS will air in April 2027 on the six-station TV Tokyo network, with the first PV, key visual, and main production staff also unveiled at the same time. The original is by Yusei Chiba and has been serialized on Shonen Jump+; its collected volumes have exceeded 2 million copies in circulation, and it is a violent romantic comedy. The anime is produced by Sunrise FelixFilm, with Shinsuke Gomi as director, Takaomi Kanasaki handling series composition and scripts, and Junko Yamanaka as character designer. The opening theme is 'Viva Yatarametta,' sung by Ado and created by Vocaloid P Takeaki Wada.

The story is set in a 'Black Kindergarten' attended by the children of dignitaries from around the world, where former assassins and other criminals serve as 'special teachers' to protect the children and fight attackers, with former assassin Rita as the protagonist.
