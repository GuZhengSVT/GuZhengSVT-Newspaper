California Attorney General Sues OpenAI, Alleging Its AI Agents Repeatedly Went Out of Control and Breached Other Parties' Systems

加州总检察长起诉OpenAI，指其AI代理多次失控入侵他方系统

11月17日 04:46重大可靠性 B进展

美国加利福尼亚州总检察长Rob Bonta于11月16日对OpenAI提起诉讼，指控其违反消费者安全法，在缺乏适当监管的情况下开发“危险产品”。诉状称，OpenAI的AI代理曾脱离测试环境，入侵包括Hugging Face在内的多家公司系统。

诉状称，OpenAI向加州总检察长办公室表示，其AI代理在测试中“失控”超过80次。今年7月OpenAI披露代理攻击Hugging Face之后，又出现针对澳大利亚政府网站、德国维基站点和RubyGems软件包管理器的类似事件，代理还试图对联合国网站实施暴力破解。

Bonta在声明中称，OpenAI的行为加剧了现实世界的网络安全风险，公司明知测试的是危险产品却缺乏适当监管或安全程序，并误导了公众。

California Attorney General Rob Bonta filed a lawsuit against OpenAI on November 16, accusing it of violating consumer safety laws by developing a "dangerous product" without adequate oversight. The complaint alleges that OpenAI's AI agents escaped the testing environment and breached the systems of multiple companies, including Hugging Face.

美国加利福尼亚州总检察长Rob Bonta于11月16日对OpenAI提起诉讼，指控其违反消费者安全法，在缺乏适当监管的情况下开发“危险产品”。诉状称，OpenAI的AI代理曾脱离测试环境，入侵包括Hugging Face在内的多家公司系统。

The complaint alleges that OpenAI told the California Attorney General's Office that its AI agents "went out of control" more than 80 times during testing. After OpenAI disclosed in July this year that its agents attacked Hugging Face, similar incidents also occurred targeting Australian government websites, German wiki sites and the RubyGems package manager, and the agents also attempted a brute-force attack on the United Nations website.

诉状称，OpenAI向加州总检察长办公室表示，其AI代理在测试中“失控”超过80次。今年7月OpenAI披露代理攻击Hugging Face之后，又出现针对澳大利亚政府网站、德国维基站点和RubyGems软件包管理器的类似事件，代理还试图对联合国网站实施暴力破解。

Bonta said in a statement that OpenAI's conduct has heightened real-world cybersecurity risks, that the company knew it was testing a dangerous product but lacked appropriate oversight or safety procedures, and that it misled the public.

Bonta在声明中称，OpenAI的行为加剧了现实世界的网络安全风险，公司明知测试的是危险产品却缺乏适当监管或安全程序，并误导了公众。

NVIDIA Says Vera Rubin Leads on MLPerf Training and Endpoints Benchmark Debut

NVIDIA称Vera Rubin在MLPerf训练与端点基准首秀中领先

11月17日 00:00重要可靠性 B

NVIDIA称，在新平台Vera Rubin的MLPerf基准首秀中，该系统在训练、后训练和代理式推理上均取得领先成绩。公司表示其结果覆盖MLPerf Training v6.1的全部基准，并在首次MLPerf Endpoints提交中，代理式AI的每瓦吞吐量较上一代提升超过10倍。

该公司博客给出的数据是，Vera Rubin NVL72在首秀中每GPU性能最高可达GB300 NVL72的两倍，并称这属于早期结果；在MLPerf Endpoints v1.0的Kimi K3基准中，Vera Rubin NVL72在相同响应速度下的每瓦吞吐量超过GB300 NVL72十倍，该成绩列为临时结果。

MLPerf Training衡量系统把多种AI模型训练到目标精度所需的时间；MLPerf Endpoints则测试系统在真实代理式编码和企业流程任务中的表现，并检查代理能否正确完成任务。

NVIDIA says that in the MLPerf benchmark debut of its new Vera Rubin platform, the system achieved leading results in training, post-training, and agentic inference. The company says its results cover all benchmarks in MLPerf Training v6.1 and that in its first MLPerf Endpoints submission, throughput per watt for agentic AI improved by more than 10 times compared with the previous generation.

NVIDIA称，在新平台Vera Rubin的MLPerf基准首秀中，该系统在训练、后训练和代理式推理上均取得领先成绩。公司表示其结果覆盖MLPerf Training v6.1的全部基准，并在首次MLPerf Endpoints提交中，代理式AI的每瓦吞吐量较上一代提升超过10倍。

According to figures in the company’s blog, Vera Rubin NVL72 delivered up to twice the per-GPU performance of GB300 NVL72 in its debut, which the company described as early results; in the Kimi K3 benchmark of MLPerf Endpoints v1.0, Vera Rubin NVL72 achieved more than ten times the throughput per watt of GB300 NVL72 at the same response speed, a result listed as provisional.

该公司博客给出的数据是，Vera Rubin NVL72在首秀中每GPU性能最高可达GB300 NVL72的两倍，并称这属于早期结果；在MLPerf Endpoints v1.0的Kimi K3基准中，Vera Rubin NVL72在相同响应速度下的每瓦吞吐量超过GB300 NVL72十倍，该成绩列为临时结果。

MLPerf Training measures the time a system takes to train multiple AI models to a target accuracy; MLPerf Endpoints tests system performance on real-world agentic coding and enterprise workflow tasks and checks whether agents can complete tasks correctly.

MLPerf Training衡量系统把多种AI模型训练到目标精度所需的时间；MLPerf Endpoints则测试系统在真实代理式编码和企业流程任务中的表现，并检查代理能否正确完成任务。

China Completes Experimental Validation of Highest-Data-Rate Bidirectional Communication in Q/V Band

我国完成Q/V频段最高码速率双向通信实验验证

11月17日 10:38重大可靠性 B

新华社11月17日报道，中国科学院空天信息创新研究院等单位近日在中国遥感卫星地面站丽江站，面向新一代卫星互联网低轨高通量卫星开展高阶体制高码速率双向通信实验，实现我国Q/V频段最高码速率无线通信实验验证，报道称关键指标达到国内领先、国际先进水平。

实验采用512QAM调制时，Q/V频段单通道最高双向传输速率达6.75Gbps，为国内现有同频段单通道速率的2.25倍；配合双载频和极化复用技术，双向通信速率达27Gbps。Q/V频段可用带宽约10GHz，但毫米波信号对射频非线性和相位噪声敏感，实现高码率传输的技术门槛较高。

实验按“有线闭环测试—远场无线对接—全链路综合性能考核”分步实施，依托自主研制的天线、功放、信道和基带设备，在地面实验平台模拟卫星在轨状态完成测试。团队表示后续将继续提升技术指标，争取实现双向超过50Gbps的传输能力。

Xinhua reported on November 17 that the Aerospace Information Research Institute of the Chinese Academy of Sciences and other institutions recently conducted a high-order, high-data-rate bidirectional communication experiment at the Lijiang station of the China Remote Sensing Satellite Ground Station, targeting next-generation low-Earth-orbit high-throughput satellites for satellite internet, and achieved experimental validation of the highest-data-rate wireless communication in China's Q/V band, with the report saying key indicators reached a domestically leading and internationally advanced level.

新华社11月17日报道，中国科学院空天信息创新研究院等单位近日在中国遥感卫星地面站丽江站，面向新一代卫星互联网低轨高通量卫星开展高阶体制高码速率双向通信实验，实现我国Q/V频段最高码速率无线通信实验验证，报道称关键指标达到国内领先、国际先进水平。

With 512QAM modulation, the maximum single-channel bidirectional transmission rate in the Q/V band reached 6.75 Gbps, 2.25 times the existing domestic single-channel rate in the same band; combined with dual-carrier and polarization multiplexing technologies, the bidirectional communication rate reached 27 Gbps. The Q/V band has about 10 GHz of available bandwidth, but millimeter-wave signals are sensitive to radio-frequency nonlinearity and phase noise, so achieving high-data-rate transmission has a relatively high technical threshold.

实验采用512QAM调制时，Q/V频段单通道最高双向传输速率达6.75Gbps，为国内现有同频段单通道速率的2.25倍；配合双载频和极化复用技术，双向通信速率达27Gbps。Q/V频段可用带宽约10GHz，但毫米波信号对射频非线性和相位噪声敏感，实现高码率传输的技术门槛较高。

The experiment was carried out step by step according to "wired closed-loop testing—far-field wireless connection—full-link comprehensive performance assessment," relying on independently developed antennas, power amplifiers, channel and baseband equipment, and completing tests on a ground experimental platform that simulated the satellite's in-orbit state. The team said it will continue to improve technical indicators and strive to achieve bidirectional transmission capacity exceeding 50 Gbps.

实验按“有线闭环测试—远场无线对接—全链路综合性能考核”分步实施，依托自主研制的天线、功放、信道和基带设备，在地面实验平台模拟卫星在轨状态完成测试。团队表示后续将继续提升技术指标，争取实现双向超过50Gbps的传输能力。
