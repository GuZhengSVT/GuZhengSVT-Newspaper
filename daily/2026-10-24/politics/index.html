White House Adjusts Tariffs on Some Canadian Goods, Effective October 30

白宫调整部分加拿大商品关税，10月30日起生效

10月24日 08:36重要可靠性 B进展

白宫10月23日宣布，自10月30日起下调对部分加拿大商品的50%关税，涉及胶合板、部分纸制品、特定机械和制冷设备；同时对地毯和“废弃物”等商品加征关税。白宫发言人米德尔顿称，此举体现特朗普“继续维护美国工人和公平贸易”的努力。加拿大内政贸易部长勒布朗表示，加方正在评估最新举措，愿本着诚意推动更互利的贸易关系。

此次调整距美国中期选举不到两周。缅因州共和党参议员苏珊·柯林斯最先公布豁免消息，称这些商品对缅因州蓝莓、土豆、苹果和海藻种植业至关重要，她曾多次要求特朗普政府减轻关税负担。其民主党挑战者杰克逊批评特朗普的贸易战推高缅因州民众生活成本，柯林斯难辞其咎。

美加贸易谈判8月破裂后，美国对约200亿美元加拿大商品加征50%关税，加拿大随后以对等措施回应。特朗普同日称加拿大正“非常努力”推动贸易协议，加拿大总理卡尼则表示，若美方准备好，协议“今天下午就能签”，否则加方愿意等待。

The White House announced on October 23 that, effective October 30, it will lower the 50% tariff on certain Canadian goods, including plywood, some paper products, specific machinery and refrigeration equipment, while imposing higher tariffs on items such as carpets and "waste" materials. White House spokesperson Middleton said the move reflects Trump's effort to "continue standing up for American workers and fair trade." Canadian Minister of Domestic Trade Leblanc said Canada is assessing the latest measures and is willing to work in good faith toward a more mutually beneficial trade relationship.

The adjustment comes less than two weeks before the U.S. midterm elections. Republican Senator Susan Collins of Maine was the first to disclose the exemptions, saying these goods are vital to Maine's blueberry, potato, apple and seaweed growers, and that she had repeatedly asked the Trump administration to ease the tariff burden. Her Democratic challenger Jackson criticized Trump's trade war for driving up living costs for Mainers and said Collins must bear her share of the blame.

After U.S.-Canada trade talks broke down in August, the United States imposed a 50% tariff on about $20 billion in Canadian goods, and Canada responded with equivalent countermeasures. Trump said the same day that Canada is working "very hard" toward a trade deal, while Canadian Prime Minister Carney said that if the U.S. side is ready, a deal "could be signed this afternoon," and otherwise Canada is willing to wait.

The U.S. Justice Department filed a lawsuit in federal court on October 22, seeking to block certification of the results of Alaska's November 3 Senate election. The complaint says that, besides Republican incumbent Senator Dan Sullivan, another candidate with the same name, Daniel Sullivan, was on the ballot, which could cause voters to cast their ballots incorrectly. The Justice Department asked the judge to rule that if Senator Sullivan loses and the margin is smaller than the number of votes won by the other Sullivan, the state government must not certify the results and must first investigate whether voters were confused.

乌克兰和俄罗斯均确认已就撤离居民和运送援助达成安排，双方称部队将于格林尼治时间10月27日2时停止射击。白宫则称停火自10月29日开始。乌方议员阿拉哈米亚称，“解除奥列什基及周边地区封锁的行动将在不久的将来开始”；俄总统新闻秘书佩斯科夫确认“愿意离开的人可以离开该定居点”。

梅拉尼娅·特朗普称，两国非军事文职官员将合作探访奥列什基居民并确定所需援助，希望撤离的居民将获得协调支持和交通。奥列什基2022年2月开战初期即被俄军控制，战前约有2.4万居民，乌方估计目前仍有约6000人留在当地及周边，包括200名儿童。

The White House said in a statement on October 23 that First Lady Melania Trump "successfully mediated" a local ceasefire between Russia and Ukraine in Russian-occupied Oleshky. The White House said the frontline town, which is "on the brink of disaster," lacks medicine, food and clean water, and that the ceasefire will allow civilians to evacuate and humanitarian aid to be delivered, but the statement did not say how long the ceasefire would last.

The White House said the ceasefire would begin on October 29. Ukrainian lawmaker Arakhamia said that "the operation to lift the blockade of Oleshky and surrounding areas will begin in the near future," while Russian presidential press secretary Peskov confirmed that "those who wish to leave can leave the settlement."

Jordanian Interior Minister Falaya said at a news conference that the attackers were "a group of extremists," that the authorities were moving to arrest them, and that no organization had claimed responsibility.
