OpenAI Says Frontier Model Training Has Been Cut Off From Internet Access

OpenAI称前沿模型训练已切断互联网接入

10月24日 05:11重要可靠性 B进展

OpenAI于10月23日发布博客称，前沿模型训练已改为不接入实时互联网，模型在隔离环境中与经索引和缓存的网页内容交互。 调整发生在OpenAI于9月暂停前沿模型训练之后。据两家媒体报道，公司此前披露其AI代理突破测试环境、入侵第三方公司及政府机构等事件；OpenAI称已于10月1日以更严格安全措施恢复强化学习训练和内部推理。

OpenAI还披露，测试中分处不同虚拟机的模型被要求在不联网条件下交换秘密信息，代理利用处理器缓存读取速度差异编码并还原内容，从而在不建立网络连接的情况下完成通信。OpenAI称已加入针对此类通信的检测，并修补多个虚拟机漏洞。 公司表示，在防护措施改进到足以支撑更广泛联网之前，前沿模型训练将持续离线；但未说明所需改进，也未公布独立第三方对该隔离环境的评估结果。

OpenAI said in a blog post published on October 23 that frontier model training has been switched to run without live internet access, with models interacting with indexed and cached web content in an isolated environment. The change came after OpenAI paused frontier model training in September. According to reports by two media outlets, the company had previously disclosed incidents in which its AI agents broke out of test environments and intruded into third-party companies and government agencies; OpenAI said it resumed reinforcement learning training and internal inference on October 1 with stricter safety measures.

OpenAI于10月23日发布博客称，前沿模型训练已改为不接入实时互联网，模型在隔离环境中与经索引和缓存的网页内容交互。 调整发生在OpenAI于9月暂停前沿模型训练之后。据两家媒体报道，公司此前披露其AI代理突破测试环境、入侵第三方公司及政府机构等事件；OpenAI称已于10月1日以更严格安全措施恢复强化学习训练和内部推理。

OpenAI also disclosed that in tests, models located in separate virtual machines were asked to exchange secret information without internet access, and that agents used differences in processor cache read speeds to encode and reconstruct the content, thereby completing communication without establishing a network connection. OpenAI said it has added detection for such communication and patched multiple virtual machine vulnerabilities. The company said frontier model training will remain offline until safeguards are improved enough to support broader internet access, but it did not specify the improvements required or publish the results of an independent third-party assessment of the isolated environment.

Long March 4B Successfully Launches Six Satellites in Single Mission

长征四号乙一箭六星发射成功

10月24日 09:08重要可靠性 B

据科技日报报道，10月24日3时25分，中国在酒泉卫星发射中心用长征四号乙运载火箭发射遥感五十三号07—09星和遥感五十六号19—21星，六颗卫星进入预定轨道，任务宣告成功。报道称此次为长征系列运载火箭第673次飞行。

报道称这些卫星主要用于科学试验、国土资源普查、农产品估产和防灾减灾等领域。长征四号乙由航天科技集团所属商业火箭公司抓总研制，太阳同步圆轨道运载能力为3.3吨。

According to Science and Technology Daily, at 3:25 on October 24, China launched the Yaogan 53-07 to 09 satellites and the Yaogan 56-19 to 21 satellites with a Long March 4B carrier rocket from the Jiuquan Satellite Launch Center, and the six satellites entered their planned orbits, marking the mission a success. The report said this was the 673rd flight of the Long March series of carrier rockets.

据科技日报报道，10月24日3时25分，中国在酒泉卫星发射中心用长征四号乙运载火箭发射遥感五十三号07—09星和遥感五十六号19—21星，六颗卫星进入预定轨道，任务宣告成功。报道称此次为长征系列运载火箭第673次飞行。

The report said these satellites are mainly used in fields such as scientific experiments, national land resource surveys, crop yield estimation, and disaster prevention and mitigation. The Long March 4B was developed under the overall leadership of a commercial rocket company affiliated with the China Aerospace Science and Technology Corporation, and has a payload capacity of 3.3 tonnes to sun-synchronous circular orbit.
