China releases new medical insurance drug catalog: 107 medicines added, including 51 Class 1 new drugs

中国新版医保药品目录公布：新增107种药品，含51个一类新药

11月14日 10:44重要可靠性 B

国家医保局11月14日在苏州发布2026年国家医保药品目录和商业健康保险创新药品目录。新版医保目录新增107种药品，其中51个为一类新药，数量再创历史新高，新增品种覆盖肿瘤、罕见病、慢性病、精神疾病等领域。

商业健康保险创新药品目录新增12种药品，包括全球首个实体瘤CAR-T、中国首个血友病基因治疗药物，调整后共28种。新版医保目录药品总数达到3331个，其中西药1935个、中成药1396个，将于2027年1月1日落地执行。

The National Healthcare Security Administration released the 2026 national medical insurance drug catalog and the commercial health insurance innovative drug catalog in Suzhou on November 14. The new medical insurance catalog adds 107 drugs, including 51 Class 1 new drugs, a number that once again set a record high, with the newly added products covering areas such as tumors, rare diseases, chronic diseases and mental illness.

国家医保局11月14日在苏州发布2026年国家医保药品目录和商业健康保险创新药品目录。新版医保目录新增107种药品，其中51个为一类新药，数量再创历史新高，新增品种覆盖肿瘤、罕见病、慢性病、精神疾病等领域。

The commercial health insurance innovative drug catalog adds 12 drugs, including the world's first CAR-T therapy for solid tumors and China's first gene therapy drug for hemophilia, bringing the total to 28 after the adjustment. The new medical insurance catalog contains a total of 3,331 drugs, including 1,935 Western medicines and 1,396 traditional Chinese patent medicines, and will take effect on January 1, 2027.

商业健康保险创新药品目录新增12种药品，包括全球首个实体瘤CAR-T、中国首个血友病基因治疗药物，调整后共28种。新版医保目录药品总数达到3331个，其中西药1935个、中成药1396个，将于2027年1月1日落地执行。

FAA Lifts Nationwide Ground Stop After More Than Four Hours as Air Traffic Control Communications System Overloads

美联邦航空局解除全国停飞令：空管通信系统过载，逾四小时后复飞

11月14日 11:10重大可靠性 B进展

美国联邦航空管理局（FAA）13日发布全国停飞令，禁止所有国内起飞航班，停止运行超过四小时，至美东时间当晚20时15分解除。这是2001年9月11日以来最长的一次全国范围停飞。

据航班追踪网站FlightAware统计，13日超过8500架次航班延误、近8000架次取消，停飞时已有3500多架飞机在空中。欧洲空管组织Eurocontrol称，应FAA请求暂停成员国赴美航班。

交通部长达菲在记者会上说，一套老式模拟空管通信系统的交换机和路由器因数据过载宕机，影响通信与雷达，系统需全面改造，估计耗资500亿美元；达菲称该系统还会继续故障。国土安全部长马林称没有证据显示是网络攻击，并排除与前一天开始的全球微软故障有关。两位部长都称停飞期间公众没有危险。

The U.S. Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) issued a nationwide ground stop on the 13th, barring all domestic departing flights and halting operations for more than four hours until it was lifted at 8:15 p.m. Eastern Time that evening. This was the longest nationwide ground stop since September 11, 2001.

美国联邦航空管理局（FAA）13日发布全国停飞令，禁止所有国内起飞航班，停止运行超过四小时，至美东时间当晚20时15分解除。这是2001年9月11日以来最长的一次全国范围停飞。

According to statistics from flight-tracking website FlightAware, more than 8,500 flights were delayed and nearly 8,000 were canceled on the 13th, while more than 3,500 aircraft were already in the air when the ground stop began. The European air traffic control organization Eurocontrol said it suspended member states' flights to the United States at the FAA's request.

Transportation Secretary Duffy said at a press conference that the switches and routers of an old analog air traffic control communications system had crashed due to data overload, affecting communications and radar; the system needs a comprehensive overhaul estimated to cost $50 billion, and Duffy said it would continue to fail. Homeland Security Secretary Malin said there was no evidence that it was a cyberattack and ruled out any link to the global Microsoft outage that began the previous day. Both ministers said there was no danger to the public during the ground stop.
