HoYoverse's Petit Planet to Stream Pre-Launch Program on November 1

HoYoverse《プチプラネット》将于11月1日播出发布前瞻节目

10月23日 11:49重要可靠性 B

HoYoverse于10月23日宣布，预定今冬发行的慢生活游戏《プチプラネット》将于11月1日20时在YouTube和官方X播出发布前瞻节目。据4Gamer报道，节目将由开发团队成员与特别嘉宾出演，介绍游戏内容以及今后计划展开的活动与活动信息。游戏对应PC、iOS和Android平台。

HoYoverse announced on October 23 that Petit Planet, its slow-life game scheduled for release this winter, will stream a pre-launch program on November 1 at 20:00 on YouTube and its official X account. According to 4Gamer, the program will feature development team members and special guests, introducing the game's content as well as information on events and activities planned for the future. The game will be available on PC, iOS, and Android.

HoYoverse于10月23日宣布，预定今冬发行的慢生活游戏《プチプラネット》将于11月1日20时在YouTube和官方X播出发布前瞻节目。据4Gamer报道，节目将由开发团队成员与特别嘉宾出演，介绍游戏内容以及今后计划展开的活动与活动信息。游戏对应PC、iOS和Android平台。

Wo Long 2 Releases Alpha Feedback Report and Improvement Plan

《卧龙2》公布α体验版反馈报告及改善方针

10月23日 11:10重要可靠性 B

光荣特库摩与Team NINJA于10月23日公开了《卧龙2：Wings of Ember》α体验版的反馈报告及改善方针。该α体验版于9月16日至30日配信，收录「长坂」开放战场，玩家可与刘备军协力对抗追击的曹操军与妖魔。据4Gamer报道，报告汇总了画质、难度、武器种类等问卷结果。

根据玩家意见，开发团队将改善新系统「凰技」的复杂度与成功判定，重新审视「气势」的收支平衡，并优化画质、提升加载速度。游戏预定2027年3月4日登陆PS5、Xbox Series、Switch 2和PC，并加入Game Pass。

Koei Tecmo and Team NINJA released the feedback report and improvement plan for the alpha trial version of Wo Long 2: Wings of Ember on October 23. The alpha trial was distributed from September 16 to 30 and includes the open battlefield "Changban," where players can fight alongside Liu Bei's army against the pursuing Cao Cao forces and demons. According to 4Gamer, the report summarizes survey results on graphics, difficulty, weapon types, and other aspects.

光荣特库摩与Team NINJA于10月23日公开了《卧龙2：Wings of Ember》α体验版的反馈报告及改善方针。该α体验版于9月16日至30日配信，收录「长坂」开放战场，玩家可与刘备军协力对抗追击的曹操军与妖魔。据4Gamer报道，报告汇总了画质、难度、武器种类等问卷结果。

Based on player feedback, the development team will improve the complexity and success judgment of the new "Phoenix Art" system, reexamine the balance of "Morale" gains and costs, and optimize graphics and increase loading speed. The game is scheduled for release on March 4, 2027, for PS5, Xbox Series, Switch 2, and PC, and will also be available on Game Pass.

根据玩家意见，开发团队将改善新系统「凰技」的复杂度与成功判定，重新审视「气势」的收支平衡，并优化画质、提升加载速度。游戏预定2027年3月4日登陆PS5、Xbox Series、Switch 2和PC，并加入Game Pass。

Dispatch Launches on iOS and Android December 9; Deluxe Edition Adds Commentary and Storyboard Modes

《Dispatch》将于12月9日登陆iOS与Android，豪华版新增解说与分镜模式

10月23日 00:56重要可靠性 B

AdHoc Studio于10月22日宣布，超级英雄职场喜剧冒险游戏《Dispatch》将于12月9日登陆iOS和Android。据Gematsu报道，手游版前四集可免费游玩，完整版解锁价格为19.99美元，App Store与Google Play的事前登录即将开启。游戏此前已于2025年10月22日登陆PS5和PC，后陆续登陆Switch 2、Switch、Xbox Series及Microsoft Store，累计销量超过500万份。

豪华版所有者现已可免费获取更新，新增收录Aaron Paul、Laura Bailey等演职人员超过16小时评论的解说模式，以及让玩家以原始分镜动画体验整个游戏的分镜模式；标准版玩家可以10.99美元购买豪华版升级。

AdHoc Studio announced on October 22 that Dispatch, a superhero workplace comedy adventure game, will launch on iOS and Android on December 9. According to Gematsu, the first four episodes of the mobile version can be played for free, the full version costs $19.99 to unlock, and pre-registration on the App Store and Google Play will open soon. The game previously launched on PS5 and PC on October 22, 2025, and later came to Switch 2, Switch, Xbox Series, and the Microsoft Store, with cumulative sales exceeding 5 million copies.

AdHoc Studio于10月22日宣布，超级英雄职场喜剧冒险游戏《Dispatch》将于12月9日登陆iOS和Android。据Gematsu报道，手游版前四集可免费游玩，完整版解锁价格为19.99美元，App Store与Google Play的事前登录即将开启。游戏此前已于2025年10月22日登陆PS5和PC，后陆续登陆Switch 2、Switch、Xbox Series及Microsoft Store，累计销量超过500万份。

Deluxe Edition owners can now obtain a free update that adds a commentary mode featuring more than 16 hours of commentary from cast and crew including Aaron Paul and Laura Bailey, as well as a storyboard mode that lets players experience the entire game with the original storyboard animation; Standard Edition players can purchase the Deluxe Edition upgrade for $10.99.

豪华版所有者现已可免费获取更新，新增收录Aaron Paul、Laura Bailey等演职人员超过16小时评论的解说模式，以及让玩家以原始分镜动画体验整个游戏的分镜模式；标准版玩家可以10.99美元购买豪华版升级。

AI Character Interaction Social Simulator ailiaili Unveils Concept Teaser

AI角色互动社交模拟器《ailiaili 爱哩爱哩》公开概念预告

10月23日 10:47重要可靠性 B

Ogopogo于10月21日正式公开新作《ailiaili 爱哩爱哩》，这是一款AI角色互动社交网络模拟器，首支概念影片同步发布，Steam与WeGame商店页也已开启。据机核报道，故事始于一台据说1998年从深圳工厂流出的AI智能学习伙伴机AI-98，玩家多年后修复设备，发现里面居民仍在生活、打工、写日记，并会按照自己的节奏在城市分区移动，把经历变成账号动态。

玩家可以悄悄影响这个世界，但不能与居民直接对话，居民不知道玩家存在。游戏预计支持简体中文与英文，发行时间与价格尚未公布。10月24日至25日，本作将亮相核聚变北京站，现场开放「AI-98现实验证／居民认领」互动。

Ogopogo officially unveiled its new title ailiaili on October 21, an AI character interaction social network simulator, alongside the release of its first concept trailer, with Steam and WeGame store pages also now live. According to Gcores, the story begins with an AI smart learning companion device, AI-98, said to have left a Shenzhen factory in 1998; years later, the player repairs the device and discovers that its residents are still living there, working, and writing diaries, moving between city districts at their own pace and turning their experiences into account updates.

Ogopogo于10月21日正式公开新作《ailiaili 爱哩爱哩》，这是一款AI角色互动社交网络模拟器，首支概念影片同步发布，Steam与WeGame商店页也已开启。据机核报道，故事始于一台据说1998年从深圳工厂流出的AI智能学习伙伴机AI-98，玩家多年后修复设备，发现里面居民仍在生活、打工、写日记，并会按照自己的节奏在城市分区移动，把经历变成账号动态。

Players can quietly influence this world but cannot talk directly with the residents, who do not know the player exists. The game is expected to support Simplified Chinese and English, with a release date and price yet to be announced. From October 24 to 25, the game will appear at Fusion Beijing, where an "AI-98 Real-World Verification / Resident Claim" interaction will be open on site.
