OPPO Launches Find X10 Series in Taiwan; Pro Max Features Three 200MP Cameras

OPPO在台发布Find X10系列，Pro Max配三颗2亿像素镜头

10月29日 14:19重要可靠性 B

OPPO于10月29日在台湾发表Find X10与Find X10 Pro Max。Pro Max搭载三颗2亿像素哈苏镜头。 Find X10采用2亿像素主摄、2亿像素长焦和5000万像素超广角；两款分别使用天玑9600M和天玑9600 Pro，电池8000mAh，支持80W有线与50W无线充电。

据壹蘋新闻网报道，Find X10在台售价42,990元起，12+512GB版本46,990元，Find X10 Pro Max 16+512GB版本58,990元。 中央社称预计11月2日全台通路开卖，壹蘋新闻网称11月6日正式开卖，两篇报道的开卖日期不一致。

OPPO launched the Find X10 and Find X10 Pro Max in Taiwan on October 29. The Pro Max features three 200-megapixel Hasselblad cameras. The Find X10 has a 200-megapixel main camera, a 200-megapixel telephoto camera and a 50-megapixel ultra-wide camera; the two models use the Dimensity 9600M and Dimensity 9600 Pro respectively, have 8000mAh batteries, and support 80W wired and 50W wireless charging.

OPPO于10月29日在台湾发表Find X10与Find X10 Pro Max。Pro Max搭载三颗2亿像素哈苏镜头。 Find X10采用2亿像素主摄、2亿像素长焦和5000万像素超广角；两款分别使用天玑9600M和天玑9600 Pro，电池8000mAh，支持80W有线与50W无线充电。

According to Yiping News Network, the Find X10 is priced from NT$42,990 in Taiwan, with the 12+512GB version at NT$46,990 and the Find X10 Pro Max 16+512GB version at NT$58,990. CNA said sales across Taiwan were expected to begin on November 2, while Yiping News Network said they would officially begin on November 6; the two reports give inconsistent on-sale dates.
