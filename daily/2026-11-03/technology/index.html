QbitAI Reports MIT Experiment: GPT-6 Astra Directly Rewrites Qwen Weights

量子位报道MIT实验：GPT-6 Astra直接改写Qwen权重

11月03日 09:57重要可靠性 B进展

据量子位报道，MIT团队进行了一项实验：让GPT-6 Astra直接读取并修改Qwen3.5-0.8B的权重，只改动其中约0.016%，全程没有使用训练或梯度下降。

报道称，在相同采样设置下，该模型的MATH-500得分从7.9%升到48.1%，数学运算能力约为原来的6倍；指令执行能力也提高3倍。

报道还称，Astra在MinAtar小游戏实验中从零生成网络权重，一次回复的平均得分为517.4，超过训练5亿步的PPO基线20倍。

According to a QbitAI report, an MIT team conducted an experiment in which GPT-6 Astra directly read and modified the weights of Qwen3.5-0.8B, changing only about 0.016% of them, without using training or gradient descent at any point.

据量子位报道，MIT团队进行了一项实验：让GPT-6 Astra直接读取并修改Qwen3.5-0.8B的权重，只改动其中约0.016%，全程没有使用训练或梯度下降。

The report said that under the same sampling settings, the model's MATH-500 score rose from 7.9% to 48.1%, making its mathematical computation ability about six times the original; its instruction-following ability also rose threefold.

报道称，在相同采样设置下，该模型的MATH-500得分从7.9%升到48.1%，数学运算能力约为原来的6倍；指令执行能力也提高3倍。

The report also said that in a MinAtar mini-game experiment, Astra generated network weights from scratch and averaged 517.4 points per response, more than 20 times that of a PPO baseline trained for 500 million steps.

报道还称，Astra在MinAtar小游戏实验中从零生成网络权重，一次回复的平均得分为517.4，超过训练5亿步的PPO基线20倍。

Lijian-2 Y2 Successfully Launches 18 Satellites in Qianfan Polar Orbit Group 21

力箭二号遥二成功发射千帆极轨21组18颗卫星

11月03日 09:33重大可靠性 B

11月3日8时15分，中科宇航力箭二号遥二运载火箭在东风商业航天创新试验区点火升空，将千帆极轨21组共18颗卫星送入预定轨道，飞行试验任务取得圆满成功。

此次任务是力箭二号运载火箭的第2次飞行，也是力箭系列运载火箭第18次发射；力箭二号是中科宇航自主研制的液体运载火箭，500公里太阳同步轨道运载能力8吨。

At 8:15 a.m. on November 3, CAS Space's Lijian-2 Y2 carrier rocket lifted off from the Dongfeng Commercial Aerospace Innovation Test Zone and placed the 18 satellites of Qianfan Polar Orbit Group 21 into their planned orbit, marking a complete success of the flight test mission.

11月3日8时15分，中科宇航力箭二号遥二运载火箭在东风商业航天创新试验区点火升空，将千帆极轨21组共18颗卫星送入预定轨道，飞行试验任务取得圆满成功。

This mission was the second flight of the Lijian-2 carrier rocket and the 18th launch of the Lijian series; Lijian-2 is a liquid-propellant carrier rocket independently developed by CAS Space, with a payload capacity of 8 tonnes to a 500-kilometer sun-synchronous orbit.

此次任务是力箭二号运载火箭的第2次飞行，也是力箭系列运载火箭第18次发射；力箭二号是中科宇航自主研制的液体运载火箭，500公里太阳同步轨道运载能力8吨。

ESA Validates ERMIS-3 CubeSat Laser Communications Link

ESA验证ERMIS-3立方星激光通信链路

11月02日 16:55重要可靠性 A

欧洲航天局11月2日说，雅典大学研制的8U立方星ERMIS-3与希腊Holomonds光学地面站建立激光链路，验证了精确指向、捕获和跟踪。

ESA称，这是希腊首次与低地球轨道卫星建立光学链路；ERMIS-3携带立陶宛公司Astrolight的ATLAS-1光学终端，于今年3月30日发射。

该演示发生在卫星调试后数周，属于小卫星通信技术的在轨验证，不是业务运行。

On November 2, the European Space Agency said the 8U CubeSat ERMIS-3, developed by the University of Athens, established a laser link with the Greek Holomonds optical ground station, validating precise pointing, acquisition and tracking.

欧洲航天局11月2日说，雅典大学研制的8U立方星ERMIS-3与希腊Holomonds光学地面站建立激光链路，验证了精确指向、捕获和跟踪。

ESA said this was Greece's first optical link with a satellite in low Earth orbit; ERMIS-3 carries the ATLAS-1 optical terminal from Lithuanian company Astrolight and was launched on March 30 this year.

ESA称，这是希腊首次与低地球轨道卫星建立光学链路；ERMIS-3携带立陶宛公司Astrolight的ATLAS-1光学终端，于今年3月30日发射。

The demonstration took place several weeks after satellite commissioning and was an in-orbit validation of small-satellite communications technology, not operational service.

该演示发生在卫星调试后数周，属于小卫星通信技术的在轨验证，不是业务运行。

Marvell to Acquire Lumentum in About $115 Billion All-Stock Deal

Marvell约1150亿美元全股票收购Lumentum

11月03日 00:53重大可靠性 B进展

据TechPowerUp转载的双方新闻稿，Marvell与Lumentum 11月2日宣布已签署最终协议，Marvell以全股票交易收购Lumentum，隐含企业价值约1150亿美元，相当于每股约1132美元，较10月28日未受影响收盘价溢价约23.9%。

按换股比例，Lumentum股东每股将获得4.5股Marvell普通股；交易完成后，Marvell现有股东预计持有合并公司约67%，Lumentum股东约33%。

交易预计2028年上半年完成，需双方股东批准、监管机构核准及其他惯常条件；Lumentum CEO Michael Hurlston等三名董事将加入Marvell董事会。

According to the joint press release republished by TechPowerUp, Marvell and Lumentum announced on November 2 that they had signed a definitive agreement for Marvell to acquire Lumentum in an all-stock transaction with an implied enterprise value of about $115 billion, or about $1,132 per share, representing a premium of about 23.9% over the unaffected closing price on October 28.

据TechPowerUp转载的双方新闻稿，Marvell与Lumentum 11月2日宣布已签署最终协议，Marvell以全股票交易收购Lumentum，隐含企业价值约1150亿美元，相当于每股约1132美元，较10月28日未受影响收盘价溢价约23.9%。

Under the exchange ratio, Lumentum shareholders will receive 4.5 shares of Marvell common stock for each Lumentum share; after the transaction closes, existing Marvell shareholders are expected to hold about 67% of the combined company and Lumentum shareholders about 33%.

按换股比例，Lumentum股东每股将获得4.5股Marvell普通股；交易完成后，Marvell现有股东预计持有合并公司约67%，Lumentum股东约33%。

The transaction is expected to close in the first half of 2028, subject to approval by both companies' shareholders, regulatory approvals and other customary conditions; three directors, including Lumentum CEO Michael Hurlston, will join Marvell's board.

交易预计2028年上半年完成，需双方股东批准、监管机构核准及其他惯常条件；Lumentum CEO Michael Hurlston等三名董事将加入Marvell董事会。

AT&T Says It Completed Handoff Between Terrestrial Cellular and Direct-to-Device Satellite Networks

AT&T称完成地面蜂窝与卫星直连网络切换

11月03日 01:57重大可靠性 A

AT&T 11月2日在X上宣布，其与AST SpaceMobile完成了一次地面蜂窝网络与直连设备卫星网络之间的切换，并称这是行业首次。

AT&T称，该演示使用了爱立信和诺基亚的技术，使普通智能手机上的卫星连接更接近商用服务；这是测试里程碑，不代表商用服务已经上线。

AT&T announced on X on November 2 that it had completed a handoff between a terrestrial cellular network and a direct-to-device satellite network with AST SpaceMobile, which it called an industry first.

AT&T 11月2日在X上宣布，其与AST SpaceMobile完成了一次地面蜂窝网络与直连设备卫星网络之间的切换，并称这是行业首次。

AT&T said the demonstration used technology from Ericsson and Nokia, bringing satellite connectivity on ordinary smartphones closer to commercial service; it is a testing milestone and does not mean commercial service has launched.

AT&T称，该演示使用了爱立信和诺基亚的技术，使普通智能手机上的卫星连接更接近商用服务；这是测试里程碑，不代表商用服务已经上线。
