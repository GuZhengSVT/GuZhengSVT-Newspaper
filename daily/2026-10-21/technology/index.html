NASA Announces Uzbekistan as a Signatory to the Artemis Accords

NASA 宣布乌兹别克斯坦成为阿尔忒弥斯协定签署方

10月20日 22:21重要可靠性 A

NASA 发布新闻稿，宣布乌兹别克斯坦成为《阿尔忒弥斯协定》的最新签署方。新闻稿以“NASA Welcomes Uzbekistan as Newest Artemis Accords Signatory”为题。

NASA issued a press release announcing that Uzbekistan has become the latest signatory to the Artemis Accords. The press release is titled "NASA Welcomes Uzbekistan as Newest Artemis Accords Signatory."
