Huawei Cloud Showcases CloudRobo Platform, Plans to Open-Source World Model on November 30

华为云展示CloudRobo平台，计划11月30日开源世界模型

11月20日 09:07重要可靠性 B进展

据量子位11月20日报道，华为云在11月16日发布昇腾原生具身世界模型CloudRobo-WM1.2，两天后在上海举办具身智能高层论坛，介绍CloudRobo平台从数据处理、模型训练、仿真验证到真机部署的链路。

报道称，CloudRobo平台已积累超过100万条导航轨迹、5万多条操作轨迹和50多种数据处理算子；计划于11月30日正式开源的CloudRobo-WM1.2也将成为平台上的模型资源。

现场还签署了备忘录，华为云与福建小状智能准备把CloudRobo和昇腾算力用于高校实训。

According to a November 20 report by QbitAI, Huawei Cloud released the Ascend-native embodied world model CloudRobo-WM1.2 on November 16 and two days later held a high-level embodied intelligence forum in Shanghai, where it introduced the CloudRobo platform's pipeline from data processing and model training to simulation validation and real-robot deployment.

据量子位11月20日报道，华为云在11月16日发布昇腾原生具身世界模型CloudRobo-WM1.2，两天后在上海举办具身智能高层论坛，介绍CloudRobo平台从数据处理、模型训练、仿真验证到真机部署的链路。

The report said the CloudRobo platform has accumulated more than 1 million navigation trajectories, over 50,000 manipulation trajectories, and more than 50 data processing operators; CloudRobo-WM1.2, scheduled for official open-source release on November 30, will also become a model resource on the platform.

报道称，CloudRobo平台已积累超过100万条导航轨迹、5万多条操作轨迹和50多种数据处理算子；计划于11月30日正式开源的CloudRobo-WM1.2也将成为平台上的模型资源。

A memorandum was also signed at the event, with Huawei Cloud and Fujian Xiaozhuang Intelligent planning to use CloudRobo and Ascend computing power for university practical training.

现场还签署了备忘录，华为云与福建小状智能准备把CloudRobo和昇腾算力用于高校实训。

Microsoft Adopts Intel EMIB Packaging; Intel Says 18A Signs First External Customer

微软采用英特尔EMIB封装，英特尔称18A签下首家外部客户

11月19日 23:12重大可靠性 B进展

据《每日经济新闻》11月19日晚间报道，微软成为首家宣布在Azure自研芯片上采用英特尔代工EMIB封装的客户。英特尔与微软达成长期协议，确认多年期英特尔至强产能供应，其中包括代号Diamond Rapids的下一代服务器CPU，并扩大在Azure路线图及基于英特尔的Azure硬件系统上的合作。

英特尔首席执行官陈立武同日在得克萨斯州奥斯汀的TomorrowX峰会上称，英特尔已为18A芯片制造工艺签下首家外部客户，但没有透露客户名称。英特尔首席技术官Pushkar Ranade称，该客户签约采用18A-P制造工艺节点，该节点已于6月进入风险试产阶段。

According to a report by National Business Daily on the evening of November 19, Microsoft became the first customer to announce that it would use Intel Foundry's EMIB packaging in its self-developed chips for Azure. Intel and Microsoft reached a long-term agreement that confirms multi-year supply of Intel Xeon capacity, including the next-generation server CPU code-named Diamond Rapids, and expands cooperation on the Azure roadmap and on Intel-based Azure hardware systems.

据《每日经济新闻》11月19日晚间报道，微软成为首家宣布在Azure自研芯片上采用英特尔代工EMIB封装的客户。英特尔与微软达成长期协议，确认多年期英特尔至强产能供应，其中包括代号Diamond Rapids的下一代服务器CPU，并扩大在Azure路线图及基于英特尔的Azure硬件系统上的合作。

Intel CEO Lip-Bu Tan said the same day at the TomorrowX summit in Austin, Texas, that Intel had signed its first external customer for the 18A chip manufacturing process, but did not disclose the customer's name. Intel Chief Technology Officer Pushkar Ranade said the customer had signed to adopt the 18A-P manufacturing process node, which entered risk production in June.

英特尔首席执行官陈立武同日在得克萨斯州奥斯汀的TomorrowX峰会上称，英特尔已为18A芯片制造工艺签下首家外部客户，但没有透露客户名称。英特尔首席技术官Pushkar Ranade称，该客户签约采用18A-P制造工艺节点，该节点已于6月进入风险试产阶段。

NVIDIA and Microsoft Expand Accelerated Computing Partnership as Vera Rubin Comes to Azure

NVIDIA与微软扩大加速计算合作，Vera Rubin进入Azure

11月20日 04:00重要可靠性 A

NVIDIA在博客中称，与微软在Microsoft Ignite扩大全栈合作，NVIDIA Vera Rubin NVL72系统正在Azure数据中心运行，面向推理、代理式应用和大规模模型训练与推理。

NVIDIA称，微软使用NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell GPU加速Bing搜索模型，并通过Microsoft Foundry提供Nemotron 3.5系列开放模型和企业定制能力。

面向Windows PC，NVIDIA称将在Ignite展示RTX Spark设备上的本地大语言模型体验，并为GPU加速的Windows 365 Cloud PC提供Direct Stream能力，有限预览已开放报名，公开预览计划于2027年初。

NVIDIA said in a blog post that it expanded its full-stack collaboration with Microsoft at Microsoft Ignite, and that NVIDIA Vera Rubin NVL72 systems are running in Azure data centers for inference, agentic applications, and large-scale model training and inference.

NVIDIA在博客中称，与微软在Microsoft Ignite扩大全栈合作，NVIDIA Vera Rubin NVL72系统正在Azure数据中心运行，面向推理、代理式应用和大规模模型训练与推理。

NVIDIA said that Microsoft is using NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell GPUs to accelerate Bing search models and is offering the Nemotron 3.5 family of open models and enterprise customization capabilities through Microsoft Foundry.

NVIDIA称，微软使用NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell GPU加速Bing搜索模型，并通过Microsoft Foundry提供Nemotron 3.5系列开放模型和企业定制能力。

For Windows PCs, NVIDIA said it will showcase a local large language model experience on RTX Spark devices at Ignite and provide Direct Stream capabilities for GPU-accelerated Windows 365 Cloud PCs, with registration open for a limited preview and a public preview planned for early 2027.

面向Windows PC，NVIDIA称将在Ignite展示RTX Spark设备上的本地大语言模型体验，并为GPU加速的Windows 365 Cloud PC提供Direct Stream能力，有限预览已开放报名，公开预览计划于2027年初。
