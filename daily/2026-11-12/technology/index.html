DeepMind Releases AlphaGenome 2 to Predict How DNA Variants Affect Cell Types

DeepMind发布AlphaGenome 2，预测DNA变异对细胞类型的影响

11月11日 23:00重要可靠性 A

人体每种细胞携带相同的DNA蓝图，但不同细胞类型和状态会开启或关闭不同基因来完成各自功能。理解DNA变异如何影响这种基因表达，是连接基因组变异与疾病机制的关键问题。

Google DeepMind于11月11日发布AlphaGenome 2，称这是其下一代DNA序列模型，可预测DNA序列及其变异如何影响特定人类细胞类型。DeepMind表示，该模型试图连接单细胞生物学与人类遗传学，预测遗传变异对数百种人类细胞类型的影响，以帮助研究疾病的细胞驱动因素。上述能力来自DeepMind的官方发布说明。

Every cell in the human body carries the same DNA blueprint, but different cell types and states turn different genes on or off to perform their respective functions. Understanding how DNA variants affect this gene expression is a key question in linking genomic variation to disease mechanisms.

人体每种细胞携带相同的DNA蓝图，但不同细胞类型和状态会开启或关闭不同基因来完成各自功能。理解DNA变异如何影响这种基因表达，是连接基因组变异与疾病机制的关键问题。

Google DeepMind released AlphaGenome 2 on November 11, describing it as its next-generation DNA sequence model that can predict how DNA sequences and their variants affect specific human cell types. DeepMind said the model seeks to connect single-cell biology with human genetics, predicting the effects of genetic variants across hundreds of human cell types to help study the cellular drivers of disease. The capabilities described above come from DeepMind's official release notes.

Google DeepMind于11月11日发布AlphaGenome 2，称这是其下一代DNA序列模型，可预测DNA序列及其变异如何影响特定人类细胞类型。DeepMind表示，该模型试图连接单细胞生物学与人类遗传学，预测遗传变异对数百种人类细胞类型的影响，以帮助研究疾病的细胞驱动因素。上述能力来自DeepMind的官方发布说明。

China Successfully Launches Pathfinder Satellite for Tanyuan Program

中国成功发射探源计划探路者卫星

11月11日 20:06重大可靠性 B

据新华社报道，11月11日19时41分，中国在酒泉卫星发射中心使用长征二号丁运载火箭，将探源计划探路者卫星发射升空，卫星顺利进入预定轨道，发射任务取得圆满成功。这是长征系列运载火箭的第677次飞行。

据新浪科技转载的快科技报道，此次发射以一箭四星组合体方式进行，共研制A、B、C、D四颗卫星。A星搭载磁场与速度场成像仪、甚大面积伽马射线空间望远镜探路者、高分辨率大气边界层激光探测仪、朗缪尔探针、空间天气效应监测仪五类载荷；B、C、D三星以编队形式构成空间分布式低频干涉成像谱仪技术试验系统。探路者卫星是太空探源系列科学卫星计划的技术试验星，核心任务是对“鸿蒙计划”“夸父二号”等任务的有效载荷关键技术开展在轨验证，设计寿命2年，运行于轨道高度600公里的太阳同步晨昏轨道。

Xinhua reported that at 19:41 on November 11, China launched the Pathfinder satellite of the Tanyuan program from the Jiuquan Satellite Launch Center using a Long March 2D carrier rocket; the satellite successfully entered the planned orbit, and the launch mission was a complete success. This was the 677th flight of the Long March series carrier rockets.

据新华社报道，11月11日19时41分，中国在酒泉卫星发射中心使用长征二号丁运载火箭，将探源计划探路者卫星发射升空，卫星顺利进入预定轨道，发射任务取得圆满成功。这是长征系列运载火箭的第677次飞行。

According to a Kuai Technology report republished by Sina Tech, this launch was carried out in a one-rocket, four-satellite combination mode, with four satellites, A, B, C, and D, developed in total. Satellite A carries five types of payloads—a magnetic field and velocity field imager, the Very Large Area Gamma-ray Space Telescope Pathfinder, a high-resolution atmospheric boundary layer lidar, a Langmuir probe, and a space weather effects monitor; satellites B, C, and D form, in formation, a space-distributed low-frequency interferometric imaging spectrometer technology test system. The Pathfinder satellite is a technology test satellite of the Tanyuan space exploration series scientific satellite program; its core mission is to conduct in-orbit verification of key payload technologies for missions such as the Hongmeng Program and Kuafu-2; it has a design life of two years and operates in a Sun-synchronous dawn-dusk orbit at an altitude of 600 kilometers.

AMD Details Venice Architecture for 2nm Server Processors, Targets 2027 Mass Production

AMD披露2nm服务器处理器Venice架构与2027年量产目标

11月12日 09:39重要可靠性 B

据ChainCatcher 11月12日报道，AMD在In Depth技术论坛披露了服务器处理器与芯片互连布局：Venice采用台积电N2制程和CoWoS-L先进封装，并引入elevated fan-out bridge（EFB）封装互连技术；后续Verano将采用N2P制程。

AMD服务器架构负责人Mahesh Subramony称，随着计算核心增多，芯片间需要传输更多数据，EFB通过短距离互连降低功耗和延迟。产品进度方面，Venice的SP7平台已出货，面向企业市场的SP8预计2027年上半年供应，Verano与Venice-X计划同年下半年发布，相关服务器处理器目标2027年量产。

According to a November 12 ChainCatcher report, AMD disclosed its server processor and chip interconnect plans at its In Depth technology forum: Venice uses TSMC's N2 process and CoWoS-L advanced packaging and introduces elevated fan-out bridge (EFB) packaging interconnect technology; the subsequent Verano will use the N2P process.

据ChainCatcher 11月12日报道，AMD在In Depth技术论坛披露了服务器处理器与芯片互连布局：Venice采用台积电N2制程和CoWoS-L先进封装，并引入elevated fan-out bridge（EFB）封装互连技术；后续Verano将采用N2P制程。

Mahesh Subramony, who leads AMD's server architecture, said that as compute cores increase, chips need to transmit more data between them, and EFB reduces power consumption and latency through short-distance interconnects. On product progress, the SP7 platform for Venice has shipped; SP8 for the enterprise market is expected to be available in the first half of 2027; Verano and Venice-X are planned for release in the second half of the same year; and the related server processors target mass production in 2027.
