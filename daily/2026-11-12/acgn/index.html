Reality Drift Launches on Steam With Keita Takahashi Contributing to Level Design

《Reality Drift》在Steam发售，高桥庆太参与关卡设计

11月12日 11:15重要可靠性 B

Arganoid Industries于11月12日在Steam正式发售《Reality Drift》，售价2100日元，11月22日前15%优惠至1785日元。

本作是融入Roguelike要素的竞速游戏，玩家扮演配送员，在奇特世界与99台对手竞速，每段赛程后从三个选项中选择下一赛道和车辆强化。

《块魂》创作者高桥庆太以客座艺术家身份参与，设计了人体内部赛道以及巨大羊、鸽子等视觉。

Arganoid Industries officially released Reality Drift on Steam on November 12 at a price of 2,100 yen, with a 15% discount to 1,785 yen until November 22.

Arganoid Industries于11月12日在Steam正式发售《Reality Drift》，售价2100日元，11月22日前15%优惠至1785日元。

This is a racing game incorporating roguelike elements, in which players take on the role of a courier, racing against 99 opponents in a strange world and choosing the next track and a vehicle upgrade from three options after each race segment.

本作是融入Roguelike要素的竞速游戏，玩家扮演配送员，在奇特世界与99台对手竞速，每段赛程后从三个选项中选择下一赛道和车辆强化。

Keita Takahashi, creator of Katamari Damacy, participated as a guest artist, designing tracks set inside the human body and visuals including a giant sheep and a pigeon.

《块魂》创作者高桥庆太以客座艺术家身份参与，设计了人体内部赛道以及巨大羊、鸽子等视觉。

Former Doom Developers Form New Studio, Debut Game Danger Pay

前Doom开发者组建新工作室，首作《Danger Pay》

11月12日 02:08重要可靠性 B进展

据Rock Paper Shotgun报道，多名前id Software开发者在被微软裁员后成立新工作室，首款作品为受《Smash TV》影响的射击游戏《Danger Pay》，工作室称其不受投资者需求约束。

According to Rock Paper Shotgun, several former id Software developers have founded a new studio after being laid off by Microsoft, with its first title being Danger Pay, a shooter influenced by Smash TV, and the studio says it is not constrained by investor demands.

Crownfall Arrives on PS5 and Xbox as Realm of the Stone Crown DLC Launches Simultaneously

《王权陨落》登陆PS5与Xbox，DLC《岩冠之境》同步推出

11月12日 11:38重要可靠性 B

《王权陨落》于11月11日登陆PS5和Xbox Series X|S，并加入Xbox Game Pass；首个大型DLC《岩冠之境》同步登陆PC、PS5和Xbox Series X|S。

DLC让玩家扮演末代矮人王，深入8张新地图，新增掷斧兵、飞艇、步行坦克等单位和机械构造体敌人；售价35人民币、8.99英镑、9.99美元、9.99欧元，Switch版即将推出。

Crownfall launched on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S on November 11 and joined Xbox Game Pass; its first major DLC, Realm of the Stone Crown, launched simultaneously on PC, PS5, and Xbox Series X|S.

《王权陨落》于11月11日登陆PS5和Xbox Series X|S，并加入Xbox Game Pass；首个大型DLC《岩冠之境》同步登陆PC、PS5和Xbox Series X|S。

The DLC lets players take on the role of the last dwarven king and venture into eight new maps, adding units such as axe throwers, airships, and walking tanks as well as mechanical construct enemies; it is priced at CNY 35, GBP 8.99, USD 9.99, and EUR 9.99, with a Switch version coming soon.

DLC让玩家扮演末代矮人王，深入8张新地图，新增掷斧兵、飞艇、步行坦克等单位和机械构造体敌人；售价35人民币、8.99英镑、9.99美元、9.99欧元，Switch版即将推出。

来源：gcores.com
