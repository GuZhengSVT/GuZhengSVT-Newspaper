CPC Central Committee Issues Revised Regulations on Work of Local Party Committees

中共中央印发修订后的《中国共产党地方委员会工作条例》

11月15日 16:56重大可靠性 A

新华社11月15日报道，中共中央近日印发修订后的《中国共产党地方委员会工作条例》并发出通知，要求各地区各部门遵照执行。条例注明经2026年3月27日中共中央政治局会议修订，2026年11月7日由中共中央发布。

条例规定，地方党委在本地区发挥总揽全局、协调各方的作用，履行把方向、管大局、作决策、保落实职责；常委会委员名额省级为11至13人，市、县两级为9至11人，设书记1名、副书记2名。

在议事规则上，条例要求全会每年至少召开2次，常委会会议一般每月召开2次；党委书记不得凌驾于组织之上、班子之上，不得搞“一言堂”“家长制”，并应在常委会会议上执行末位表态制。

Xinhua reported on November 15 that the CPC Central Committee recently issued the revised Regulations on the Work of Local Committees of the Communist Party of China and a notice requiring all regions and departments to comply. The regulations note that they were revised at a meeting of the Political Bureau of the CPC Central Committee on March 27, 2026, and issued by the CPC Central Committee on November 7, 2026.

新华社11月15日报道，中共中央近日印发修订后的《中国共产党地方委员会工作条例》并发出通知，要求各地区各部门遵照执行。条例注明经2026年3月27日中共中央政治局会议修订，2026年11月7日由中共中央发布。

The regulations stipulate that local Party committees shall play a role in taking overall charge and coordinating all parties in their regions and shall perform the duties of setting direction, managing the overall situation, making decisions, and ensuring implementation; the number of members of standing committees shall be 11 to 13 at the provincial level and 9 to 11 at the municipal and county levels, with one secretary and two deputy secretaries.

条例规定，地方党委在本地区发挥总揽全局、协调各方的作用，履行把方向、管大局、作决策、保落实职责；常委会委员名额省级为11至13人，市、县两级为9至11人，设书记1名、副书记2名。

On rules of procedure, the regulations require that the plenary session be held at least twice a year and that Standing Committee meetings generally be held twice a month; Party committee secretaries must not place themselves above the organization or the leadership team, must not engage in "one-person decision-making" or "patriarchal rule," and should follow the practice of speaking last at Standing Committee meetings.

在议事规则上，条例要求全会每年至少召开2次，常委会会议一般每月召开2次；党委书记不得凌驾于组织之上、班子之上，不得搞“一言堂”“家长制”，并应在常委会会议上执行末位表态制。

According to the BBC, Estonia announced that it will close all its border crossings with Russia starting December 1. Australia, Canada, Italy and UK foreign ministers jointly call on Israel to allow Gaza aid through

澳加意英外长联合呼吁以色列放行加沙援助

11月16日 03:03重要可靠性 A

英国政府网站11月15日发布澳大利亚、加拿大、意大利和英国外长的联合声明，称随着冬季到来，加沙人道局势严峻，近日洪水带来更多苦难，当地民众急需住所和人道援助。

声明称，15万顶帐篷及其他重要物资滞留在边境，被阻止进入加沙。四国外长敦促以色列政府立即停止拖延，开放过境点，允许人道物资立即、快速、无阻通行，并放行人道行动和早期恢复所需的两用物资。

声明还呼吁各方落实加沙综合方案，遵守停火，并履行国际人道法义务。

The UK government website published a joint statement on November 15 by the foreign ministers of Australia, Canada, Italy and the United Kingdom, saying that with winter approaching, the humanitarian situation in Gaza is severe, recent floods have brought further suffering, and people there urgently need shelter and humanitarian aid.

英国政府网站11月15日发布澳大利亚、加拿大、意大利和英国外长的联合声明，称随着冬季到来，加沙人道局势严峻，近日洪水带来更多苦难，当地民众急需住所和人道援助。

The statement said that 150,000 tents and other important supplies were stranded at the border and blocked from entering Gaza. The four foreign ministers urged the Israeli government to immediately stop delaying, open crossing points, allow humanitarian supplies to pass immediately, rapidly and without obstruction, and allow through dual-use materials needed for humanitarian operations and early recovery.

声明称，15万顶帐篷及其他重要物资滞留在边境，被阻止进入加沙。四国外长敦促以色列政府立即停止拖延，开放过境点，允许人道物资立即、快速、无阻通行，并放行人道行动和早期恢复所需的两用物资。

The statement also called on all parties to implement the comprehensive plan for Gaza, observe the ceasefire, and fulfill their obligations under international humanitarian law.

声明还呼吁各方落实加沙综合方案，遵守停火，并履行国际人道法义务。
