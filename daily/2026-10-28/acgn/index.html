Where Winds Meet Reveals Hutuo Update, Launching November 13

《Where Winds Meet》公布Hutuo版本，11月13日上线

10月27日 23:01重大可靠性 A进展

PlayStation Blog于10月27日刊出《Where Winds Meet》发行负责人Eric Zheng的文章，宣布游戏将于11月13日上线新区域“Hutuo”，作为首个全球周年更新的一部分。官方称这是作品迄今最关键的叙事篇章。

Hutuo以战乱边境为舞台，围绕中都桥之战展开：北方入侵者越境、前线防线崩溃，玩家要在效忠崩坏王朝与保护平民之间作出选择。

PS5版将获得针对PS5 Pro的增强PSSR和升级的光线追踪反射，官方称湿地与河流场景可在60 FPS下运行；DualSense自适应扳机与触觉反馈用于强化拉弓和挥刃的手感。

周年更新还包含奖励以及赛马棋、江湖战术等棋盘玩法；官方将于11月2日举行周年特别直播，11月下旬上线由袁和平指导的双矛武学。

On October 27, the PlayStation Blog published an article by Eric Zheng, publishing lead for Where Winds Meet, announcing that the game will launch its new region “Hutuo” on November 13 as part of its first global anniversary update. The official announcement calls this the game’s most pivotal narrative chapter to date.

PlayStation Blog于10月27日刊出《Where Winds Meet》发行负责人Eric Zheng的文章，宣布游戏将于11月13日上线新区域“Hutuo”，作为首个全球周年更新的一部分。官方称这是作品迄今最关键的叙事篇章。

Set on a war-torn border, Hutuo revolves around the Battle of Zhongdu Bridge: northern invaders cross the border and the frontline collapses, leaving players to choose between loyalty to a collapsing dynasty and protecting civilians.

Hutuo以战乱边境为舞台，围绕中都桥之战展开：北方入侵者越境、前线防线崩溃，玩家要在效忠崩坏王朝与保护平民之间作出选择。

The PS5 version will receive enhanced PSSR for PS5 Pro and upgraded ray-traced reflections, with the official announcement saying wetland and river scenes can run at 60 FPS; DualSense adaptive triggers and haptic feedback will enhance the feel of drawing a bow and swinging a blade.

PS5版将获得针对PS5 Pro的增强PSSR和升级的光线追踪反射，官方称湿地与河流场景可在60 FPS下运行；DualSense自适应扳机与触觉反馈用于强化拉弓和挥刃的手感。

The anniversary update will also include rewards and board game modes such as horse-racing chess and Jianghu Tactics; an official special anniversary livestream is scheduled for November 2, and a dual-spear martial art choreographed by Yuen Woo-ping will launch in late November.

周年更新还包含奖励以及赛马棋、江湖战术等棋盘玩法；官方将于11月2日举行周年特别直播，11月下旬上线由袁和平指导的双矛武学。

来源：blog.playstation.com

Aloft to Make Console Debut on PS5 in Early 2027 With Full 1.0 Release

《Aloft》将于2027年初以1.0完整版首登PS5

10月28日 00:00重要可靠性 A进展

Funcom通信经理Jarle Hrafn Grindhaug在PlayStation Blog宣布，云中合作沙盒生存游戏《Aloft》将于2027年初首次登陆主机，由Funcom发行PS5版。

开发团队Astrolabe由一群朋友发展而来，历时六年制作。PS5版包含PC抢先体验期间的全部更新和1.0完整内容，玩家可独自或与朋友把浮岛改造成空中船、在云间建家。

官方表示，两款游戏的具体发售日期和更多细节将在之后公布。

Funcom communications manager Jarle Hrafn Grindhaug announced on the PlayStation Blog that Aloft, a cooperative cloud-based sandbox survival game, will make its console debut in early 2027, with Funcom publishing the PS5 version.

Funcom通信经理Jarle Hrafn Grindhaug在PlayStation Blog宣布，云中合作沙盒生存游戏《Aloft》将于2027年初首次登陆主机，由Funcom发行PS5版。

The development team Astrolabe grew out of a group of friends and has spent six years making the game. The PS5 version will include all updates from the PC early access period and the full 1.0 content, and players can transform floating islands into airships and build homes among the clouds, either alone or with friends.

开发团队Astrolabe由一群朋友发展而来，历时六年制作。PS5版包含PC抢先体验期间的全部更新和1.0完整内容，玩家可独自或与朋友把浮岛改造成空中船、在云间建家。

The team said that specific release dates and more details for the two games will be announced later.

官方表示，两款游戏的具体发售日期和更多细节将在之后公布。

Conan Exiles Enhanced to Launch on PS5 in Early 2027, With Free Upgrade for PS4 Owners

《Conan Exiles Enhanced》2027年初登陆PS5，PS4版可免费升级

10月28日 00:00重要可靠性 A进展

PlayStation Blog宣布，《Conan Exiles Enhanced》将于2027年初登陆PS5，具体发售日期后续公布。

拥有PS4版《Conan Exiles》的玩家可免费获得PS5增强版；PS4与PS5版买一得二，已购Bazaar物品和DLC可从PS4继承。

增强版改进环境、角色、光照和特效，重做UI并提升性能。主机版发售时包含“Legacy of the Giant-Kings”新故事线，Isle of Siptah仍为单独购买。

PlayStation Blog announced that Conan Exiles Enhanced will launch on PS5 in early 2027, with the specific release date to be announced later.

PlayStation Blog宣布，《Conan Exiles Enhanced》将于2027年初登陆PS5，具体发售日期后续公布。

Players who own the PS4 version of Conan Exiles can receive the PS5 Enhanced version for free; the PS4 and PS5 versions are offered on a buy-one-get-both basis, and purchased Bazaar items and DLC can be carried over from PS4.

拥有PS4版《Conan Exiles》的玩家可免费获得PS5增强版；PS4与PS5版买一得二，已购Bazaar物品和DLC可从PS4继承。

The Enhanced version improves environments, characters, lighting, and effects, reworks the UI, and boosts performance. The console version will include the new "Legacy of the Giant-Kings" storyline at launch, while Isle of Siptah remains a separate purchase.

增强版改进环境、角色、光照和特效，重做UI并提升性能。主机版发售时包含“Legacy of the Giant-Kings”新故事线，Isle of Siptah仍为单独购买。

Terrible Lizards Set for October 29 Release

《Terrible Lizards》定于10月29日发售

10月27日 22:00重要可靠性 A进展

PlayStation Blog于10月27日刊出WDR Studios工作室总监John W. Gelardi的文章，宣布第一人称3D平台跑酷恐怖游戏《Terrible Lizards》将于10月29日发售。

游戏设定在1990年代初，玩家扮演一名沿66号公路旅行的大学生，汽车抛锚后进入名为Dalton’s Dinos的恐龙主题路边景点，却遭遇可怕事物。

WDR Studios表示，团队多数成员是首次制作游戏、此前从事网络内容创作；游戏强调奔跑、跳跃、滑铲、蹬墙和抓边等移动技巧。

On October 27, PlayStation Blog published an article by John W. Gelardi, studio director at WDR Studios, announcing that the first-person 3D platforming parkour horror game Terrible Lizards will be released on October 29.

PlayStation Blog于10月27日刊出WDR Studios工作室总监John W. Gelardi的文章，宣布第一人称3D平台跑酷恐怖游戏《Terrible Lizards》将于10月29日发售。

Set in the early 1990s, the game follows a college student traveling along Route 66 who, after their car breaks down, enters a dinosaur-themed roadside attraction called Dalton’s Dinos and encounters something terrifying.

游戏设定在1990年代初，玩家扮演一名沿66号公路旅行的大学生，汽车抛锚后进入名为Dalton’s Dinos的恐龙主题路边景点，却遭遇可怕事物。

WDR Studios said that most team members are making a game for the first time and previously worked in online content creation; the game emphasizes movement techniques such as running, jumping, sliding, wall kicking, and ledge grabbing.

WDR Studios表示，团队多数成员是首次制作游戏、此前从事网络内容创作；游戏强调奔跑、跳跃、滑铲、蹬墙和抓边等移动技巧。

来源：blog.playstation.com
