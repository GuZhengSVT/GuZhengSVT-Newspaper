DeepMind Releases AlphaProof Code, an AI Agent Framework for Formal Verification of Signal Protocol Code

DeepMind 发布 AlphaProof Code，AI 智能体形式验证 Signal 协议代码

11月10日 20:00重要可靠性 A

Google DeepMind 11月10日发布 AlphaProof Code，一个用于深度形式验证的智能体框架。DeepMind 称，这是首次用 AI 对广泛部署的密码协议 Signal 的实际代码进行深度形式验证，也是该协议实现首次被形式验证，无论是否使用 AI。

该框架将形式验证工作拆分给人类专家与 AI 智能体：人类与 AI 共同确定需要证明的安全属性，由 Gemini Carbon 驱动的智能体生成超过六百万行 Lean 证明。DeepMind 称形式验证是最高级别的安全保证，但通常困难且耗时；规范由人类专家逐条审查，智能体生成的证明也会经过审核，标记需要人工复查的部分。

Google DeepMind released AlphaProof Code on November 10, an agent framework for deep formal verification. DeepMind said this was the first time AI had been used for deep formal verification of the actual code of the widely deployed cryptographic protocol Signal, and the first time the protocol's implementation had been formally verified, whether or not AI was used.

Google DeepMind 11月10日发布 AlphaProof Code，一个用于深度形式验证的智能体框架。DeepMind 称，这是首次用 AI 对广泛部署的密码协议 Signal 的实际代码进行深度形式验证，也是该协议实现首次被形式验证，无论是否使用 AI。

The framework divides formal verification work between human experts and AI agents: humans and AI jointly identify the security properties that need to be proved, and agents powered by Gemini Carbon generate more than six million lines of Lean proofs. DeepMind said formal verification is the highest level of security assurance but is usually difficult and time-consuming; the specification was reviewed item by item by human experts, and the proofs generated by the agents were also reviewed, with parts requiring manual re-examination flagged.

该框架将形式验证工作拆分给人类专家与 AI 智能体：人类与 AI 共同确定需要证明的安全属性，由 Gemini Carbon 驱动的智能体生成超过六百万行 Lean 证明。DeepMind 称形式验证是最高级别的安全保证，但通常困难且耗时；规范由人类专家逐条审查，智能体生成的证明也会经过审核，标记需要人工复查的部分。

MiniMax Open-Sources H3-Image, Claiming 10-Second 4K Image Generation

MiniMax 开源 H3-Image，宣称 10 秒生成 4K 图像

11月10日 23:00重要可靠性 A

MiniMax 11月10日发布图像生成模型 H3-Image 并开放权重。该模型衍生自 H3，支持文生图、图像参考和图像编辑，官方称可在10秒内生成4096×4096像素（约16MP，4K）图像。

MiniMax 称其速度可达市面主流水平的5倍，支持最多16张参考图片，并强调指令遵循能力。除 Hugging Face 开放权重外，该模型也通过 MiniMax 开放平台、MiniMax Code 和海螺提供。

MiniMax 称标准模式生成速度为市面主流工具的1.5至2倍，Highspeed 模式为3至4倍，价格为主流同档位的50%至70%，发布首周4K及以上请求五折。

MiniMax released the image-generation model H3-Image on November 10 and opened its weights. Derived from H3, the model supports text-to-image, image reference and image editing, and MiniMax says it can generate 4096×4096-pixel (about 16MP, 4K) images within 10 seconds.

MiniMax 11月10日发布图像生成模型 H3-Image 并开放权重。该模型衍生自 H3，支持文生图、图像参考和图像编辑，官方称可在10秒内生成4096×4096像素（约16MP，4K）图像。

MiniMax says its speed can reach five times the mainstream level on the market, that it supports up to 16 reference images, and highlights its instruction-following capability. In addition to open weights on Hugging Face, the model is also available through the MiniMax open platform, MiniMax Code and Hailuo.

MiniMax 称其速度可达市面主流水平的5倍，支持最多16张参考图片，并强调指令遵循能力。除 Hugging Face 开放权重外，该模型也通过 MiniMax 开放平台、MiniMax Code 和海螺提供。

MiniMax says Standard mode generates images 1.5 to 2 times as fast as mainstream tools, Highspeed mode 3 to 4 times as fast, with prices at 50% to 70% of mainstream tools in the same tier, and a 50% discount on 4K and above requests in the first week after launch.

MiniMax 称标准模式生成速度为市面主流工具的1.5至2倍，Highspeed 模式为3至4倍，价格为主流同档位的50%至70%，发布首周4K及以上请求五折。

Ant Lingbo Open-Sources Tactile Foundation Model LingBot-Touch

蚂蚁灵波开源触觉基础模型 LingBot-Touch

11月10日 15:57重要可靠性 B

据量子位获授权转载的蚂蚁灵波文章，蚂蚁灵波科技11月10日开源触觉基础模型 LingBot-Touch。该模型基于近5000小时真实触觉数据预训练，覆盖磁触觉、视触觉和压阻式三类传感原理，可将不同传感器信号转化为统一触觉表征，并已接入 LingBot-VLA。

文章称，在26项真机测试中，接入 LingBot-Touch 后 LingBot-VLA、π0.5、Diffusion Policy 和 T-Rex 四种动作模型的平均任务成功率较无触觉时提高约14至26个百分点；T-Rex 控制 Sharpa Wave 拧瓶盖的成功率从56.25%升至90%。

模型提供约530万参数的 Tiny 版和更大的 Small 版，技术报告、模型和代码已在 Hugging Face、GitHub 和魔搭社区开源。

According to an Ant Lingbo article that QbitAI is authorized to republish, Ant Lingbo Technology open-sourced the tactile foundation model LingBot-Touch on November 10. Pre-trained on nearly 5,000 hours of real tactile data, the model covers three sensing principles—magnetic tactile, vision-based tactile, and piezoresistive—and can convert signals from different sensors into a unified tactile representation; it has already been integrated into LingBot-VLA.

据量子位获授权转载的蚂蚁灵波文章，蚂蚁灵波科技11月10日开源触觉基础模型 LingBot-Touch。该模型基于近5000小时真实触觉数据预训练，覆盖磁触觉、视触觉和压阻式三类传感原理，可将不同传感器信号转化为统一触觉表征，并已接入 LingBot-VLA。

According to the article, in 26 real-robot tests, after integrating LingBot-Touch, the average task success rates of the four action models LingBot-VLA, π0.5, Diffusion Policy, and T-Rex increased by about 14 to 26 percentage points compared with no tactile sensing; T-Rex's success rate in controlling Sharpa Wave to twist bottle caps rose from 56.25% to 90%.

文章称，在26项真机测试中，接入 LingBot-Touch 后 LingBot-VLA、π0.5、Diffusion Policy 和 T-Rex 四种动作模型的平均任务成功率较无触觉时提高约14至26个百分点；T-Rex 控制 Sharpa Wave 拧瓶盖的成功率从56.25%升至90%。

The model is available in a Tiny version with about 5.3 million parameters and a larger Small version, and its technical report, models, and code have been open-sourced on Hugging Face, GitHub, and the ModelScope community.

模型提供约530万参数的 Tiny 版和更大的 Small 版，技术报告、模型和代码已在 Hugging Face、GitHub 和魔搭社区开源。
