GTA VI PS5 Preloads Open, With Rolling Country-by-Country Unlock on November 19

《GTA VI》PS5预载开启 11月19日按国家滚动解锁

11月10日 23:41重大可靠性 A

索尼互动娱乐11月10日宣布，《Grand Theft Auto VI》PS5版预下载已在PlayStation Store开启，玩家可在11月19日发售前提前九天装载。

索尼称本作已成为PlayStation Store史上预购最多的游戏，发售将采用按国家滚动午夜解锁，从新西兰11月19日0点开始。

索尼称PS5版支持DualSense扬声器、触觉反馈和自适应扳机，并使用Tempest 3D音频与高速SSD；PS5 Pro版提供PSSR和光追阴影增强。

Sony Interactive Entertainment announced on November 10 that pre-downloads for the PS5 version of Grand Theft Auto VI have opened on the PlayStation Store, allowing players to preload it nine days ahead of the November 19 release.

索尼互动娱乐11月10日宣布，《Grand Theft Auto VI》PS5版预下载已在PlayStation Store开启，玩家可在11月19日发售前提前九天装载。

Sony said the title has become the most pre-ordered game in PlayStation Store history, and the release will use rolling midnight unlocks by country, starting with New Zealand at 12:00 a.m. on November 19.

索尼称本作已成为PlayStation Store史上预购最多的游戏，发售将采用按国家滚动午夜解锁，从新西兰11月19日0点开始。

Sony said the PS5 version supports the DualSense speaker, haptic feedback and adaptive triggers, and uses Tempest 3D Audio and a high-speed SSD; the PS5 Pro version offers PSSR and enhanced ray-traced shadows.

First Story DLC for The Outer Worlds 2 Set for December 2

《The Outer Worlds 2》首个剧情DLC定档12月2日

11月11日 02:00重要可靠性 A

据PlayStation Blog与Xbox Wire 11月10日发布的消息，黑曜石娱乐《The Outer Worlds 2》首个剧情DLC《Side Effects Guaranteed》将于12月2日推出，是两部分故事的第一部分，第二部分计划2027年春。

DLC新增Elysium Valley地区，等级上限从30提高到36，加入15个新Perk、4个新缺陷、5件新武器和20首新歌的Sub Rosa电台。

DLC还提供27级预设角色，玩家可从主菜单直接开始该DLC；同步更新将允许玩家随时重置角色，并可在自定义选项调整每级获得的技能点；DLC包含在Premium Edition或Premium Edition升级包中。

According to posts published on November 10 by PlayStation Blog and Xbox Wire, Obsidian Entertainment's first story DLC for The Outer Worlds 2, Side Effects Guaranteed, will launch on December 2 as the first part of a two-part story, with the second part planned for spring 2027.

据PlayStation Blog与Xbox Wire 11月10日发布的消息，黑曜石娱乐《The Outer Worlds 2》首个剧情DLC《Side Effects Guaranteed》将于12月2日推出，是两部分故事的第一部分，第二部分计划2027年春。

The DLC adds the Elysium Valley region, raises the level cap from 30 to 36, and adds 15 new Perks, 4 new defects, 5 new weapons, and a Sub Rosa radio station featuring 20 new songs.

DLC新增Elysium Valley地区，等级上限从30提高到36，加入15个新Perk、4个新缺陷、5件新武器和20首新歌的Sub Rosa电台。

The DLC also offers a level 27 preset character, allowing players to start the DLC directly from the main menu; a simultaneous update will allow players to reset their characters at any time and adjust the skill points gained per level in the customization options; the DLC is included in the Premium Edition or Premium Edition upgrade pack.

DLC还提供27级预设角色，玩家可从主菜单直接开始该DLC；同步更新将允许玩家随时重置角色，并可在自定义选项调整每级获得的技能点；DLC包含在Premium Edition或Premium Edition升级包中。

19 IGF Judges Refuse to Vote for Generative AI Games; Big Walk Responds

19名IGF评委拒绝为生成式AI游戏投票 《Big Walk》回应

11月11日 09:25重要可靠性 B

据机核报道，包括Sam Barlow与Austin Walker在内的19名2027年独立游戏节IGF评委发表联合声明，拒绝为任何使用生成式AI制作的游戏投票，并呼吁IGF制定严格政策，全面禁止含AI生成内容的作品参选。 评委称决定是为声援担忧行业前景的创作者，认为生成式AI在生态、经济、认知及劳动权益等方面造成负面影响，并称有其他评委因职业风险未联署。

House House确认《Big Walk》项目后期曾用LLM解决性能问题，但称仅辅助技术排查，AI生成内容占游戏总代码不到0.1%，其他创作环节未使用；团队承认未建立内部规范，V社豁免AI辅助编码披露，商店页面没有相关说明。 机核报道称，鉴于评委决定抵制任何有AI参与的游戏，《Big Walk》很可能无缘IGF。

According to a report by Gcores, 19 judges for the 2027 Independent Games Festival (IGF), including Sam Barlow and Austin Walker, issued a joint statement refusing to vote for any game made using generative AI and calling on the IGF to formulate a strict policy that fully bans works containing AI-generated content from entry. The judges said the decision was made to support creators worried about the industry's future, argued that generative AI has caused negative impacts in areas including the ecosystem, the economy, cognition and labor rights, and said other judges did not sign the statement due to professional risks.

据机核报道，包括Sam Barlow与Austin Walker在内的19名2027年独立游戏节IGF评委发表联合声明，拒绝为任何使用生成式AI制作的游戏投票，并呼吁IGF制定严格政策，全面禁止含AI生成内容的作品参选。 评委称决定是为声援担忧行业前景的创作者，认为生成式AI在生态、经济、认知及劳动权益等方面造成负面影响，并称有其他评委因职业风险未联署。

House House confirmed that the Big Walk project used an LLM in its later stages to address performance issues, but said it was only used to assist technical troubleshooting, that AI-generated content accounted for less than 0.1% of the game's total code and was not used in other creative stages; the team acknowledged it had not established internal guidelines, that Valve exempts AI-assisted coding from disclosure, and that the store page has no relevant explanation. Gcores reported that, in light of the judges' decision to boycott any game involving AI, Big Walk is likely to miss out on the IGF.

House House确认《Big Walk》项目后期曾用LLM解决性能问题，但称仅辅助技术排查，AI生成内容占游戏总代码不到0.1%，其他创作环节未使用；团队承认未建立内部规范，V社豁免AI辅助编码披露，商店页面没有相关说明。 机核报道称，鉴于评委决定抵制任何有AI参与的游戏，《Big Walk》很可能无缘IGF。

来源：gcores.com
