Mistral and NVIDIA Partner to Advance Engineering-Simulation AI, Plan Public Benchmark

Mistral与NVIDIA合作推进工程仿真AI，并计划发布公开基准

10月21日 20:00重要可靠性 A进展

Mistral 10月21日宣布与NVIDIA合作开发面向工程领域的AI原生工作流，把Mistral Large 4与数据生成、物理建模流程结合，在CUDA-X库和Warp上加速仿真，并连接仿真、模型开发与工程设计。

合作还包括把Mistral模型接入Omniverse库，用于GPU加速3D场景生成和SimReady资产制作。双方称将建立公开基准和开放数据集，评测模型调用工程工具、完成多步流程和产出技术正确结果的能力。

Mistral称Large 4已公开预览，总参数约1万亿、激活参数490亿，在欧洲自有数据中心用3800块Grace Blackwell GPU训练，权重计划10月底发布。基准、数据集与工程效果均为公布的计划或初步成果，尚待独立验证。

On October 21, Mistral announced a partnership with NVIDIA to develop AI-native workflows for engineering, combining Mistral Large 4 with data generation and physical modeling processes, accelerating simulation on CUDA-X libraries and Warp, and connecting simulation, model development, and engineering design.

Mistral 10月21日宣布与NVIDIA合作开发面向工程领域的AI原生工作流，把Mistral Large 4与数据生成、物理建模流程结合，在CUDA-X库和Warp上加速仿真，并连接仿真、模型开发与工程设计。

The partnership also includes integrating Mistral models into the Omniverse libraries for GPU-accelerated 3D scene generation and SimReady asset creation. The two sides said they will establish a public benchmark and open datasets to evaluate models' ability to invoke engineering tools, complete multi-step processes, and produce technically correct results.

合作还包括把Mistral模型接入Omniverse库，用于GPU加速3D场景生成和SimReady资产制作。双方称将建立公开基准和开放数据集，评测模型调用工程工具、完成多步流程和产出技术正确结果的能力。

Mistral said Large 4 is already in public preview, with about 1 trillion total parameters and 49 billion active parameters, trained on 3,800 Grace Blackwell GPUs in its own data centers in Europe, with weights planned for release at the end of October. The benchmark, datasets, and engineering results are all announced plans or preliminary results and await independent verification.

NVIDIA Releases Kumo Relational, a Foundational Model for Relational Data, Claiming Prediction Without Training

NVIDIA发布关系数据基础模型Kumo Relational，称无需训练即可预测

10月22日 02:26重要可靠性 B进展

NVIDIA研究团队10月21日在Hugging Face博客发布Kumo Relational，称这是首个在常见关系数据基准上超过监督机器学习的少样本基础模型。用户连接数据库、用预测查询语言描述要预测的目标（如流失、需求或欺诈），模型读取最多1万个历史样本作为上下文，在一次前向计算中给出结果，不需要任务训练、特征工程或把多表压平成单表。

团队称，在15个数据库的41项任务上，该模型优于监督式关系深度学习模型和经专家特征工程的梯度提升树流程；在SAP SALT企业基准上，其平均倒数排名（MRR）比AutoGluon集成高8%。在RelBench V1分类任务上，其AUROC平均比专家特征梯度提升树高近4分。模型可扩展到5000亿行以上的数据库。

这些性能数字来自发布方的基准评测，博客同时列出论文编号与文档入口；模型预训练使用合成关系数据，推理时依赖上下文样本，因此实际企业数据上的效果仍需独立复现。

NVIDIA's research team released Kumo Relational on the Hugging Face blog on October 21, calling it the first few-shot foundational model to outperform supervised machine learning on common relational data benchmarks. Users connect a database and describe the prediction target (such as churn, demand, or fraud) in a predictive query language; the model reads up to 10,000 historical samples as context and produces results in a single forward computation, without task-specific training, feature engineering, or flattening multiple tables into one.

NVIDIA研究团队10月21日在Hugging Face博客发布Kumo Relational，称这是首个在常见关系数据基准上超过监督机器学习的少样本基础模型。用户连接数据库、用预测查询语言描述要预测的目标（如流失、需求或欺诈），模型读取最多1万个历史样本作为上下文，在一次前向计算中给出结果，不需要任务训练、特征工程或把多表压平成单表。

The team says that across 41 tasks on 15 databases, the model outperforms supervised relational deep learning models and gradient-boosted tree pipelines with expert feature engineering; on the SAP SALT enterprise benchmark, its mean reciprocal rank (MRR) is 8% higher than that of the AutoGluon ensemble. On RelBench V1 classification tasks, its AUROC is on average nearly 4 points higher than that of expert-feature gradient-boosted trees. The model can scale to databases with more than 500 billion rows.

团队称，在15个数据库的41项任务上，该模型优于监督式关系深度学习模型和经专家特征工程的梯度提升树流程；在SAP SALT企业基准上，其平均倒数排名（MRR）比AutoGluon集成高8%。在RelBench V1分类任务上，其AUROC平均比专家特征梯度提升树高近4分。模型可扩展到5000亿行以上的数据库。

These performance figures come from the publisher's own benchmark evaluation, and the blog also lists the paper number and documentation entry point; the model is pretrained on synthetic relational data and relies on in-context samples at inference, so its effectiveness on real enterprise data still requires independent reproduction.

这些性能数字来自发布方的基准评测，博客同时列出论文编号与文档入口；模型预训练使用合成关系数据，推理时依赖上下文样本，因此实际企业数据上的效果仍需独立复现。

Steel Structure Collapse at TSMC's Kaohsiung F22 Plant P4 Site Kills 4, Halts Steel Work Across Taiwan

台积电高雄F22厂P4工地钢构坠落致4人死亡，全台钢构作业暂停

10月21日 16:37重大可靠性 B进展

据朝鲜商业网10月21日转述台湾中央社报道，10月20日下午，台积电高雄楠梓科学园区F22厂第四期（P4）工地发生钢构坍塌。当时一家合作商的4名工人在建筑三层吊装钢构，钢构与人员一同坠落，3人当天死亡，最后1名伤者于21日上午在重症监护室去世，死亡人数增至4人。

南部科学园区管理局初步判断，三层外墙钢构连接板的固定螺栓断裂，导致结构和工人一同坠落，正在调查螺栓老化或数量不足的可能。4名工人都系有安全绳，但也有说法指安全锚点固定在坠落的钢构上，令防护装置失效，确切原因仍在调查。

台积电称事故后立即停止高雄工地相关作业，并自主暂停全台在建新厂的同类钢构作业，正与相关部门和施工承包商调查原因、准备后续安全措施。园区管理局已对P4工地钢构吊装作业下达停工令，承包商须提交改善计划并通过政府审查才能复工。事故涉及的是建设中的厂区，台积电未宣布停产现有生产线，对其2纳米扩产进度的影响尚不明确。

According to Taiwan's Central News Agency as relayed by Choson Business Network on October 21, a steel structure collapsed on the afternoon of October 20 at the Phase 4 (P4) construction site of TSMC's F22 plant in the Nanzih Science Park in Kaohsiung. At the time, four workers from a contractor were hoisting steel structures on the third floor of the building when the steel and the workers fell together; three died that day, and the last injured worker died in intensive care on the morning of October 21, bringing the death toll to four.

据朝鲜商业网10月21日转述台湾中央社报道，10月20日下午，台积电高雄楠梓科学园区F22厂第四期（P4）工地发生钢构坍塌。当时一家合作商的4名工人在建筑三层吊装钢构，钢构与人员一同坠落，3人当天死亡，最后1名伤者于21日上午在重症监护室去世，死亡人数增至4人。

The Southern Taiwan Science Park Bureau preliminarily determined that fixing bolts on the connection plates of the third-floor exterior wall steel structure had broken, causing the structure and the workers to fall together, and it is investigating whether the bolts were aged or insufficient in number. All four workers were wearing safety ropes, but some accounts say the safety anchor points were attached to the steel structure that fell, rendering the protective devices ineffective, and the exact cause is still under investigation.

南部科学园区管理局初步判断，三层外墙钢构连接板的固定螺栓断裂，导致结构和工人一同坠落，正在调查螺栓老化或数量不足的可能。4名工人都系有安全绳，但也有说法指安全锚点固定在坠落的钢构上，令防护装置失效，确切原因仍在调查。

TSMC said it immediately stopped related work at the Kaohsiung site after the accident and voluntarily suspended similar steel structure work at new plants under construction across Taiwan, and that it is working with relevant authorities and the construction contractor to investigate the cause and prepare follow-up safety measures. The park bureau has issued a work stoppage order for steel structure hoisting at the P4 site, and the contractor must submit an improvement plan and pass a government review before resuming work. The accident involved a plant area under construction; TSMC has not announced a halt to production at existing lines, and the impact on its 2-nanometer capacity expansion schedule remains unclear.

台积电称事故后立即停止高雄工地相关作业，并自主暂停全台在建新厂的同类钢构作业，正与相关部门和施工承包商调查原因、准备后续安全措施。园区管理局已对P4工地钢构吊装作业下达停工令，承包商须提交改善计划并通过政府审查才能复工。事故涉及的是建设中的厂区，台积电未宣布停产现有生产线，对其2纳米扩产进度的影响尚不明确。

NVIDIA Partners with Six Supply Chain Companies to Expand Production, Aiming to Increase Product Substrate Capacity 2.5-Fold in Three Years

NVIDIA联合六家供应链伙伴扩产，目标三年基板产能增2.5倍

10月21日 18:33重要可靠性 A进展

NVIDIA 10月21日在柏林GTC宣布与Amkor、IBIDEN、京元电子、三星电机、矽品和欣兴合作扩产。公司计划三年内把产品基板产能提升2.5倍：三星电机加入基板供应链，欣兴台湾扩产预计2027年第二季度贡献产能，IBIDEN大野工厂扩产预计2028年第一季度爬坡。

封装测试方面，NVIDIA与Amkor计划到2028年底把韩国中介层晶圆产能提升逾300%；NVIDIA预计半导体测试总产能2026年至2027年翻倍。公司向京元电子提供11亿美元产能预订预付款，支持其在美国新建测试设施，目标2027年4月量产；矽品计划到2027年中把共封装光学光电引擎产能提升15倍，到2028年底再翻一倍。

黄仁勋称需求极高而产能是约束。上述扩产幅度、投资与量产节点均为NVIDIA与伙伴公布的计划，并附前瞻性声明风险提示，尚待实际投产验证。

On October 21, NVIDIA announced at GTC in Berlin that it is partnering with Amkor, IBIDEN, King Yuan Electronics, Samsung Electro-Mechanics, Siliconware Precision Industries (SPIL), and Unimicron to expand production. The company plans to increase product substrate capacity 2.5-fold within three years: Samsung Electro-Mechanics is joining the substrate supply chain, Unimicron's expansion in Taiwan is expected to contribute capacity in the second quarter of 2027, and IBIDEN's expansion at its Ono plant is expected to ramp up in the first quarter of 2028.

NVIDIA 10月21日在柏林GTC宣布与Amkor、IBIDEN、京元电子、三星电机、矽品和欣兴合作扩产。公司计划三年内把产品基板产能提升2.5倍：三星电机加入基板供应链，欣兴台湾扩产预计2027年第二季度贡献产能，IBIDEN大野工厂扩产预计2028年第一季度爬坡。

On packaging and testing, NVIDIA and Amkor plan to increase interposer wafer capacity in South Korea by more than 300% by the end of 2028; NVIDIA expects its total semiconductor testing capacity to double from 2026 to 2027. The company is providing King Yuan Electronics with a US$1.1 billion capacity reservation prepayment to support its construction of a new testing facility in the United States, targeting mass production in April 2027; SPIL plans to increase co-packaged optics photonic engine capacity 15-fold by mid-2027 and double it again by the end of 2028.

The above expansion magnitudes, investments, and mass production milestones are all plans announced by NVIDIA and its partners, accompanied by forward-looking statement risk disclosures, and remain subject to verification through actual production.
