热门游戏事件与热梗

BLG Beats T1 3:1 to Become First LPL Team to Win a Worlds Best-of-Five Against T1

BLG 3:1战胜T1，成为首支S赛BO5击败T1的LPL战队

11月09日 10:01重大可靠性 B进展

据机核报道，在《英雄联盟》S16全球总决赛淘汰赛半决赛第二日，BLG以3:1战胜T1，晋级2026全球总决赛冠亚军决赛，成为LPL赛区首支在全球总决赛BO5中战胜T1的战队。

决赛定于11月15日3时举行，BLG将对阵LCK赛区的GEN。

赛后采访中，BLG选手Bin称这是历史性时刻，并表示希望LPL在S赛战胜T1和Faker这样的传奇对手；这是选手个人表态。

According to Gcores, on the second day of the knockout-stage semifinals at the League of Legends S16 World Championship, BLG defeated T1 3:1 to advance to the 2026 World Championship final, becoming the first team from the LPL region to beat T1 in a best-of-five at the World Championship.

据机核报道，在《英雄联盟》S16全球总决赛淘汰赛半决赛第二日，BLG以3:1战胜T1，晋级2026全球总决赛冠亚军决赛，成为LPL赛区首支在全球总决赛BO5中战胜T1的战队。

The final is scheduled for November 15 at 3:00, with BLG set to face GEN from the LCK.

决赛定于11月15日3时举行，BLG将对阵LCK赛区的GEN。

In a post-match interview, BLG player Bin called it a historic moment and said he hoped the LPL could defeat legendary opponents like T1 and Faker at the World Championship; this was the player's personal statement.

赛后采访中，BLG选手Bin称这是历史性时刻，并表示希望LPL在S赛战胜T1和Faker这样的传奇对手；这是选手个人表态。
