Firaxis Games Lays Off About 60 as Studio Head Prepares to Depart

Firaxis Games裁员约60人，工作室负责人将离职

11月18日 09:10重大可靠性 B

据机核11月18日报道，2K Games旗下Firaxis Games裁员约60人，约占员工总数三分之一；2K发言人证实，工作室负责人Heather Hazen也将离职。

2K在声明中称，Firaxis正进行重组并重新聚焦核心优势，缩减规模以专注设计、技术和玩家体验，继续开发策略游戏。

机核援引未获授权公开发言的知情人士称，Firaxis一直在开发一款新的《幽浮》游戏，项目仍会继续，但此次裁员可能给项目带来较大变动。

According to a November 18 report by Gcores, 2K Games-owned Firaxis Games has laid off about 60 people, roughly one-third of its total workforce; a 2K spokesperson confirmed that studio head Heather Hazen will also depart.

据机核11月18日报道，2K Games旗下Firaxis Games裁员约60人，约占员工总数三分之一；2K发言人证实，工作室负责人Heather Hazen也将离职。

In a statement, 2K said Firaxis is restructuring and refocusing on its core strengths, scaling down to focus on design, technology and player experience, and continuing to develop strategy games.

2K在声明中称，Firaxis正进行重组并重新聚焦核心优势，缩减规模以专注设计、技术和玩家体验，继续开发策略游戏。

Gcores, citing people familiar with the matter who were not authorized to speak publicly, reported that Firaxis has been developing a new XCOM game; the project will continue, but the layoffs could bring significant changes to it.

机核援引未获授权公开发言的知情人士称，Firaxis一直在开发一款新的《幽浮》游戏，项目仍会继续，但此次裁员可能给项目带来较大变动。

来源：gcores.com

Sony Pushes PS5 System Update, Says It Is Required to Play GTA VI

索尼推送PS5系统更新，称为《GTA VI》游玩所必需

11月18日 11:25重要可靠性 B

据4Gamer报道，索尼互动娱乐已开始推送PS5系统软件更新26.07-14.23.00。PlayStation Support官方X称，该更新是11月19日发售的《GTA VI》游玩所必需，建议玩家在11月19日0时前下载。

官方支持页面称，此次更新包含系统安全修正，但未公开具体修正内容。

According to 4Gamer, Sony Interactive Entertainment has begun rolling out PS5 system software update 26.07-14.23.00. The official PlayStation Support X account said the update is required to play GTA VI, which launches on November 19, and advised players to download it before 0:00 on November 19.

据4Gamer报道，索尼互动娱乐已开始推送PS5系统软件更新26.07-14.23.00。PlayStation Support官方X称，该更新是11月19日发售的《GTA VI》游玩所必需，建议玩家在11月19日0时前下载。

The official support page said the update includes system security fixes but did not disclose the specific contents of the fixes.

官方支持页面称，此次更新包含系统安全修正，但未公开具体修正内容。

SAKAMOTO DAYS Season 2 Set for January 18, 2027; Creepy Nuts to Perform Opening Theme

《SAKAMOTO DAYS》第2期定档2027年1月18日，Creepy Nuts担当片头曲

11月18日 11:05重要可靠性 B

据Anime!Anime!报道，《SAKAMOTO DAYS》第2期公布主视觉和首支主PV，定于2027年1月18日在东京电视台系等频道每周一深夜24时播出，Netflix同日全球配信并先行播出第1话。

片头曲确定为Creepy Nuts的《パパはいまだに Juvenile》，歌曲1月19日配信；追加声优为堀内賢雄饰廟堂、谷山紀章饰クラブ・ジャム。

官方还定于2027年1月9日在TOHO Cinemas新宿举行第2期先行上映会，杉田智和、島崎信長、M・A・O、日野聡将登台，票务详情日后公布。

According to Anime! Anime! reports the following. SAKAMOTO DAYS Season 2 has unveiled a key visual and its first main PV and is set to air on TV Tokyo network and other channels at 24:00 every Monday starting January 18, 2027, with Netflix streaming globally the same day and pre-releasing Episode 1.

据Anime!Anime!报道，《SAKAMOTO DAYS》第2期公布主视觉和首支主PV，定于2027年1月18日在东京电视台系等频道每周一深夜24时播出，Netflix同日全球配信并先行播出第1话。

The opening theme is confirmed as Creepy Nuts' “パパはいまだに Juvenile”, which will be released digitally on January 19; the additional cast includes Kenyu Horiuchi as Byōdō and Kisho Taniyama as Club Jam.

片头曲确定为Creepy Nuts的《パパはいまだに Juvenile》，歌曲1月19日配信；追加声优为堀内賢雄饰廟堂、谷山紀章饰クラブ・ジャム。

It has also been officially announced that an advance screening event for Season 2 will be held on January 9, 2027, at TOHO Cinemas Shinjuku, with Tomokazu Sugita, Nobunaga Shimazaki, M・A・O, and Satoshi Hino taking the stage, and that ticket details will be announced later.

官方还定于2027年1月9日在TOHO Cinemas新宿举行第2期先行上映会，杉田智和、島崎信長、M・A・O、日野聡将登台，票务详情日后公布。

来源：animeanime.jp
