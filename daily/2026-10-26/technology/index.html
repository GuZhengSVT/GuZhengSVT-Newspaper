Reports of Screen and Hinge Damage on iPhone Duo Foldable; Teardown Points to Weak Hinge Link

iPhone Duo折叠屏出现屏幕与铰链损坏报告，拆解指向铰链薄弱环节

10月26日 04:16重要可靠性 B进展

The Verge于10月25日报道，社交媒体和iPhone Duo子版块出现多起用户报告，称这款折叠屏手机在短时间内出现铰链卡滞、发出响声并导致屏幕破裂，裂纹位置相似。iFixit拆解后给出5/10的可维修评分，称这是该网站迄今给折叠屏手机的最高分，但屏幕本身维修仍昂贵困难。

iFixit认为，内屏的哑光纳米纹理覆盖层不仅提升强光下可读性，还通过散射反射帮助掩盖折痕；显微镜下可见椭圆形子像素以不同角度排列，可能用于减少伪影并进一步隐藏折痕。该纳米纹理外层较易更换，无需更换下方OLED。

YouTuber CuriousHagi在耐久测试中复现了屏幕开裂，拆解后发现铰链由齿轮状中央铰链和两种连接铰链组成，断裂的是中部一个比上下连接件更薄的连接件，并在最薄弱的缺口处断开；他还发现铰链与支撑板接触处有压痕，可能表明卡滞位置。The Verge称苹果未立即回应置评请求。

The Verge reported on October 25 that multiple user reports have appeared on social media and in the iPhone Duo subreddit, saying that this foldable phone developed hinge jamming and clicking sounds within a short period, leading to screen cracking at similar locations. After teardown, iFixit gave it a repairability score of 5/10, saying this is the highest score the site has ever given a foldable phone, but that repairing the screen itself remains expensive and difficult.

iFixit believes that the matte nanotexture coating on the inner display not only improves readability under strong light but also helps conceal the crease by scattering reflections; under a microscope, oval subpixels can be seen arranged at different angles, possibly to reduce artifacts and further hide the crease. This nanotexture outer layer is relatively easy to replace, without the need to replace the OLED underneath.

YouTuber CuriousHagi reproduced the screen cracking in a durability test; after teardown, he found that the hinge consists of a gear-shaped central hinge and two types of connecting hinges, with the broken part being a connecting piece in the middle that is thinner than the upper and lower connecting parts and that snapped at its weakest notch; he also found indentations where the hinge contacts the support plate, possibly indicating the location of the jamming. The Verge said Apple did not immediately respond to a request for comment.

WPS for Pad Android Native Version Launches on Xiaomi App Store, Supporting Xiaomi 18 Fold and Tablets

WPS for Pad安卓原生版上线小米应用商城，支持小米18 Fold与平板

10月26日 11:37重要可靠性 B

据IT之家10月26日报道，金山办公WPS官方微博宣布，专为平板打造的原生桌面级应用WPS for Pad已正式上线小米应用商城。使用小米平板和小米18 Fold的用户将设备升级至小米澎湃OS4后，可在小米应用商城搜索下载。此次登陆的是安卓原生版本，而非此前基于Linux容器的方案。

WPS for Pad最早于2026年3月9日面向iPad发布，金山办公称其为iPadOS生态首款原生桌面级办公应用，界面采用与Mac版一致的ribbon布局，内置WPS AI 3.0与WPS灵犀。安卓版完整支持文字、表格、演示、PDF四大模块编辑，依托澎湃OS原生多窗口能力实现多文档并行与拖拽排布，并支持AI生成PPT、WPS 365团队协作和触控笔手写批注。

According to an IT Home report on October 26, Kingsoft Office's official WPS Weibo account announced that WPS for Pad, a native desktop-class application built specifically for tablets, has officially launched on the Xiaomi App Store. Users of Xiaomi tablets and the Xiaomi 18 Fold can search for and download it from the Xiaomi App Store after upgrading their devices to Xiaomi HyperOS 4. The version launched this time is the Android native version, rather than the earlier Linux container-based solution.

WPS for Pad was first released for iPad on March 9, 2026, and Kingsoft Office calls it the first native desktop-class office application in the iPadOS ecosystem, featuring an interface with the same ribbon layout as the Mac version and built-in WPS AI 3.0 and WPS Lingxi. The Android version fully supports editing in the four major modules of Writer, Spreadsheets, Presentation, and PDF, leverages the native multi-window capabilities of HyperOS to enable parallel multi-document work and drag-and-drop arrangement, and supports AI-generated PPT, WPS 365 team collaboration, and stylus handwriting annotations.
