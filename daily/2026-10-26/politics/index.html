Israel Closes West Bank Crossings Ahead of Election as Anti-Government Rally Erupts in Tel Aviv

以色列选举前关闭西岸口岸，特拉维夫爆发反政府集会

10月26日 00:00重要可靠性 B进展

以色列定于周二举行议会选举。据BBC报道，选前民调显示执政联盟与主要反对派阵营支持率接近，约50万首次选民将投票。反对派阵营主要领导人之一、前军方总参谋长加迪·艾森科特与总理内塔尼亚胡在民调中不相上下。

以色列军方宣布，选举日（周二）关闭约旦河西岸所有陆路口岸，并禁止巴勒斯坦工人进入包括工业区在内的所有定居点。以军称，自周日（10月25日）起大多数工人已被禁止进入定居点，仅工业区和少数区域例外。在两名以军士兵于检查站遇袭身亡后，以军在希伯伦展开行动；以防长卡茨称约2000名居民流离失所。

据BBC报道，周六（10月24日）晚，数千人在特拉维夫举行反政府集会，要求就2023年10月7日哈马斯袭击事件成立国家调查委员会。集会由袭击受害者及被扣押人员家属组成的“十月委员会”组织。希伯伦市长贾巴里称，以军行动“由选举驱动”，意在争取极右翼和定居者选票。

Israel is scheduled to hold parliamentary elections on Tuesday. According to BBC reporting, pre-election polls show the ruling coalition and the main opposition camp are close in support, with about 500,000 first-time voters set to cast ballots. Former military Chief of Staff Gadi Eisenkot, one of the main leaders of the opposition camp, is running neck and neck with Prime Minister Netanyahu in the polls.

The Israeli military announced that all land crossings in the West Bank will be closed on election day (Tuesday) and that Palestinian workers will be barred from entering all settlements, including industrial zones. The Israeli military said that since Sunday (October 25) most workers had already been barred from entering settlements, with exceptions only for industrial zones and a few areas. After two Israeli soldiers were killed in an attack at a checkpoint, the Israeli military launched an operation in Hebron; Israeli Defense Minister Katz said about 2,000 residents had been displaced.

According to BBC reporting, on Saturday (October 24) night, thousands of people held an anti-government rally in Tel Aviv demanding a state commission of inquiry into the October 7, 2023, Hamas attack. The rally was organized by the "October Council," made up of victims of the attack and families of detainees. Hebron Mayor Jabari said the Israeli military operation is "election-driven" and aimed at winning votes from the far right and settlers.

Machado Returns to Venezuela After Exile

马查多结束流亡返回委内瑞拉

10月26日 05:00重要可靠性 B进展

委内瑞拉反对派领袖玛丽亚·科里纳·马查多在流亡超过10个月后返回委内瑞拉。她在社交平台X上写道：“我在这里。和你们在一起。”据BBC报道，她当天在拉拉州与支持者见面，随后在支持者车队陪同下前往首都加拉加斯，晚间抵达。

美国国务卿鲁比奥在社交平台X上表示，马查多安全返回的条件是美国支持的和解谈判的直接结果，谈判已促成数百名政治犯获释、数百名政治反对派人士结束流亡。马查多的发言人斯莫兰斯基感谢特朗普政府、鲁比奥及美国国会。特朗普对记者称，他不知道马查多回国的消息，但希望她安全。

马查多是委内瑞拉反对派主要领导人，2023年在非官方初选中赢得超过90%选票，但被马杜罗政府禁止参选。BBC报道称，她此前曾表示，若举行选举将再次参选。

Venezuelan opposition leader María Corina Machado has returned to Venezuela after more than 10 months in exile. She wrote on the social platform X: "Here I am. With you." According to BBC reporting, she met with supporters in Lara state that day and then traveled to the capital, Caracas, accompanied by a convoy of supporters, arriving in the evening.

委内瑞拉反对派领袖玛丽亚·科里纳·马查多在流亡超过10个月后返回委内瑞拉。她在社交平台X上写道：“我在这里。和你们在一起。”据BBC报道，她当天在拉拉州与支持者见面，随后在支持者车队陪同下前往首都加拉加斯，晚间抵达。

U.S. Secretary of State Rubio said on the social platform X that the conditions for Machado's safe return were a direct result of U.S.-backed reconciliation talks, which have led to the release of hundreds of political prisoners and the end of exile for hundreds of political opposition figures. Machado's spokesperson Smolansky thanked the Trump administration, Rubio and the U.S. Congress. Trump told reporters that he had not known about news of Machado's return but hoped she was safe.

Machado is the main leader of Venezuela's opposition; in 2023 she won more than 90% of the vote in an unofficial primary but was barred from running by the Maduro government. BBC reported that she had previously said she would run again if an election were held.

Gabon Lifts Eight-Month Social Media Ban

加蓬解除实施八个月的社交媒体禁令

10月25日 22:39重要可靠性 B

加蓬高级通信委员会在电视声明中宣布，解除实施八个月的社交媒体禁令。该禁令于2月17日实施，涉及Facebook、TikTok、YouTube和Instagram等平台。

禁令实施时，高级通信委员会称网络内容正在助长冲突、加深国家分裂。反对派称暂停社交媒体是滥用权力。禁令实施之际，加蓬总统布里斯·奥利吉·恩圭马正面临社会动荡，教师和公务员就低工资和恶劣工作条件举行罢工。许多加蓬人转而使用VPN继续访问社交媒体。

Gabon's High Council for Communication announced in a televised statement that it was lifting an eight-month social media ban. The ban, implemented on February 17, covered platforms including Facebook, TikTok, YouTube and Instagram.

加蓬高级通信委员会在电视声明中宣布，解除实施八个月的社交媒体禁令。该禁令于2月17日实施，涉及Facebook、TikTok、YouTube和Instagram等平台。

When the ban was imposed, the High Council for Communication said online content was fueling conflict and deepening divisions in the country. The opposition said suspending social media was an abuse of power. The ban came as Gabon's President Brice Oligui Nguema faced social unrest, with teachers and civil servants striking over low pay and poor working conditions. Many Gabonese turned to VPNs to continue accessing social media.
