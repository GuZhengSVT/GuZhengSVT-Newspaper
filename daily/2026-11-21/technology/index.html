Tencent Hunyuan Team: Hy4 Post-Training Still Has Room for Improvement, Plans to Train Larger Models

腾讯混元团队：Hy4后训练仍有改进空间，计划训练更大模型

11月21日 11:24重要可靠性 B进展

据IT之家11月21日报道，腾讯首席AI科学家、混元大模型负责人姚顺雨和团队成员11月21日凌晨在Reddit举行持续120分钟的AMA，回答开发者提问。

团队称，Hy3和Hy4表明做得扎实的小模型也能超过更大模型，成本更低；但Hy4仍有很大改进空间，尤其是后训练。团队正投入大规模强化学习，并计划训练更大规模模型。

被问及是否会推出240亿至310亿参数的稠密模型时，团队表示“有可能，我们正在考虑”。

IT Home reported on November 21 that Yao Shunyu, Tencent's Chief AI Scientist and head of the Hunyuan large model, and team members held a 120-minute AMA on Reddit early on November 21 to answer developers' questions.

据IT之家11月21日报道，腾讯首席AI科学家、混元大模型负责人姚顺雨和团队成员11月21日凌晨在Reddit举行持续120分钟的AMA，回答开发者提问。

The team said Hy3 and Hy4 show that well-built small models can also outperform larger models at lower cost, but Hy4 still has considerable room for improvement, especially in post-training. The team is investing in large-scale reinforcement learning and plans to train larger-scale models.

团队称，Hy3和Hy4表明做得扎实的小模型也能超过更大模型，成本更低；但Hy4仍有很大改进空间，尤其是后训练。团队正投入大规模强化学习，并计划训练更大规模模型。

When asked whether they would launch dense models with 24 billion to 31 billion parameters, the team said, “It’s possible; we’re considering it.”

被问及是否会推出240亿至310亿参数的稠密模型时，团队表示“有可能，我们正在考虑”。

Windows 11 Introduces USB4 RDMA, Allowing Multiple PCs to Be Daisy-Chained to Share Workloads

Windows 11引入USB4 RDMA，多台PC可串联分担负载

11月21日 01:51重要可靠性 B

据TechPowerUp 11月21日报道，微软开始为Windows 11提供USB4协议的远程直接内存访问（RDMA）支持，允许多台PC以菊花链方式互联并共同处理大负载；报道未说明该功能是正式上线还是预览。报道称，这使家庭AI推理等场景可调用多台PC的算力和内存。

报道引述微软的测试称，两台PC互联时，USB4 RDMA的往返延迟为28至50微秒，而USB4上基于UDP的以太网方案为116至194微秒，前者约快四倍；USB4链路带宽最高80 Gbps。

该功能要求使用由Windows管理的USB4端口并启用内核DMA保护；兼容框架可通过NetworkDirect接入，无需编写USB4专用代码。

According to a November 21 report by TechPowerUp, Microsoft has begun providing Remote Direct Memory Access (RDMA) support over USB4 for Windows 11, allowing multiple PCs to be connected in a daisy chain and jointly handle large workloads; the report did not say whether the feature is officially launched or a preview. The report said this allows scenarios such as home AI inference to use the computing power and memory of multiple PCs.

据TechPowerUp 11月21日报道，微软开始为Windows 11提供USB4协议的远程直接内存访问（RDMA）支持，允许多台PC以菊花链方式互联并共同处理大负载；报道未说明该功能是正式上线还是预览。报道称，这使家庭AI推理等场景可调用多台PC的算力和内存。

Citing Microsoft's tests, the report said that when two PCs are connected, USB4 RDMA has a round-trip latency of 28 to 50 microseconds, compared with 116 to 194 microseconds for a UDP-based Ethernet solution over USB4—about four times faster; USB4 link bandwidth is up to 80 Gbps.

报道引述微软的测试称，两台PC互联时，USB4 RDMA的往返延迟为28至50微秒，而USB4上基于UDP的以太网方案为116至194微秒，前者约快四倍；USB4链路带宽最高80 Gbps。

The feature requires the use of Windows-managed USB4 ports and enabled kernel DMA protection; compatible frameworks can connect via NetworkDirect without the need to write USB4-specific code.

该功能要求使用由Windows管理的USB4端口并启用内核DMA保护；兼容框架可通过NetworkDirect接入，无需编写USB4专用代码。

Taiwan's 6G-FORMOSA Experimental Network Fully Launched, Links International 6G Validation Platforms

台湾6G-FORMOSA实验网全面启用，串联国际6G验证平台

11月20日 18:37重大可靠性 B进展

据中央社和经济日报11月20日报道，台湾经济部与国科会宣布，次世代通讯实验网（6G-FORMOSA）全面启用。该网由工研院与联发科、和硕、明泰、启碁、濰元等18家产研学伙伴共同建置。

实验网以网络战情室整合通讯感知融合、高效节能、智慧网络、3GPP非地面网络及多轨道宽带卫星五大主题，已协助20家台湾业者进行技术验证；并首度引入美国VIAVI与西班牙马拉加大学等验测能量，完成6G通感融合基站低空无人机侦测、3GPP非地面网络手机直连卫星即时传送救援信息两项在台测试。

经济部称，该网已串联欧盟、英国等国际6G实验网及Eutelsat OneWeb、SES等卫星运营商；2027年将与欧盟委员会通讯网络暨科技总署共同启动台欧盟6G联合征案，初步聚焦6G芯片、AI-RAN与ISAC技术研发和验测。国际6G标准预计2029年释出。

这一实验网定位为测试验证平台，旨在让台湾业者在国际标准确立前对接跨国验证资源。其启用不等于6G商用网络开通或标准冻结。

According to CNA and Economic Daily News on November 20, Taiwan's Ministry of Economic Affairs and National Science and Technology Council announced the full launch of the next-generation communications experimental network (6G-FORMOSA). The network was jointly built by ITRI and 18 industry-academia-research partners, including MediaTek, Pegatron, Alpha Networks, Wistron NeWeb and Weiyuan.

据中央社和经济日报11月20日报道，台湾经济部与国科会宣布，次世代通讯实验网（6G-FORMOSA）全面启用。该网由工研院与联发科、和硕、明泰、启碁、濰元等18家产研学伙伴共同建置。

Through a network situation room, the experimental network integrates five themes—communication-sensing integration, high energy efficiency, smart networks, 3GPP non-terrestrial networks and multi-orbit broadband satellites—and has assisted 20 Taiwanese companies in technology validation; it has for the first time brought in testing capabilities from the U.S. VIAVI and Spain's University of Malaga, among others, and has completed two tests in Taiwan: low-altitude drone detection by a 6G integrated sensing and communication base station, and real-time transmission of rescue information via direct smartphone-to-satellite communication under 3GPP non-terrestrial networks.

实验网以网络战情室整合通讯感知融合、高效节能、智慧网络、3GPP非地面网络及多轨道宽带卫星五大主题，已协助20家台湾业者进行技术验证；并首度引入美国VIAVI与西班牙马拉加大学等验测能量，完成6G通感融合基站低空无人机侦测、3GPP非地面网络手机直连卫星即时传送救援信息两项在台测试。

The Ministry of Economic Affairs said the network has linked with international 6G experimental networks in the EU, the UK and elsewhere, as well as satellite operators such as Eutelsat OneWeb and SES; in 2027, it will jointly launch a Taiwan-EU 6G joint call for proposals with the European Commission's Directorate-General for Communications Networks, Content and Technology (DG CONNECT), initially focusing on the R&D and testing of 6G chips, AI-RAN and ISAC technologies. International 6G standards are expected to be released in 2029.

经济部称，该网已串联欧盟、英国等国际6G实验网及Eutelsat OneWeb、SES等卫星运营商；2027年将与欧盟委员会通讯网络暨科技总署共同启动台欧盟6G联合征案，初步聚焦6G芯片、AI-RAN与ISAC技术研发和验测。国际6G标准预计2029年释出。

This experimental network is positioned as a testing and validation platform aimed at enabling Taiwanese companies to connect with cross-border validation resources before international standards are established. Its launch does not mean that a commercial 6G network has been opened or that the standards have been frozen.

这一实验网定位为测试验证平台，旨在让台湾业者在国际标准确立前对接跨国验证资源。其启用不等于6G商用网络开通或标准冻结。
