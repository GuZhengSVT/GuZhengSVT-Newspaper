Rockstar Reports 32.5 Million Pre-Release Sales for GTA VI, Estimated $3.79 Billion in First-Day Revenue

Rockstar公布《GTA VI》发售前销量3250万份，首日收入估算37.9亿美元

11月21日 08:34重大可靠性 B进展单一来源报道

4Gamer报道，Rockstar Games于11月21日宣布，《Grand Theft Auto VI》在11月19日发售前全球销量达到3250万份，并称这是全平台发售前销量的新纪录。

4Gamer称，截至11月19日结束，该作全球销售额估算为37.9亿美元（约6030亿日元），为娱乐作品首日销售额最高；这一数字为估算。

4Gamer提到，Take-Two Interactive在11月9日的财报说明会上曾表示预订量大幅超出预期，此次公布了具体数字。

4Gamer reported that Rockstar Games announced on November 21 that Grand Theft Auto VI had reached 32.5 million copies sold worldwide before its November 19 release, and called this a new record for pre-release sales across all platforms.

4Gamer报道，Rockstar Games于11月21日宣布，《Grand Theft Auto VI》在11月19日发售前全球销量达到3250万份，并称这是全平台发售前销量的新纪录。

4Gamer said that as of the end of November 19, the game's global sales were estimated at $3.79 billion (about 603 billion yen), the highest first-day sales revenue for an entertainment work, and that the figure was an estimate.

4Gamer称，截至11月19日结束，该作全球销售额估算为37.9亿美元（约6030亿日元），为娱乐作品首日销售额最高；这一数字为估算。

4Gamer mentioned that Take-Two Interactive had said at its November 9 earnings briefing that preorders had significantly exceeded expectations, and that the specific figure was announced this time.

4Gamer提到，Take-Two Interactive在11月9日的财报说明会上曾表示预订量大幅超出预期，此次公布了具体数字。

NVIDIA Releases Hotfix Driver 621.35 to Fix Persistent SSD Writes

NVIDIA发布热修复驱动621.35，修复驱动持续写入SSD问题

11月21日 11:18重要可靠性 B进展单一来源报道

4Gamer报道，NVIDIA于美国时间11月20日发布非WHQL认证的GeForce Hotfix Driver 621.35，修复Release 620世代驱动中NVDisplay.Container.exe的nvtops插件持续过量写入存储的问题。

该媒体称，热修复版省略了WHQL版的质量保证流程，安装存在一定风险；希望规避风险的用户可回退到GeForce 617.42，等待下一版WHQL驱动。

Rock Paper Shotgun在11月20日的报道中称，621.24驱动似乎会无必要地写入PC固态硬盘，并建议回退。

4Gamer reported that NVIDIA released the non-WHQL-certified GeForce Hotfix Driver 621.35 on November 20, U.S. time, to fix an issue in which the nvtops plug-in of NVDisplay.Container.exe in Release 620-generation drivers continuously performed excessive writes to storage.

4Gamer报道，NVIDIA于美国时间11月20日发布非WHQL认证的GeForce Hotfix Driver 621.35，修复Release 620世代驱动中NVDisplay.Container.exe的nvtops插件持续过量写入存储的问题。

The outlet said that the hotfix version omits the quality assurance process of WHQL versions, so installation carries a certain risk; users who wish to avoid the risk can roll back to GeForce 617.42 and wait for the next WHQL driver.

该媒体称，热修复版省略了WHQL版的质量保证流程，安装存在一定风险；希望规避风险的用户可回退到GeForce 617.42，等待下一版WHQL驱动。

In a report on November 20, Rock Paper Shotgun said that the 621.24 driver appeared to perform unnecessary writes to PC solid-state drives and recommended rolling back.

Rock Paper Shotgun在11月20日的报道中称，621.24驱动似乎会无必要地写入PC固态硬盘，并建议回退。

来源：4gamer.net · rockpapershotgun.com

Arika董事Akira Kurabayashi因心力衰竭去世

Rock Paper Shotgun于11月20日报道，Arika公司董事Akira Kurabayashi因心力衰竭去世。该媒体列出的参与作品包括《Street Fighter EX》《Tetris 99》和《Fighting EX Layer》。
