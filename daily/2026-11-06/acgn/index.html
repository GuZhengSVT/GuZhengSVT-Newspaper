Atlus to Adjust Metaphor: ReFantazio Prices on Existing Platforms, Switch 2 Version to Launch Same Day

11月06日 11:53重要可靠性 B

据4Gamer报道，Atlus将于11月12日调整PC、PS5、Xbox Series X|S和PS4版《暗喻幻想：ReFantazio》的价格：通常版从9878日元降至3990日元，35周年数字纪念版从15500日元降至5990日元（均含税）。

报道同时称，Nintendo Switch 2版《暗喻幻想：ReFantazio》定于11月12日发售。

According to 4Gamer, Atlus will adjust the prices of the PC, PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and PS4 versions of Metaphor: ReFantazio on November 12: the standard edition will drop from 9,878 yen to 3,990 yen, and the 35th Anniversary Digital Edition from 15,500 yen to 5,990 yen (all prices include tax).

据4Gamer报道，Atlus将于11月12日调整PC、PS5、Xbox Series X|S和PS4版《暗喻幻想：ReFantazio》的价格：通常版从9878日元降至3990日元，35周年数字纪念版从15500日元降至5990日元（均含税）。

The report also states that the Nintendo Switch 2 version of Metaphor: ReFantazio is set to be released on November 12.

报道同时称，Nintendo Switch 2版《暗喻幻想：ReFantazio》定于11月12日发售。

New Game from Rain World Team, Airframe Ultra, Launches on Steam

《Rain World》团队新作《Airframe Ultra》登陆Steam

11月06日 11:45重要可靠性 B

据4Gamer报道，曾开发《Rain World》的VIDEOCULT新作《Airframe Ultra》已在Steam发售，由Akupara Games发行，售价1700日元；11月20日前购买可享25%折扣，折后1275日元。

游戏以近未来巨大都市“Megacity West”为舞台，玩家骑悬浮摩托参加地下竞速，可用球棒、铁管、链条和等离子炬攻击对手，并支持本地分屏与在线多人。

According to 4Gamer, VIDEOCULT, the developer of Rain World, has released its new game Airframe Ultra on Steam, published by Akupara Games and priced at 1,700 yen; purchases made by November 20 receive a 25% discount, bringing the price to 1,275 yen.

据4Gamer报道，曾开发《Rain World》的VIDEOCULT新作《Airframe Ultra》已在Steam发售，由Akupara Games发行，售价1700日元；11月20日前购买可享25%折扣，折后1275日元。

Set in the near-future megacity Megacity West, the game has players ride hoverbikes in underground races, attack opponents with baseball bats, iron pipes, chains, and plasma torches, and supports local split-screen and online multiplayer.

游戏以近未来巨大都市“Megacity West”为舞台，玩家骑悬浮摩托参加地下竞速，可用球棒、铁管、链条和等离子炬攻击对手，并支持本地分屏与在线多人。

EA to Re-release 25 Classic PC Games

EA将重新发行25款经典PC游戏

11月06日 11:38重要可靠性 B

据机核报道，EA宣布将在Steam重新发行25款经典PC游戏：7款周年纪念版现已推出，包括《暴力摩托：30周年纪念版》和6款《银河飞将》系列作品；另有《创世纪》系列、《十字军》和《主题医院》等18款将首次在Steam重新发行。EA称完整合集将在Steam、GOG和EA App发售。

据报道，每款周年纪念版保留原作视觉风格和体验，并针对现代系统与操作方式加入优化；EA称团队从实体媒体资料库数字化修复中找回七款游戏的Master FMV影像，重新剪辑超过1500段过场。

《暴力摩托》和《银河飞将》周年纪念版建议零售价每款58元，其余经典游戏每款28元；11月5日至20日Steam和GOG提供五折优惠。已在GOG或EA App拥有《银河飞将》数字版的玩家可自动免费获得周年纪念版升级。

According to Gcores, EA announced that it will re-release 25 classic PC games on Steam: seven anniversary editions are now available, including Road Rash: 30th Anniversary Edition and six Wing Commander titles; another 18 titles, including games from the Ultima series, Crusader, and Theme Hospital, will be re-released on Steam for the first time. EA said the complete collection will go on sale on Steam, GOG, and the EA App.

据机核报道，EA宣布将在Steam重新发行25款经典PC游戏：7款周年纪念版现已推出，包括《暴力摩托：30周年纪念版》和6款《银河飞将》系列作品；另有《创世纪》系列、《十字军》和《主题医院》等18款将首次在Steam重新发行。EA称完整合集将在Steam、GOG和EA App发售。

According to reports, each anniversary edition retains the original visual style and experience while adding optimizations for modern systems and controls; EA said the team recovered the Master FMV footage for seven games through digital restoration from its physical media archive and re-edited more than 1,500 cutscenes.

据报道，每款周年纪念版保留原作视觉风格和体验，并针对现代系统与操作方式加入优化；EA称团队从实体媒体资料库数字化修复中找回七款游戏的Master FMV影像，重新剪辑超过1500段过场。

The suggested retail price for the Road Rash and Wing Commander anniversary editions is 58 yuan each, while the other classic games are 28 yuan each; Steam and GOG will offer a 50% discount from November 5 to November 20. Players who already own digital versions of Wing Commander on GOG or the EA App will automatically receive the anniversary edition upgrade for free.

《暴力摩托》和《银河飞将》周年纪念版建议零售价每款58元，其余经典游戏每款28元；11月5日至20日Steam和GOG提供五折优惠。已在GOG或EA App拥有《银河飞将》数字版的玩家可自动免费获得周年纪念版升级。

Zombie Survival Game Quarantine Zone Leaves Early Access, Launches 1.0

丧尸生存游戏《隔离区》结束抢先体验，推出1.0正式版

11月06日 11:50重要可靠性 B

据机核报道，由酱油一人开发的丧尸生存游戏《隔离区》在经历约三年抢先体验后，于11月6日推出正式版，首发两周提供六折优惠。

正式版新增可探索的远岛区域、最多八人联机、种植系统，以及灾变模式和尸潮模式；还加入新Boss、叉车与自行车等载具和连接二岛各区域的铁路。

According to Gcores, the zombie survival game Quarantine Zone, developed single-handedly by Jiangyou, released its full version on November 6 after about three years in early access, offering a 40% discount during its first two weeks.

据机核报道，由酱油一人开发的丧尸生存游戏《隔离区》在经历约三年抢先体验后，于11月6日推出正式版，首发两周提供六折优惠。

The full version adds an explorable remote island area, online multiplayer for up to eight players, a farming system, as well as a disaster mode and a zombie horde mode; it also adds new bosses, vehicles such as forklifts and bicycles, and a railway connecting the various areas of the second island.

正式版新增可探索的远岛区域、最多八人联机、种植系统，以及灾变模式和尸潮模式；还加入新Boss、叉车与自行车等载具和连接二岛各区域的铁路。
